# VII cold re-dump

Workshop only. This is a fresh pass at the four-second event, not a proposed replacement. The chapter file remains untouched.

## Cold pass 1

Then the room nearest me opened.

A man sat at a table working a strap through a buckle. The leather had split around one of the holes and he was trying to make the next hole take the strain. I looked at his hands first. There was nothing remarkable in them. One thumbnail was black at the edge.

I looked at his face.

He was not better than me.

I know how that sounds. I looked for the better thing anyway. I waited for him to put the strap down and turn toward the person in the room with him, or ask after her, or perform some small act that would explain what happened next. He kept his head bent over the buckle.

She came up behind him and laid her palm against the back of his neck. Her fingers went under his hair. The hand did not press or call him away from what he was doing. It rested there.

He kept working.

Four seconds.

I counted because I have counted every second of that kind I have ever been given and there have been eleven and I know where I was for each one and what month it was and what I had said to make the touch possible and this man had said nothing he had not even turned around at one he pulled the strap tighter at two and I could feel you behind my right shoulder and I lowered my head a little not enough to ask for anything there is an amount a man can move and still say he was only changing how he stood and the air reached the place below my hair and at three he said something to her that had nothing to do with the hand and she answered and at four he laughed and her palm lifted and he did not look up he did not know what had been given to him he had not made himself ready to receive it he had not kept still he would not remember it and she would do it again and I wanted the four seconds back from him though they had never been mine I wanted to put my hand through the room and lift his body out from under hers and stand where he had been and when my mouth filled I swallowed and it came back and both my hands went over the wall before I knew I had moved and the wall held me out and then my legs went

and I called it running.

There is nowhere on that floor to run. I went along the wall with one shoulder striking it and my hands over my ears. The rooms did not become quieter. At the first divider my foot landed badly. At the second I missed the floor and went down far enough to catch myself, then got up and continued as though the getting up proved I had chosen to fall.

Something moved above me each time my foot struck. It followed the turn of my head and continued after the head stopped, lifting at the back half a beat late. I heard its edge touch my hair.

At the far end my knees struck stone.

There was spit from my mouth to the floor. I wiped my mouth with the back of my hand before I looked toward you. The hand left my mouth and went to the place I had opened to the air.

The thing on my head was exactly as warm as the skin beneath it. Exactly the temperature of the place where her hand had rested on him.

I decided that proved the heat was spreading.

## Facts to test, not conclusions

- He examines the other man's hands before his face.
- One thumbnail is dirty.
- He waits for a deserving action that never comes.
- He exposes the back of his neck by an amount he can deny.
- His mouth fills before he runs; the spit follows from the failed swallow.
- He calls the loss of his legs running.
- He gets up once and uses that fact to recast the fall as chosen.
- He wipes his mouth before looking toward her.
