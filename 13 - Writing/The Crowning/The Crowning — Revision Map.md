## The operational law

> Before the fire, he can know facts, recognise bodily behaviour, and accurately advertise a flattering capacity. He cannot correctly name the moral function of his behaviour or its cost to another person. When he claims to know another person's meaning, he is deciding without recognising that he is deciding.

After the fire, correctness is allowed and necessary, but it must carry cost, embarrassment, or immediate evidence of how long he taught the opposite. IX already does this at *they wrote it down*.

### The law is already encoded in the text

| | |
|---|---|
| `01:5` | I am very fast at **knowing** what a look means (about her) |
| `04:165` | and I **decided** that was the heat (about the crown) |
| `07:125` | I **decided** that proved the heat was spreading (about the crown) |
| `10:17` | I have always been fast at **deciding** what a body means (about her) |

The verbs are sorted by object. He knows what she means. He decides what the crown means, twice, wrongly. X is the first place the crown verb reaches her body, about a hundred lines before the paper goes to dust.

### Two rules for candidate generation

**Knowledge-verb search is a candidate generator, not a deletion rule.** It also under-fires: III's boundary/injury verdict contains no knowledge verb and is the clearest violation in the book.

**A general sentence he produces as a defence is evidence. A general sentence that arrives to summarise a scene is authorial.** `01:47` (*A number should not be abandoned merely because the person it is being demonstrated to has left*) is the first kind. It teaches no metaphysical rule, dramatises him finishing a performance after losing his audience, and exposes him without supplying the verdict. Keep.

**He may mark that a substitution occurred without naming what it was for.** *And I called it running* passes because it exposes the wrong name and withholds the mechanism. *As though the getting up proved I had chosen to fall* fails because it explains the defence. This distinction calibrates the II–IV pass: preserve the narrator's self-administered categories; remove the author's diagnosis of their function.

### II–IV rulings

Protect:
- II, the triple *I knew* while holding the warm shape. He knows all three external facts and they do not alter the desire.
- II, his knowledge of the gate height. Loop evidence.
- III, *I take them out to have them out.* Physical self-report, not moral interpretation.
- IV, *This was fairer, I thought.* The interpretation is wrong.
- IV, *I decided that was the heat.* Part of the encoded verb sequence.

Cut:
- II, *I did not consider that turning the burden into a sentence was another way of holding it.* Correctly diagnoses his own formulation. Better: let him conclude the formulation was insufficiently exact, which is another wrong diagnosis.
- III, *I took what was offered, separated the warmth from the boundary that gave it a shape, and called the boundary an injury.* The cold paper against his mouth is the real sentence.
- IV, any sentence that correctly names what the counting, pocket-checking, correcting, arranging and weighing mean. Keep all the actions.

### Known false positive

`07:127`, *I know what I thought the look meant. I will not make you responsible for the thought.* Pre-fire, and he correctly names the moral function of his own interpreting. Not a violation. He is performing one act of restraint that costs him, not diagnosing a habit, and it sits ninety seconds before his collapse. VII is protected. Do not let the search eat this.

Two Interlude lines also survive the law:

- *My feet had already turned downward. I returned because returning was the first decision I had made there that was not governed by duration.* *Already* exposes the bodily fact before he calls it a decision. The second sentence is his wrong name for the movement, not a correct verdict on it.
- *I had restored the distinction and could name what was happening while it happened.* This is correct pre-fire self-knowledge and must remain because IX cashes it: *Two rooms. I had it right. I had been right from the fourth floor and was right now, and being right had done nothing for me in eleven years.* Knowing the distinction does not cure him.

The earlier `2,310` baseline counted the entire file, including 745 words of author-pass material. The manuscript itself is 1,565 words. The first map-pass reached 1,066 but removed voice and exposition in roughly equal proportion. Restoring the six voice-bearing pieces—the address, second promise, fifth-floor physical run, two short confidences, and the two weighted objects—brings the working draft to 1,169 words, 396 below the original. The possession gloss and the three competing systems remain out.

Judge pace after the three competing systems are gone, not by word count. The chapter's problem was never length, it was seven systems arriving before anything happens to him. Removing the stone, the inward lecture and the possession gloss removes three outright. The remaining four are all either physical or promissory.

### Re-homed gesture

Cutting the stone costs her three of her sixteen actions in Chapter I, including `01:85`, the only moment in the opening where she acts on an object for a reason of her own rather than in reaction to him.

- Give her the broken lip outright: fit the fragment, turn it once, set it beside the wall, wipe her fingers. He does not interpret the action.
- Establish separately, elsewhere on the descent, that he anticipates where the stone changes beneath his left foot. Word it to match `08:127` exactly. That detail currently has no antecedent in Chapter I, so this closes an existing gap as well as uncrowding the beat.
- Restore her second look at his hands before the footsteps.
- Preserve `01:125`, *You looked at my face then, not my hands*, as the payoff.

## Resolved — VI

No catechism audit. Under the A ruling, the old man is the Lamb speaking through a shape the narrator constructed from his own ambition, not a future self who escaped the loop. The dialogical-immortality passage is therefore the thing itself addressing a man who already has an objection prepared; it remains a conflict rather than two characters jointly explaining the theme.

### Authorship — already embodied in the drafts

The novella's growth from the original one-page version through the full chapter sequence is the authorship work. A cold re-dump is one optional revision instrument, not a test that prose must pass before it counts as the author's.

- The remaining `ADD IF TRUE` boxes are optional working prompts. An unanswered prompt does not make a chapter unauthored or incomplete.
- VII was re-dumped because that specific scene benefited from it. The other chapters do not owe the process ten more cold dumps.
- Ari's reason for descending is intentionally unavailable. `08:209` states the only fact the narrator can possess: this is the first descent on which he found her already coming down. Supplying a reason would turn her freedom—and his fear that she may never come—into another mechanism he can master. Do not add one.
- Publication is a separate personal decision. No site or listing change has been made.
