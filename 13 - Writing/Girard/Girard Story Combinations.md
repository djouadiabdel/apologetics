# Girard Story Combinations

Fifty setups. Two to four concepts each. Pick one, get the dossier for those concepts, read it, close it, write whatever comes.

Numbers refer to `GIRARD'S DECEIT, DESIRE AND THE NOVEL — COMPLETE CONCEPT EXTRACTION.md`. Named concepts with no number come from books outside *Deceit, Desire* and are marked.

Grouped only by who is in the room. That is not a theory, it is so you can find one fast.

---

## Two people

1. **22 + 24** — each waits for the other to show first, and neither ever does.
2. **23 + 13** — he wants whatever her indifference seems to prove she has.
3. **25 + 30** — he gives things up so that the timing will finally match.
4. **24 + 60** — the concealing has become automatic and he no longer knows he is doing it.
5. **33 + 10** — the more she refuses, the more divine she looks.
6. **39 + 12** — a rival appears and the woman he was bored with becomes unbearable.
7. **18 + 13** — he gets her, and the value drains out inside a week.
8. **25 + 64** — he insults her constantly and it is the only way he can say it.
9. **21 + 39** — he mentions her to a friend and the friend's interest revives his own.
10. **22 + 19** — he is a different man depending on which of them is speaking.
11. **23 + 35** — she moves from refusing to enjoying the refusing.
12. **24 + 67** — he sees the whole game plainly at the end, too late for it to matter.
13. **13 + 18 + 49** — wanting her being, getting it, and what is left after.
14. **30 + 32** — he denies himself because wanting has become an obligation he is behind on.
15. **27 + 25** — a marriage held together entirely by successful concealment.

## Friends

16. **21 + 11** — he warns a friend off a thing and takes it up himself.
17. **8 + 9** — he cannot stop seeking the approval of a man who openly despises him.
18. **4 + 43** — a friend moves into his field and becomes a figure he cannot look away from.
19. **28 + 5** — his whole rebellion is shaped by the thing he is rebelling against.
20. **21 + 47** — the friend becomes the obstacle he keeps going back to.
21. **43 + 45** — the friend turns out to be empty inside and he wants it anyway.
22. **66 + 63** — he is certain he is alone and that everyone else is one single thing.
23. **9 + 38** — he covets not what the man has but what the man is.
24. **11 + 60** — he condemns in public what he does in private and never connects them.
25. **21 + 29** — the one who came to help gets it worst.

## Family

26. **4 + 8** — a brother close enough to collide with.
27. **24 + 64** — a father whose cruelty is the only shape his need can take.
28. **6 + 19** — a mother living from a borrowed template and a child taking his shape off it.
29. **10 + 13** — a parent standing in God's slot.
30. **21 + 28** — a son who becomes his father by refusing to.
31. **43 + 20** — two siblings who are one person in two bodies.
32. **5 + 57** — a house where the words for love have quietly inverted.

## Work, teachers, rank

33. **3 → 4** — a man admired from a distance takes a job in the same building.
34. **27 + 60** — hypocrisy that began as strategy and is now the entire personality.
35. **9 + 55** — a place where everyone is equal and therefore nobody is safe.
36. **32 + 30** — ambition as a duty he is falling behind on.
37. **26 + 5** — one person in the room whose wanting is actually his own, and what the others do about it.

## Groups and strangers

38. **47 + all against one** *(I See Satan ch.1, ch.7)* — a small group settles, and settling requires someone.
39. **Stereotypes of persecution** *(The Scapegoat ch.2)* — narrated by one of the persecutors, reported as plain fact.
40. **Undifferentiation** *(Violence and the Sacred ch.2)* — everyone becomes interchangeable and they blame other things for the tension.
41. **Monstrous double** *(Violence and the Sacred ch.6)* — the rival stops being a person he can describe.
42. **44 + 62** — it runs all the way to the end.
43. **36 + 37** — two worlds side by side, neither able to see into the other.

## Alone

44. **14 + 61** — emptiness kept as a discipline and called strength.
45. **46 + 63** — the underground.
46. **16** — one hour of accidental freedom, and losing it.
47. **31 + 30** — a saint's techniques aimed at a sinner's object.
48. **45** — a man with nothing at the centre, and everyone copying him.
49. **40** — he can only see what he wanted once it is gone.
50. **12 + 18** — the coating goes on, the coating comes off.

## Endings

Not setups. Attach one of these to any of the fifty above.

- **49 + 52** — real conversion, at the bottom, with the inversion.
- **67** — disenchantment, no religion in it.
- **False conversion** *(DDN V, the Rênal pattern)* — everything changes and nothing does.
- **53 + 48** — the old self dies and there is one person in the room who was never in the machine.
- **Nothing.** He ends where he began, more fluent about it.

Most stories get the last one. Two in the whole collection get the first.
