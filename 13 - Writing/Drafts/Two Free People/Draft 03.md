# Two Free People

*Draft 3. Assembled from The Log, 13-17 Aug 2026.*

---

I was not waiting for anything.

The wifi came in a box and somebody put it down next to my bed at nine in the morning. I looked at the box. I went back to sleep and I had a dream. When I woke up a man was setting it up on the floor. He was fat and he was nice about it.

It started raining, lightly, in the summer. A dog was running in circles in it.

A friend messaged me that he was stuck eighty kilometres from any city with no gas. I read it. I ate the brains of a sheep with a salad. I listened to the one song I like by that nepo baby singer. Then I listened to it again.

I called her. She said she was sad. She laughed about how wet everything was.

I called her because nothing else was happening that day.

Somebody asked me to spot them and I said no. She was talking to me.

My brother was not home. I heard a voice and I turned around and there was nobody there. The kitchen was empty. The window was open.

The song was still going. I was looking at the walls and I heard the singer say holding me down and I blinked. Outside the dogs were howling and the cats were running around and it kept raining.

She said, at first I was never interested in talking to you at all. Then she said, until you said that thing. The wind is hissing.

---

She said she loves my writing. I was looking at a bottle of water shaped like a cow. She said my writing feels real. I shrugged my head. I smiled at the screen. The AC got louder. It had been thirty six hours since last I slept.

The picture of the dormition of the Theotokos was in front of me. It was night and I was in my corner again and the AC was playing its music.

I clicked her profile. I took a screenshot of her profile picture. Her nose was there and her hair was red. I zoomed in and I clicked away.

She was answering me in five words. Yepp then enjoy anyways. I left the chat and went to tell my friend the story. He did not reply. It was five in the morning and she had said that to me twelve hours ago.

It has been seven hours and my last message to her is a vocal. She had asked me about one of my followers. I was outside walking when she asked.

She answers when she answers. I am not sitting by the phone for it.

I bought an energy drink. The price was the usual. The seller looked at me for too long. I was staring at the other bottle, the one that was triple the size, and I did not have enough money on me.

A girl complimented my muscles. I licked my lips and I ignored her.

I could have said something back to her. I had the whole walk home to say something back to her and I did not want to.

I opened the book she gave me. I read the notes I wrote in it. The ones I wrote for her. About her, the Beloved I wrote. I wrote a note underneath it about whether it was true. I close the book. Looked at her profile.

I read this: what is the use of being famous on earth when heaven does not know your name. Then my friend called me. Dude, he said. I said no. We laughed and he went to sleep and I was looking at her profile.

I am staring at an I love you message and she isn't her. I am in my room, at my desk, writing. The house is quiet.

---

I read the part where the father does not look at her.

I told her I missed cardiology. Then I told her I only missed the ECG. She said beat beat more.

I typed that I was trying to make her pass a verdict on me and I sent it.

I asked her to tell me before she went to sleep. Three hours later she was gone and she had not said it.

I told her that is what a chess master says before he eats the king. She said she hates the king and she would give it up if she could. She said she gives up the queen so she can play without the fear of losing her.

I asked her not to give me up.

She said it is not really the fear. She said it is that you cannot breathe.

I told her I do not play chess. I only know strategy from combat games.

I said good girl good cat. She said no. I said bad girl bad cat. She said mem squeeze lemons in girls eyes. She said rusted metal. I said sweet and citrusy and woody, the scent of the church.

She asked if I like eyes. Then she said of course I do, I like scary things.

I said I would put my hand on her chin to make her look at me. She said she would cry if she saw something she did not want to see. I said it would look hot on her.

I said let us turn into temple knights together. I said what if you were the jail. I said it was the yummiest.

I told her about the thing that sticks to you and lives off your blood, and I said that was me, minus the blood.

She said cardiology test then. Write notes, tell a doctor.

I told her I would stay on a call for hours and listen to her voice. I told her I have a friend rated 1800 and that I would never let her get near him.

I said let me tell you a story.

I did not tell her a story.

I am up at this hour anyway. I was up at this hour before I knew her.

The clock said 03:04. I typed u bored and I put a sad face after it.

She sent nothing.

I sent a sticker.

She sent nothing.

She had said earlier that we are two free people. I had said I was a car nobody can afford. She said it was deeper than that. I said a car nobody wanted.

I asked the machine if I was a bad person.

It was four in the morning and I was not tired.

---

I am picking a song for my story. My picture is on the screen in front of me and I switch to the next song.

I am eating watermelon, a quarter of it, the red part. My hand is wet and my mouth stays clean. The song says oh oh oh oh my love my love.

I almost hit a car. I was driving. It has been five days.

I have not been counting them.

My mother called me to take her out. She looked at me with her cheeks full and I looked at the wall behind her. I cannot play music when she is in the car.

She said I am her brother. She kept talking about her other brother, about him telling her to wear a scarf, about the men around her looking at her like a toy. She asked what I was looking at.

She wrote: U ain't a body U write i read U talk i argue I'm human here Human to a human not a toy or body.

The screen was purple. My paragraph was there, the seventeenth one, from days ago, the tabs open across the top, and where were you.

My heart is beating while I wait for her message. She said, U want me to beg? I would. I would.

There were crumbs on my desk. I touched one. The other one moved away from my hand and then it stood still and I ate the crumbs.

She said lean on me, I will see you. I wrote her a letter. I sat at the desk. I smiled.

---

I left the house at one in the afternoon with the sun out. I walked until I got to the store and it was closed. I passed the ice cream man. The next store did not have my drink. The one after that was closed. I walked another kilometre and I got four of them and some for my brother.

I wanted the drink.

I saw Spiderman kiss Catwoman on the ceiling, hanging upside down, the scratches on his back.

I was in my room there waiting.

I was not waiting for anything.
