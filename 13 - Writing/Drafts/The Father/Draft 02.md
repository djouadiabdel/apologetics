# The Confessor

*Draft 2. Claim: father is father regardless. Trapdoor at 60%.*

---

**1.** Father is father regardless.

**2.** She told me her father caught her with a boy.

**3.** He did not want to hear it. He hit her.

**4.** She packed her things and went to her grandmother's.

**5.** I typed, no, I do not want to see you being hit.

**6.** Then I typed, last time my father hit me, he hit me with a whole hammer.

**7.** She sent two crying faces and a laugh.

**8.** He is not a violent man.

**9.** She said she drinks one cup of water a day when she remembers.

**10.** I said one cup? My father forces me to not drink water, that now I buy my own water.

**11.** Then I sent her a Georgian chant and told her she was set for a week.

**12.** She said she buys her own water too. To challenge herself.

**13.** A friend told me she cried at school and her mascara ran.

**14.** She went home and told her dad and he hugged her.

**15.** I typed, if I tell my dad anything he would just say fuck off.

**16.** Good dad good dad.

**17.** I asked her what else, girl with the mascara.

**18.** My mother only cooks for my father.

**19.** I told a girl that on the first of August. I told a different girl the same sentence on the sixteenth.

**20.** Neither of them asked what I eat.

**21.** In Khenchela I can walk out and get whatever I want. They know my father so they will not give me any.

**22.** He wants me to go to Oran. He has been saying it for weeks. Go to Oran, go to Oran.

**23.** I said no.

**24.** Then I typed, he likes Oran and wants me to go there more? I do not know.

**25.** He has farms. He got more of them this year.

**26.** He gave me one for random shit. For fucking around and finding out.

**27.** I asked a friend what happens to the thing I built when my father and my mother die.

**28.** She said she was seventeen when she stopped talking to hers.

**29.** I asked her how she started again.

**30.** No joke in front of it.

**31.** At one in the morning his friends message me. They send me their blood work and ask me how fucked they are.

**32.** I say nothing for ten minutes. Then I tell them.

**33.** They always say, I like this one, he is honest.

**34.** I look at the age at the top of the file. Forty seven.

**35.** I think, you have all of this and you are forty seven.

**36.** Somebody told my father and the man came to the house.

**37.** I looked at what he had and I wrote him the script.

**38.** My father told me afterwards that the man was so scared he shat himself.

**39.** In the pharmacy he said, oh, a doctor here.

**40.** A girl with psychosis came and talked to me.

**41.** Her father stood next to her and answered the questions I asked her.

**42.** I was trying to ask her.

**43.** I converted and my father did not really care.

**44.** My mother was worried until I explained it, and then she understood.

**45.** On the second of August I came home from a girl and my father and my brother looked at me and were shocked at how depleted I looked.

**46.** I wrote it down.

**47.** A woman I do not know was telling me she freezes. She studies psychology. She said she knows all of it from outside and none of it from inside.

**48.** I said it is like two swordsmen.

**49.** I said, even when the son is better, he cannot win.

**50.** I said, father and son, the son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.

**51.** She said yes, exactly. Fear makes people lose when they had the ability to win.

**52.** I asked her if she had ever read a novel about a man's progression against his fear.

**53.** She said no. She said she had experienced it though.

**54.** He saw my uncle's wife with blond hair and called her a whore.

**55.** I typed it to a girl who was saying that men here do what they like and she gets called one for less.

**56.** Then I told her I liked how she thinks.

**57.** Twenty seventh of July, ten in the morning. A stranger and I were talking about whether men cry.

**58.** I told her about my best friend of twelve years. I have seen the man cry once.

**59.** I do not think my father cried when his dad died.

**60.** I remember that day pretty well. All of it.

**61.** She said that is sad, that crying does not make a man weak.

**62.** I said yes, I agree. It is not healthy to hide your stuff like that.

**63.** Then I asked her about her own thing, and she talked for a while, and I told her that was a story worth having.

**64.** A girl started calling me Father. As in priest.

**65.** I forwarded it to my friend at midnight.

**66.** I typed, she calls me Father as in priest. Then I typed, there is another girl.

**67.** She writes, I am a horrible woman and I do not even know what I am looking for. Is it love? Not really, because I fear it and I hate it.

**68.** She writes, father, there is this man using my old methods against me, he is flattering me, it scares me father.

**69.** She writes, I want to confess father.

**70.** I read whatever they send.

**71.** Another one asked me if she was really talking to the father.

**72.** She said, I do not think father will listen. Or maybe he is not the father for now. Till he becomes.

**73.** I typed, father is father regardless.

**74.** I typed it about myself.

**75.** She said she is unable to tell father what is on her mind.

**76.** I said, you are just unable to tell father what is on your mind.

**77.** Then I told her that nobody looking at her does not mean she is not worth looking at, that it tells you what they came for and not her worth, and she said exactly, and I said even good people are hurt, people who are worth loving are not loved, and she said exactly again, and I kept going.

**78.** One of them sent me a message calling me a selfish liar, a fraud psychiatrist, and a bad father.

**79.** I pasted the whole thing to my friend.

**80.** I typed, I am laughing.

**81.** I typed, I cannot.

**82.** I typed, I am crying. I typed, dude.

**83.** Another one calls me dad to make me answer. Hi dad. Whatever you say dad. You are my dad, do not forget that.

**84.** Alright father, I type back. Alright padre.

**85.** She said she would come to my funeral to see my mother and my father.

**86.** I typed, take good care of my father.

**87.** I typed, I am just dying.

**88.** Not a single one would give a fuck about me.

**89.** She sent a laughing face and asked what I would do if our kid did it.

**90.** He is fine. He is just like that.

**91.** On the eighteenth of August I told her I did not want to see her get hit, and forty seconds later I told her about the hammer, and she laughed, and I let her laugh, and I asked her what she was going to wear.

**92.** Father is father regardless.
