# The Confessor / The Father

*Draft 12.  the narrator is actively writing the story, depressed, thinking about a girl who won't commit.*

---

The cursor blinked. It was the third of August, late afternoon. The heat was stuck in the room like a dead animal. I looked at the newspaper on the desk. A boring fucking newspaper. Somewhere in the middle of it I had to remind myself it wasn't 1989. It felt like 1989. Everything was grey and tasted like dust.

I looked at my phone. Nothing. She wasn't going to text. She liked having me around to listen to her shit, to bleed her trauma out on, but commit? To me? No. I was just the guy on the other side of the screen. 

I hit the keys.

*The last time my father hit me, he hit me with a whole hammer.*

I stopped and looked at the sentence. It was true. Three minutes before I had told her that story, she was crying about her own father. My father had just seen my uncle's wife with blond hair and called her a whore. I typed that out too. Two crying faces and a laugh came back. That was all you got these days. Emojis for blood. 

I picked up a drawing from the desk. My grandpa's dead stuff. I stared at it, then put it back down. Family needs to stop needing me. 

I lit a cigarette. Or I wanted to. In Khenchela I could go out into the dark right now and get whatever I wanted, except a drink. They know my father there. They wouldn't sell me a drop. In Algiers it's cheaper than water. 

I hit the keys again.

*Twenty-first of July, one in the morning. Pitch black.*

I wrote about how my father had looked at me that night. *You've been reading many books in French. Try making up a sentence.* I told him I sucked at French. I tried translating some shit from the Bible. I looked at him, looked at the sky. He just said *enough, enough*, and walked off to the bathroom. 

I leaned back in the chair. The screen was too bright. I thought about the girl again. She was probably out somewhere. Probably not thinking about how I was sitting here trying to bleed out a story about my father so I wouldn't have to think about her. 

That same night in July, he had been selling me at the pharmacy. *Oh, a doctor here,* he told some girl with literal psychosis. At one in the morning, his friends send me their blood work and their scans. *How fucked am I, sergeant?* I make them wait ten minutes. I just sit there and let the dread build. Then I tell them the truth. They like me for it. Honesty. A man came to the house once. I looked at his file, wrote the script. My father told me later the guy was so scared he shat himself.

I laughed, a dry sound in the empty room. 

My mother doesn't cook for us anymore. Years ago, she stopped. She only cooks for him. 

I typed: *My father forces me to not drink water. Now I buy my own.* 

Once, in the Sahara, I went two wild days without a single drop. Someone told me to drink less water anyway. Something about my face. It didn't matter. She buys her own water too. To challenge herself, she said. 

I checked the phone again. Still black. I was a dumping ground. A priest. 

*So father, there is this man using my old methods against me, he flatters me, it scars me father. I wanna confess father.*

They actually sent me that. They called me Father. Mommy. Daddy. Padre. I kept the right energy going. What else was I going to do? Half an hour later, another message: *U selfish liar, fraud psychiatrist and a bad father.* 

I pasted it to two friends. *Idk man, I am laughing. Am crying. Dude.*

I rubbed my eyes. The screen was burning holes in my retinas. He wanted me to go to Oran. Been saying it for weeks. *Go to Oran, go to Oran.* Maybe he just wanted me out of his sight. What about the farms? I built those. Was I supposed to leave them to rot when he and my mother died? She told me to leave it to my brother. He's an atheist, he'll leave the country anyway. 

I type: *Father and son are two swordsmen. Even when the son is better, he cannot win. The son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.*

She had agreed with that once. *At first I lose control and feel weak, then I say fuck it and something inside says, who are you to be afraid.*

FUCK IT WE BALL.

I took a drink of warm water from the glass on the desk. It tasted like metal. 

Men don't cry. They condition themselves not to. My best friend of twelve years can't cry anymore. I cry easily. Always have. 

When my grandpa died, my father didn't cry. I remember the day perfectly. All of it. The dirt, the standing around. The absolute silence of the man. 

I stared at the blinking cursor at the end of the page. Seven minutes later, I typed it out.

*I still am unable to talk about how I feel.*

It sounded like a strange thing to even think about. I looked at the phone one last time. Dead. She wasn't going to commit. I was just the Confessor. 

Father is father regardless.
