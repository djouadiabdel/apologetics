 # The Confessor

*Draft 11. Rebuilt 26 Aug on his verdict: plot and reason, no fragments, no other men's daughters. Non-linear, jumpy. Controlling object: water, never connected. Claim stays.*

---

Last time my father hit me, he hit me with a whole hammer.

Three minutes before I told that story, he saw my uncle's wife with blond hair and called her a whore. I was arguing with a girl about who gets called what in this country, and I supplied him as her evidence.

Two crying faces and a laugh came back.

Third of August, late afternoon. A boring newspaper. Somewhere in the middle of it I remembered this is not 1989.

It is so fucking boring.

On the desk a drawing with my grandpa's dead stuff in it. I put it back in my grandpa's dead stuff drawing.

Family needs to stop needing me.

---

Twenty first of July, one in the morning. My father said to me, hmm, you have been reading many books in french. Try making up a sentence or two in french about something. Hmm i know u suck at french. Yeh, why u asking. And then as I started making sense, translating some shit from english to french out of the bible, I looked at him and looked at the sky, and he said enough, enough, and went off toward the bathroom, and he never mentioned it again. Till now I don't get what happened.

Is this the dad version of your own son doing you.

The same hour he was selling me at the pharmacy. A girl with literally psychosis talked to me irl bcz my father said oh a doctor here. A lot of my father's friends ask me some weird shit. At one in the morning they send their blood work and their scans and type, how fucked am I sergeant. Ten minutes of saying nothing at all, every time. Then the answer. Then the same thing back, we like this one bcz of honesty and shit. Someone told my father, and the man came to the house, and I saw his shit and gave him the script, and my father told me afterwards the man had been so scared he shat himself.

---

In Khenchela I can go out tonight and get some, and they know my father so they will not give me any. Annaba is full of it. In Algiers it is cheaper than water, anyone can get it.

My mother stopped cooking for us years ago. She cooks for my father. Said it twice in August. When my aunt visits she sometimes cooks for me.

One cup of water a day, when she remembers. Holy holy. My father forces me to not drink water, that now I buy my own water.

By morning it had solved itself on her side too. Forgets to feel thirsty. Buys her own water. To challenge herself.

I usually drink lots of water. There were two days once with none at all, in the Sahara. Wild days. Someone told me drink less water, about my face.

---

When I converted, my father didn't really care. My mother was worried until I explained, then she understood. My brother simply just is an atheist, soo yep.

Four minutes after the hammer story I asked her how she started talking to her dad again. Ten minutes after that I was telling her that with my father we bought a car, and I am sitting irl irl spending how I like.

My dad changed some settings in the car so I had to reset them too. Got AC in the car so it is fine.

My only problem is the farms I have in this country. Will I go and just leave what I have built rot after my father and mother die?

Leave it to your brother, she said. He will also leave the country, saving money for him too.

Go to Oran, go to Oran, been saying it for weeks. Am like no? He is from Biskra, lived most of his years between Oran and Annaba.

Maybe he likes Oran and wants me there more. I do not know.

I listen to my dad. Alright Father. Alright Padre.

---

A stranger spent the small hours telling me she wants to be pregnant one day, that it is so feminine. Thank you Mommy, she said.

Good good Daddy, I said, keep the right energy going on. I really like being called that LOOOL.

Another one wrote hi dadyyy. Hi momma.

Then a confession arrived. So father, there is this man using my old methods against me, he flatters me, it scars me father. I wanna confess father.

Half an hour later a different message, and I pasted it whole to two friends in the same minute. U selfish liar, fraud psychiatrist and a bad father. Idk man, iam laughing. Am crying. Dude.

Yep u can meet them and tell em about urself and so on, take good care of my father. She said she would come to my funeral to see my mother and my father.

I am just dying. Not a single one would give a fuck about me.

If our kid did it, what would you do.

Sixteen minutes later, idk depending on if i chose the priesthood. U gotta remember there is no wife of mine yet yet.

---

Whether men cry. Men condition themselves not to cry at all. I saw the man cry once.

My best friend, twelve years, daily, and lately it feels like he is unable to cry, he told me. Me, I easily cry. Since I was a kid. Never minded who was in front of me.

Crying does not make a man weak, she said. Yes I agree, and in a way that shit ain't healthy at all, to hide ur stuff like that.

Would be hard to convince men of that.

Father and son are two swordsmen. Even when the son is better, he cannot win. The son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.

At first I lose control and feel weak, she said, then I say fuck it and something inside says, who are you to be afraid.

FUCK IT WE BALL.

I don't think my father cried when his dad died. I remember that day pretty well. All of it.

Seven minutes later I typed, I still am unable to talk about how I feel. Sounds like a strange thing to even think about.

---

The newspaper is still boring. It is still not 1989.

Father is father regardless.
