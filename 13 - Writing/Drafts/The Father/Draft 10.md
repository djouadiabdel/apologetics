# The Confessor

*Draft 10. Jump form, no conversational scaffold. Controlling object: water, never named. Claim: father is father regardless.*

---

Father is father regardless.

A boy sent her a message. Her father saw it. Would not hear her. Hit her. She packed and went to her grandmother's, her mother came to bring her back, they found her sulking at home afterwards.

Last time my father hit me, he hit me with a whole hammer.

Two crying faces and a laugh arrived.

Then a girl cried on the bus going home, back seat, window open, sunset the whole way, mascara running that she had spent the morning on. She got in the house and acted like nothing happened. Then she told her dad, and he hugged her.

If I tell my dad anything he would just say fuck off LOL.

Good dad good dad.

Her dad had also told her she put too much blush on that day.

---

The third of August, late afternoon. A boring newspaper, and remembering this is not 1989. It is so fucking boring.

On the desk a drawing with my grandpa's dead stuff in it. I put it back in my grandpa's dead stuff drawing.

Family needs to stop needing me.

---

One cup of water a day, when she remembers.

Holy holy. My father forces me to not drink water, that now I buy my own water.

Some Georgian chanting and you are set. This is cool.

By morning it had solved itself on her side too. Forgets to feel thirsty. So she buys her own water. To challenge herself.

I usually drink lots of water. There were two days once with none at all, in the Sahara. Wild days.

My face holds its water. Eating less to lose it. Drink less water, one of them said.

Annaba is full of it. In Khenchela I could go out tonight and get some, and they know my father so they will not give me any. In Algiers it is cheaper than water.

---

Twenty first of July, one in the morning. My father said to me, hmm, you have been reading many books in french. Try making up a sentence or two in french about something. Hmm i know u suck at french. Yeh, why u asking. And then as I started making sense, translating some shit from english to french out of the bible, I looked at him and looked at the sky, and he said enough, enough, and went off toward the bathroom, and he never mentioned it again. Till now I don't get what happened.

Is this the dad version of your own son doing you.

---

That same hour, the pharmacy. A girl with literaly psychosis talked to me irl bcz my father said oh a doctor here. Her father came along and stood answering the questions I was asking her. Got her the medication for fun and explained each one and its role irl irl. His friends ask me some weird shit. Someone told my father and the man came to the house, saw his shit, gave him the script, and my father told me afterwards the man had been so scared he shat himself irl. At one in the morning they send their blood work and their scans and type, how fucked am I sargent. Ten minutes of saying nothing at all, every time. Then the answer. Then the same thing back, we like this one bcz of honesty and shit. And the age at the top of the file. Dammmmmn. You have all of this and you are forty seven.

---

My mother only cooks for my father. Said it twice in August. Nobody asked what I eat. When my aunt visits she sometimes cooks for me.

My qahwi dad will worship women, hell yeah. Loves all of them unless it's my mom. My mother wants to be his slave.

The same night as the hammer, three minutes before it, my father saw my uncle's wife with blond hair and called her a whore.

Took a random detour driving one afternoon. My dad changed some settings in the car so I had to reset them too. Got AC in the car so it is fine.

---

More farms this year. Gave me one for random shit, for fucking around and finding out.

The farms are my only problem in this country. Leave what I built rot after my father and mother die?

Came home from a girl's on the second of August and they were shocked at how depleted I looked. Like I had seen Buddha on top of it. Wrote down that he noticed before anyone did.

When I converted, my father didn't really care. My mother was worried until I explained, then she understood. My brother simply just is an atheist, soo yep. He never once stopped me.

Go to Oran, go to Oran, been saying it for weeks. Am like no? He is from Biskra, lived his years between Oran and Annaba. Maybe he likes Oran and wants me there more. I do not know.

---

Whether men cry. Men condition themselves not to cry at all. I saw the man cry once.

My best friend, twelve years, daily. Lately it feels like he is unable to cry, he said.

Me, I easily cry. Since I was a kid. Never minded who was in front of me.

Crying does not make a man weak, a stranger said.

Yes I agree, and in a way that shit ain't healthy at all, to hide ur stuff like that.

Would be hard to convince men of that. I don't think my father cried when his dad died. I remember that day pretty well. All of it.

Seven minutes later I typed, I still am unable to talk about how I feel. Sounds like a strange thing to even think about.

Three days later, the same stranger. She freezes. Psychology. All of it from outside, none of it from inside.

It's like how two swordsmen. Even when the son is better, he cannot win. Father and son, the son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.

At first I lose control and feel weak, she said, then I say fuck it and something inside says, who are you to be afraid.

FUCK IT WE BALL.

---

A girl started calling me Father. As in priest. Forwarded it. There is another one.

The other one writes that she is a horrible woman and does not know what she is looking for. Is it love, not really, because she fears it and hates it and knows she cannot have it. Then, father, there is this man using my old methods against me, he flatters me, it scares me father. I wanna confess father.

A message arrived calling me a selfish liar, a fraud psychiatrist, and a bad father. Pasted it whole. Idk man, iam laughing. I cant. Am crying. Dude.

Hi dad. Whatever you say dad. You are my dad dont forget that.

Alright father. Alright padre.

Necessary to be told what to do by ur dad.

Me and my dad, wrote another one, u are my dad dont forget that. Always my girl and my dad so dont worry.

Someone said she would come to my funeral to see my mother and my father.

Take good care of my father. I am just dying.

Not a single one would give a fuck about me.

If our kid did it, what would you do. A laughing face. Sixteen minutes later, the name on the site.

There is no wife of mine yet. Depends on whether I choose the priesthood.

---

A story arrived this month, written by one of them.

We are all sad songs with nothing to say about a lifelong wait for a hospital stay.

A child trying to understand a father's love when that love was expressed through looking away from abuse.

The looking away is meant as mercy, at least from the child's perspective.

Look away love in the only way i know.

Avoidance is the first language of love someone learns. Genuine attention can initially feel like danger.

Exactly, she said.

He is fine. He is just like that.

Thank you dada, I said once, to a different one. Without you I wouldn't know what to do.

Father is father regardless.

## Editing log, 26 Aug, second pass (strip before publish)

His verdict on Draft 9: *as if it is me told her i told her*, and *if someone knows the story a nigga just would know it was her*. Both correct. The scaffold was the disease.

**The rule extracted, standing for 4-7:** the piece may not be built as relay-units. No I told her, no I asked her, no staged exchanges. His side stands bare. The women arrive as weather, one cup of water, a bus window, Hi dad messages, a story that arrived, never as scene partners. The father is the only character allowed a voice, which is its own joke since the man never speaks in the record. His whole spoken side across the dad game is Alright father. Alright padre.

**Attribution budget:** one I typed in the whole piece (saved for the seven-minutes line), one I said once (dada), nine arrivals total (said, wrote, arrived, someone said). Everything else unattributed.

**Ambiguity moves:** the twice-told cooking line lost its two dates and two girls, became said it twice in August. The blond-hair whore lost the argument it was supplying, kept only the same-night tie to the hammer. The essay scene lost the publishing details and the best friend, kept only a story arrived written by one of them. The mascara girl kept her bus and her blush and no conversation. Anybody counting girls in this piece now counts weather.

**Kept from Draft 9 unchanged:** the object placements, the now layer, the idle beats, the trapdoor, all verification corrections. Nothing new was mined and nothing verified got cut except the identifying frames around it.
