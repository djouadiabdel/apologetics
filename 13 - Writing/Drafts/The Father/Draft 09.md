# The Confessor

*Draft 9. Jump form. Controlling object: water, never named as such. Claim: father is father regardless.*

---

Father is father regardless.

A boy she does not know sent her a message. Her father saw it. He would not hear her out. He hit her. She packed her things and went to her grandmother's. Her mother came to try to bring her back. They found her at home afterwards, sulking.

I typed, nooo i don't wanna see u being hit.

Then I typed that last time my father hit me, he hit me with a whole hammer.

Two crying faces and a laugh came back.

I asked her whether he had called. I asked how she started talking to her dad again.

He is not a violent man.

A girl cried on the bus going home once. Back seat. Window open. The sunset the whole way. Her mascara ran and she let it run, she had spent the morning on it. She got in the house and acted like nothing had happened. Then she told her dad, and he hugged her.

If I tell my dad anything he would just say fuck off LOL.

Good dad good dad.

What else, girl with the mascara.

Her dad had also told her that day that she put too much blush on.

---

The third of August, late afternoon. I am reading a boring newspaper and I remember this is not 1989. It is so fucking boring.

On the desk there is a drawing with my grandpa's dead stuff in it. I put it back in my grandpa's dead stuff drawing.

Family needs to stop needing me.

---

She told me she has people remind her to drink water. One cup a day, when she remembers.

One cup? Holy holy. My father forces me to not drink water, that now I buy my own water.

Some Georgian chanting and you are set. This is cool.

In the morning she had it solved. She forgets to feel thirsty. So she buys her own water too. To challenge herself.

I usually drink lots of water. There were two days once with none at all, in the Sahara. Wild days.

My face holds its water. I eat less trying to lose it. A different one answered me, drink less water.

Annaba is full of it. In Khenchela I could go out tonight and get some. They know my father so they will not give me any. In Algiers it is cheaper than water.

---

Twenty first of July, one in the morning. My father said to me, hmm, you have been reading many books in french. Then, try making up a sentence or two in french about something. Then, hmm i know u suck at french. I said yeh, why u asking. And then as I started making sense, translating some shit from english to french out of the bible, I looked at him and looked at the sky, and he said enough, enough, and went off toward the bathroom, and he never mentioned it again, till now I don't get what happened.

Is this the dad version of your own son doing you.

---

That same hour I was telling my friend about the pharmacy. Today a girl who has literaly psychosis talked to me irl in the pharmacy bcz my father said oh a doctor here. Her father had come with her, and he stood there answering the questions I was asking her. I got her the medication for fun and was explaining each one and its role irl irl. A lot of my father's friends ask me some weird shit. Someone literally told my father, and the man came to the house, and I saw his shit and gave him the script, and my father told me afterwards that the man had been so scared he shat himself irl. At one in the morning his friends message me, they send their blood work and their scans and type, how fucked am I sargent, and I always say nothing at all for ten minutes, and when I do answer they always say the same thing, we like this one bcz of honesty and shit, and I always look at the age at the top of the file and say dammmmmn, you have all of this and you are forty seven years old.

---

My mother only cooks for my father. I told that to a girl on the first of August. I told a different girl the same sentence on the sixteenth. Neither of them asked what I eat. When my aunt visits she sometimes cooks for me.

My qahwi dad will worship women, hell yeah. He loves all women unless it's my mom. And my mother wants to be my father's slave.

Three minutes before the hammer story, the same night, I told her that my father saw my uncle's wife with blond hair and called her a whore. She had been saying that men here do whatever they like and she gets called one for less.

I told her I liked how she thinks. You only ever go out for fried chicken, i know i know.

Took a random detour driving, one afternoon. My dad changed some settings in the car so I had to reset them too. Got AC in the car so it is fine.

---

He got more farms this year. He gave me one for random shit. For fucking around and finding out.

My only problem is the farms I have in this country. Will I go and leave what I built rot after my father and mother die?

On the second of August I came home from a girl's and they were shocked at how depleted I looked. Like I had seen Buddha on top of it. I wrote down that he noticed before anyone did.

When I converted, my father didn't really care. My mother was worried until I told her what I believe, and then she understood. My brother simply just is an atheist, soo yep. He never once stopped me.

My father been saying to me, go to Oran, go to Oran, and am like no? He is from Biskra. He lived most of his years in Oran and Annaba. I do not know if he likes Oran and wants me to go there more. I do not know.

---

Twenty seventh of July, ten in the morning. A stranger and I were talking about whether men cry.

Men just condition themselves to not even cry at all. I saw the man cry once.

My best friend, twelve years, daily for twelve years. He told me recently that it feels like he is unable to cry.

Me, I easily cry. Since I was a kid. I never minded who was in front of me.

She said crying does not make a man weak.

Yes I agree, and in a way that shit ain't healthy at all, to hide ur stuff like that.

Would be hard to convince men of that. I don't think my father cried when his dad died. I remember that day pretty well. All of it.

Seven minutes later I typed, I still am unable to talk about how I feel. It sounds like a strange thing to even think about.

Three days later the same stranger again. She freezes, she said. She studies psychology. All of it from outside, none of it from inside.

It's like how two swordsmen. Even when the son is better, he can't win. Father and son, the son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.

Sometimes fear makes someone lose even when they have the ability to win, she said.

Have you ever read a novel about a man's progression against his fear.

At first I feel like I lose control, she said, and that I'm weak. Then I say fuck it and something inside me says, who are you to be afraid.

FUCK IT WE BALL.

---

A girl started calling me Father. As in priest.

I forwarded it to my friend. She calls me Father as in priest. There is another one.

The other one writes that she is a horrible woman and does not even know what she is looking for. Is it love. Not really, because she fears it and hates it and understands that she cannot have it. That same day, father, there is this man using my old methods against me, he flatters me, it scares me father. I wanna confess father.

Another one sent me a message calling me a selfish liar, a fraud psychiatrist, and a bad father. I pasted the whole thing to my friend. Idk man, iam laughing. I cant. Am crying. Dude.

Another one plays dad with me to make me answer. Hi dad. Whatever you say dad. You are my dad, do not forget that.

Well it is necessary to be told what to do by ur dad, I told her. Alright father, alright padre.

She made a picture of the two of us and wrote, me and my dad. U are my dad dont forget that. Always my girl and my dad so dont worry.

She said she would come to my funeral to see my mother and my father.

Take good care of my father, I told her. I am just dying.

Not a single one would give a fuck about me.

She sent a laughing face and asked what I would do if our kid did it.

Sixteen minutes later she asked about the name on the site. There is no wife of mine yet. It depends on whether I choose the priesthood.

---

One of them wrote a story and published it under her own name. Her best friend told her never to show it to anyone. She sent it to me and asked me why people misunderstand people.

We are all sad songs with nothing to say about a lifelong wait for a hospital stay, I told her.

A child trying to understand a father's love, I asked her, when that love was expressed through looking away from abuse?

The father's looking away is meant as an act of mercy, at least from the child's perspective?

Yep yep, she said.

Do you mean looking away is the only love the narrator has experienced, or the only love they know how to accept.

Look away love in the only way i know, she said.

When avoidance is the first language of love someone learns, genuine attention can initially feel like danger.

Exactly, she said.

He is fine. He is just like that.

Thank you dada, I told a different one once. Without you I wouldn't know what to do.

Father is father regardless.
