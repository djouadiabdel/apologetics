# The Confessor / The Father

*Draft 15. The frame from 14, the file from 12. The now-layer jumps and the writing itself fails. Theme unstated.*

---

Six forty. I had four sentences. I had been at it since two.

The cursor blinked. Third of August, late afternoon before that, the heat stuck in the room like a dead animal. I looked at the newspaper on the desk. A boring fucking newspaper. Somewhere in the middle of it I had to remind myself it wasn't 1989. It felt like 1989. Everything was grey and tasted like dust.

I hit the keys.

*The last time my father hit me, he hit me with a whole hammer.*

Then nothing. I sat with that for a long time. It was true and it just sat there being true, a fact with no floor under it, and I couldn't get the next line to come.

Three minutes before I told her that story, she was crying about her own father. My father had just seen my uncle's wife with blond hair and called her a whore. I typed that out too. Two crying faces and a laugh came back. That was all you got. Emojis for blood.

I picked up the rosary. Tuesday. The Sorrowful. You are supposed to meditate on the Agony, the Scourging, the Thorns, and I rolled the beads and thought about the phone. The beads were greasy with sweat. I was supposed to be fasting, doing the psalters, doing the work. I had stopped all of it. I had called her an answered prayer once. *Grace, not earned.*

I lit a cigarette. Or I wanted to. In Khenchela I could go out into the dark right now and get whatever I wanted, except a drink. They know my father there. They wouldn't sell me a drop. In Algiers it's cheaper than water.

*Twenty-first of July, one in the morning. Pitch black.*

He had looked at me that night. *You've been reading many books in French. Try making up a sentence.* I told him I sucked at French. I tried translating some shit from the Bible. I looked at him, looked at the sky. He just said *enough, enough*, and walked off to the bathroom.

Four fifty. I had written half a page about Monday the tenth of August and I read it back and deleted it to the last character. It had the words *I just wanted her* in it. I went and stood by the window with nothing outside it.

That same night in July he was selling me at the pharmacy. *Oh, a doctor here,* he told some girl with literal psychosis. At one in the morning his friends send me their blood work and their scans. *How fucked am I, sergeant?* I make them wait ten minutes. I sit there and let the dread build. Then I tell them the truth. They like me for it. Honesty. A man came to the house once. I looked at his file, wrote the script. My father told me later the guy was so scared he shat himself.

I laughed, dry, in the empty room.

My mother doesn't cook for us anymore. Years ago she stopped. She only cooks for him.

I typed: *My father forces me to not drink water. Now I buy my own.*

That one came out whole. I didn't have to fix a word of it.

Once, in the Sahara, I went two wild days without a single drop. Someone told me to drink less water anyway. Something about my face. It didn't matter. She buys her own water too. To challenge herself, she said.

Seven ten. Still four sentences and the one about the water, so five.

*So father, there is this man using my old methods against me, he flatters me, it scars me father. I wanna confess father.*

They actually sent me that. They called me Father. Mommy. Daddy. Padre. I kept the right energy going. What else was I going to do. Half an hour later, another one: *U selfish liar, fraud psychiatrist and a bad father.*

I pasted it to two friends. *Idk man, I am laughing. Am crying. Dude.*

I checked the phone. Black. I had told her about the suicidal thoughts. I handed her the darkest part of the room and she said *respectfully I'm curious*, asked about the gap after an attempt, and thanked me for satisfying her curiosity.

I poured warm water into the glass and drank it. Metal.

He wants me to go to Oran. Been saying it for weeks. *Go to Oran, go to Oran.* Maybe he wants me out of his sight. What about the farms. I built those. Was I supposed to leave them to rot when he and my mother died. She told me to leave it to my brother. He's an atheist, he'll leave the country anyway.

I type: *Father and son are two swordsmen. Even when the son is better, he cannot win. The son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.*

She had agreed with that once. *At first I lose control and feel weak, then I say fuck it and something inside says, who are you to be afraid.*

FUCK IT WE BALL.

Two thirty, the start, before any of it. I had opened the file and typed her name into the dedication line and taken it out and put sixteen verses of regret there instead. She never noticed. Then I went and made coffee and came back and the page was still empty.

Men don't cry. They condition themselves not to. My best friend of twelve years can't cry anymore. I cry easily. Always have.

When my grandpa died, my father didn't cry. I remember the day perfectly. All of it. The dirt, the standing around. The absolute silence of the man.

I squeezed the rosary. The fifth one. The cheap plastic cut into my palm. I wasn't going to curse and I wasn't going to text her and I was going to sit here.

Seven minutes later I typed it out.

*I still am unable to talk about how I feel.*

It sounded like a strange thing to even think about. I read the whole file from the top. It was short. It was nothing. Everything that had happened in this room since two in the afternoon was not in it anywhere.

I looked at the phone one last time. Dead. I was just the Confessor.

Father is father regardless.
