# The Forerunner

*Draft 7. The writer's day put back in. He is writing the script and filming while the parcel is due, and the tellings keep taking the day off him. Controlling object: the books he wrote between nine and sixteen, in frame behind his head. The buried fact arrives late. Claim: I bring people to something.*

---

The parcel was due Saturday or the day after. A rosary, a necklace of the mother of God, and an icon of John the Baptist, from the head of the church in Carthage, who has my email and writes back.

I had the script open since nine and I had the first move and nothing after it. The address, then the text, then the difficulty. I had the address. I have had the address for three days.

A girl asked me on the sixteenth when I converted, and I gave her the whole thing.

Around eight I was studying, because of my family, in the associations and the mosques and the groups where you memorise and then talk about what you memorised. Between nine and sixteen I was making the books. Then I became an atheist around sixteen, because of a lot of problems I saw that I couldn't really deal with. From sixteen to twenty two I was an atheist who still was learning more about logic, and whatever. Read lots of books. Searched too myself.

That is the fourth telling this month and I have got it down to about ninety seconds. In front of a camera it takes eleven minutes and four takes and I still put the difficulty in the wrong place.

I do not tell it in order. Nobody wants the order, they want the turn, so I go to the turn and fill in behind it, and by the time they have the shape of it they think they asked for it. This is not a trick. It is how you keep somebody in a conversation they did not know they were having.

I started at nine and I begged my family to let me. I memorised half of it. I did it until I was eighteen. The books are in Arabic and they are on the shelf behind me when I film and they are in every video I have made. I have moved the camera twice to get them out of frame and both times the light was wrong and I moved it back.

Twenty sixth of July, three in the afternoon. She typed, nor christian nor jewish. She typed, I wanna be mslm. She typed, I don't feel like.

Those were the reasons I left Islam, I said. And why my brother too left it. Then I said, yes it's fine tbh, as long as you keep learning. Then I gave her the word for a person who believes in God and holds no religion.

She typed, yea exactly, but idk how would I bring it to mum.

It would be super hard for her to even accept that tho.

Then we went back to sending each other videos.

Half eleven. I wrote the difficulty and read it back and it was the answer with a question mark on the end of it. I cut it. The script was two hundred words and one of them was mine.

Same day, twenty past ten at night, a different one. She had just typed that her family is religious as fuck. I said, around the start of this year, I made my brother leave islam lol. Now my nigga is an athiest. For fun. It's rare to find a non religious household.

Two minutes after that I gave her the argument from John of Damascus, that their own book calls him the word.

In the last two years I have done the following. Memorised half of a book I no longer hold. Written four books in a language I do not write in any more and kept them at eye level behind my head. Learned the names of four churches I have been inside and one I have not. Got the emails. Kept the emails. Told the story of my life to four people this month and to nine people before them. Not been baptised.

Second of August, two in the afternoon. Somebody asked how my family reacted.

One of my friends really tried to. Hmm. Try to kill me? I am not silent about my faith. All peopl who know me in uni knows. So some got angry. And yeh.

She typed, you can chose whatever you want. She typed, your free btw.

Then she typed, did you know that I am Muslim? And would you have a problem if I wasn't Muslim?

I said I just assumed she was. Everyone is.

I had given her the whole thing first.

I filmed the address four times before lunch. The fourth was the best and my eyes went to the shelf on the word associations, and I have watched that second back more times than I have watched the take.

I said I do not tell it in order because nobody wants the order. That is not right. I do not tell it in order because in order it is a boy in an association at nine who did what he was told, and out of order it is a man who read his way out.

Sixteenth of August, half past midnight. She said the book in my video looked nice. The arabic books are usually just islamic books, I said. I used to write them between 9-16 yo. Well i got it yeaaaars ago. Then a sad face.

She typed, arabic book yayyy, you normal human.

Three minutes later I typed that I think I stole an arabic book before. Then, wait, let me find a book.

Thirteenth of August, twenty five to eleven at night. We were talking about the night we first spoke. My friend had told me, nigga, stop acting like a puppy, don't mess it up as you usually do.

Then I said, at first I did with my brother till he left islam, but well ngl was so dissapointed in myself, since all i can do to ppl is just make them leave islam and that's it. I never really had someone to talk to about Christianity in the last months. Was just writing and writing.

Three minutes later she typed, so am i really gonna be a Christian. She typed, I sometime think about all what ur saying is true.

I did not stop.

Two. I opened the script and the difficulty was still not there and I answered three messages instead and told myself that was the work.

Fifteenth of August, half six. I told her about the parcel before it was sent. She typed, hmmm. She typed, so you have a church. Good to hear that. She typed, I thought you are a closed Christian, there is not much of churches around. She typed, show me the gift when it arrives.

I said I ain't leaving no where even if it was November. Then that I am kinda a closted christian LOL, and that I have connections in the Carthage church, and that I know the head of it, and that I have got his emails, and that sometimes we talk.

She typed, how many church have you being into.

She typed that she has always looked at the church in Algiers, the one of the Virgin, maybe the one on top. She typed, loved it really really. She typed, but never had the courage.

Turkey. France. Alger. Annaba. And well that's it.

I have not been to Carthage. My family said nope, we won't let you go alone, and the only relative who would take me is a strict muslim.

She typed, I bet it wasn't easy to get to here.

Four minutes later I typed, now am attracted to you. Then, shit.

Six. Still the address, the text, and a hole where the difficulty goes. I have written that move eleven times this year and every time it comes out as the turn arriving early.

I said the fourth telling this month. I have counted it again and it is the fifth, because I gave it to a man on the second of August as well and I had filed that one under something else.

It occurs to me that I have not put down the part they all assume. Last November I did convert. I have not been baptised. Nine months, the emails, four churches, and I have not gone in.

Saturday the parcel did not come. On the day after it did. I opened it at the desk and put the icon on the shelf, in front of the books, where it covers three of them and not the fourth.

Then I sat down and filmed the address a fifth time with it behind my head, and it was the best one, and I have not written the difficulty yet.

I bring people to something.
