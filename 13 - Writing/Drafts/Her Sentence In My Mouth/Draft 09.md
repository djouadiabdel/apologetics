# The Evangelist

A girl had been messaging me about the book since the afternoon and I had been answering her between paragraphs. She had got as far as the third floor. She asked what the woman in it gives him.

What she didn't give was one sentence, I said. She left it out kindly, in a way that let him pretend he hadn't been refused, and the whole descent is about it. So technically nothing.

I do not write a chapter through. I never have. You go where the heat is and you take what is warm that night, and the book gets built in the order it wants instead of the order it is read in, and anyone who works straight down a chapter is producing a document. I have said this to four people and all four agreed with me.

Since June I have read the ledger floor eleven times. I have moved one paragraph out of six and into eight and back. I have cut chapter one to eleven sixty nine and lost the only sentence in it I liked. I have not opened chapter six in nine days. I have three files on this machine that are not the book. One is the book with her name still in it. One is a list of what she said about me in 2023, and every line in it is hers. One is called asks and there is no ask in it anywhere.

Half one. The ledger floor. Everybody on it is settling up and I had the gate line a month ago and it has not moved since.

*You do not settle up. You are changed, or you are not.*

Second of August, twenty past one in the morning, a girl I had known three weeks. I said then that bitch LEFT. She typed, she died?????? No. She left me. Like broke up. It came up. I did not bring it up.

I went to ten instead, because ten is easier to work on when nothing else moves.

*I have the colour of the skin and the shape of the arms and the way you stand with the weight on one side. I have all of it. The face is light.*

I said the fourth chapter has been fine since June. That is not right. I opened it in June and I have not been able to look at it since, and I have been calling that fine because nothing in it is wrong.

Two rooms behind one name. A man in the desert counted eight and a man in a chair centuries later tidied it to seven, and the door got a single name, and the name was wrong.

*I called my not-caring a sorrow. I nursed it. I sat with it in the evenings like a man sitting with a sick relative and admired my own constancy, and I thought myself a man of deep feeling because I could not get up, and I wrote about it, and people told me it was beautiful.*

The girl asked whether people who write in a journal way, like Kafka, like Plath, can be considered well written.

Everything written is a well considered thing, I said, as long as a person writes it for a purpose.

She typed, or those pages are for her.

These pages he thought were for her. He just wrote them for himself out of his selfish desires.

I was explaining a book.

I filled the kettle and did not switch it on. This happens most nights and I have stopped noticing which of us does it.

For a year and a half I have done the following. Kept the old phone in the drawer with the cable. Read the ledger floor. Told three people the story of the day she left and told it the same way each time. Kept a woman's messages in a file with her name on it. Not deleted the file. Not opened the file. Written nineteen thousand words to a woman who is not her and given her the good sentences.

Twelfth of July, thirteen past midnight. I typed that even during it I just feel hollow, like this shit is not even fun. Tenth of July, that she was slaved so hard in the kitchen and I would get a bit annoyed, because I was being robbed of time with her.

Fourteenth of August. Sometimes I hold myself so I do not vomit and I move to the neck so I do not vomit. Dude, you can see I am literally dissociating. She got to a level where she wanted to find a girl for us. I didn't tell her all those things. I can't let another person be in that situation.

Yeah. But it was too late, I know I am disgusting. At some point I should have just ended it, and I was still with her, and I could not stand talking to her for one minute.

He typed, you both were wrong and rightful in a way.

Two twenty. The interlude is the stair between six and seven, where nothing is asked and nothing can be paid, and more is lost on it than on any floor. It is the shortest thing in the book and it took the longest.

*I said a relation cannot be reduced to its quotations. Behaviour has a grammar prior to speech, and insisting on a sentence can become a way of refusing what somebody has communicated because it arrived in a form you did not choose.*

Looking at what I have written I see that I said it came up and that I did not bring it up. That is not what happened. She had been going at her mother and her sister for an hour and she had just got to her father, and I waited for a gap and typed the message out into it, and she had not asked me for anything. I have told it the other way to three people since and I believed it every time.

It occurs to me that I have not put down what the message actually said, which is the thing the whole floor is about.

I know i am a blessing even when i leave .. But i didn't benefit anything from you that's unfaire huh?

Three ten. I read the gate line again. *You do not settle up. You are changed, or you are not.* It is still right and I still cannot get the man through it, and I have been telling myself for a month that the problem is the sentence.

The girl said goodnight and that she would read the fourth floor tomorrow.

I went and got the old phone out of the drawer and put it on the cable and sat there while it decided whether it would come on.

I do not talk about her any more.
