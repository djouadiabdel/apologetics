# The Stylite

*Draft 5. No dates. Written from after. The structure is the Joyful and the Luminous, forced through by a man who is sorrowful to the death. Controlling object: the sky. Claim lands mid-piece, not at the end.*

---

Monday is the Joyful ones and I do them anyway.

The first is the announcement. A girl asked me once whether I have a personality, and then whether I had ever tried being without one. I sat with that for nineteen minutes and then told her I don't think I have a personality, do you, do we, idk. She said she thought I did. Maybe. Then she said she doesn't have one either, that she steals from others.

If being a sad nigga who feels that sadness is his natural state is one, then idk.

The second is one woman going to another and the child moving before anybody has said anything.

A woman told me she feels that she is a concept, a vibe, and doesn't exist in real life as a human. Another told me she doesn't even look like her own mother, and the woman who gave birth to me, and what makes you think you could ever become me. In the morning she wrote to correct it, she had meant her family, not her mother. Another told me she is dead, that she is a grave with nobody visiting. Another said she was confused about something she couldn't name. Another asked whether a mind can deceive you with things that do not exist and feel real.

Five of them. None of them know each other.

I'm the only one like this.

The third is a birth in a place where they keep animals, because there was nowhere else.

She said vacuum. She said no matter how much we suck it was never ours to last. She said hollow no matter what.

She had never seen the name of my site. She never will now. She used to read what I wrote and say things about it and I built a lot on that, and it turns out that a person can do all of that and then be gone, and the reading goes with them, and it was never a fact about me, it was a thing she was doing for a while.

The fourth is the child brought to the temple and the old man saying a sword will go through you too.

Somebody typed to me, I'm talking to god not u.

The fifth is the boy lost for three days and found in the temple asking questions of the teachers, and his mother telling him they have been looking everywhere.

Nobody has been looking anywhere.

That is the Joyful ones done and I did all five and I felt nothing in any of them.

Thursday is the Luminous and I do those too.

The first is a man in a river and a voice from the sky saying this is my son, whom I love.

I like no skies. I hate the sky kinda idk. That is a thing I typed to somebody in a conversation about my day, which I had described as waking up under a lot of hormones and then writing writing editing and so on over and over again, and then reading a bit. She said, nice day tbh. She said, why not the sky. And I typed although hmm giggling, and it sounds, and agrees agrees, and I did not answer her.

The second is a wedding running out of wine and a woman telling them to do whatever he tells them.

She asked what meal ever made me think, this is going to taste good to make. Sometimes tofu. Usually just potato, and whatever with potato. I don't really eat meals that taste good. Sometimes soup of veggies, and sometimes lentils and loubia soup with whatever veggies. She said, you gonna be a broccoli one day.

Then she said Spinoza said we shouldn't believe in god for miracles, not in the fire that didn't burn Ibrahim, but in the fire that burns us every time.

I said it sounds like a fun experience to figure out what death is by killing others and not by killing yourself.

She never asked again.

The third is a man walking around telling people the kingdom is near and to change their minds about everything.

I told a woman that everyone stays in their religion but few are faithful to it, and few really leave it fully, so they stayed and in their staying they are already gone. She asked what she was missing. She said, when leaving sound more honest then staying. She said, is staying though with no meaning better, or leaving.

Four minutes of nothing.

Then I told her that if you say honesty is in leaving then you are saying honesty is in never looking at the truth, and that if you keep looking and looking at a thing you may have more chances at studying its truth. I asked whether she would prefer a judge who looks at a case once, or one who looked at it for eternity, and then makes the verdict. Not like the judge who never even came to the trial.

She said, so staying to look, even if your look isn't the right look, is better than leaving where no right and wrong is there.

She said, not the right look and the right judge, but being there.

I was still typing about the judge hours later. The lawyer and the judge are to be held as lookers, but they are doing something with what they are looking at. What I was saying to you. You know.

The fourth is the face changed on a mountain and three men wanting to build shelters and stay up there.

I told somebody I am the worst of the worst sinners. I told somebody I don't deserve the lamb's perfect blood. I told somebody he just came down here to try to save me whom I am the worst. I told her that to her I should be called a useless animal, the worst of the worst, worse than an animal.

She had not called me anything.

I won that one too.

The fifth is a meal on the last night and a man handing himself over and telling them to take it and eat.

I noticed something in your voice but I ignored it, because it sounded good. Keep feeding me your voice, keep giving me. Send your voice, I need to hear it to know truly if it matches the way you talk. You don't wanna send your voice? Time for my voice to shine. I probably was looking like a clown in my vocals, it's fine, after all it's us, safe space. Somebody else's ruptured my ears trying to listen to it.

I keep the ones that sound good and I say so.

She had asked me once why I don't ask her to be rude with me, and then answered it herself. Because you want it, and I'm ain't giving it to you. I told her that refusing me is a way of being rude, so she was giving me what I wanted anyway.

There is nobody left to lose that argument.

That is the Luminous done and I did all five.

Then I went on into the ones that are not for today, the garden and the pillar and the thorns, and I did not decide to, I was just already in them, and my soul was overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death, and above me there was nothing, which is what I have always said I wanted.
