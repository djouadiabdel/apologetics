# The Corner

*Draft 2. Twelve rooms, built from the record 20 May to 21 Aug 2026.*

---

I like being alone.

I have been walking this room for an hour. Bed, door, window, bed. I do not sit down when I talk to her. I have been doing this since I was small and I have never got tired of it and I do not know one other person who does it. The only thing that changed is that I keep it quiet now. My father is down the hall.

I have always liked the quiet.

The first year of primary school. Not the preschool, the first year. I do not remember one thing about being inside that building. Not a face, not a desk, not the yard, not which way the door opened. I remember being left there. That is all of it. Everything else went and that stayed.

In middle school I was the loudest person in the room. I did the stupidest things I have done in my life in those three years, on purpose, so people would look. If somebody annoyed me I went at him. I was not quiet once. I cannot tell you the name of a single person I ate with.

At the gym I make them turn the music off. If there are three people in the room it is a crowded day. Most days it is me and the rings and the dumbbells at home in the evening, and then I eat, and then I read for three hours or five.

In la cité I used to bring a whole roasted chicken up and eat it by myself. Louai can tell you about it. I weighed 105.99 that year, never 106.

At the hospital in July nobody came. Not one patient, the whole shift. I found a corner and I slept in it and it was the best sleep I have had.

Nobody asked me to sit there.

I do not remember the last time I was on a bus. If I am on one I stand in the corner of it and I do not look at anybody.

I am trying to act half dead.

At the end of July everybody left the house without telling me. I was alone in it for three days and then the next week too. I ate what was in the fridge and then what was in the freezer. I read in the afternoon and I read at night, and at some point I said out loud that the only person I was talking to that day was the book, and I said it to a girl on the phone who I have never met.

They were the ones who left.

I do not like short hair. It makes my face seen.

At night I am at the desk and the desk is in the corner and I am behind it. The dormition of the Theotokos is on the wall in front of me. Mary on the bed, the apostles standing over her, Christ holding her soul like a small child. The AC is playing its music. It has been thirty six hours and I am not tired.

There are crumbs on the desk. I touch one. The other one moves away from my hand and then it stands still and I eat the crumbs.

The music is in my ears and the hands are above my head but they are not, it is the lyrics.

I was in bed crying in the dark at two in the morning. There is nothing to talk about, I am just crying while walking.

In August we went to the beach. They kept asking me to come and I told them to leave me alone. I spent it near the water. The car in the morning and the car at night and nothing in between.

In Seraidi I finished the day at the hotel and they had the music going. I got up and walked away from it, far enough that it was only a sound, and I sat down on my own and looked at the view.

The view was better from where I was.

Sometimes I go out at night because there is nobody out at that hour. I like going out in winter for the same reason.

I told her that women know what to do with their feelings and men just hide it and die in their corner. I said it like it was about men.

My brother was not home. I heard a voice and I turned around and there was nobody. The kitchen was empty. The window was open.

I like being alone.
