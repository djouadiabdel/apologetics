# The Corner

*Draft 1. Built from Story Plans/04 and the record, 20 May to 21 Aug 2026.*

---

I like being alone.

I have been walking the room since I was small. Wall to the other wall, then back. I have never got tired of it and I do not know anyone else who does it. I still do it. The only thing that changed is that I keep it quiet now, because my father is down the hall, and if he hears me he comes.

I have always liked the quiet.

The first year of primary school. Not the preschool, the first year. I do not remember a single thing about being in that building. Not a face, not a desk, not what the yard looked like or which way the door opened. I remember being left there. That is the whole of it. Everything else went and that stayed.

In middle school I was the loudest person in the room. I did the most stupid shit I have ever done in my life in those years, on purpose, for people to look at. If somebody annoyed me I went at him. I was not quiet once in three years and I could not tell you the name of one person I ate lunch with.

In June the house was empty for a week. I ate what was in the fridge and then I ate what was in the freezer. I read in the afternoon and I read at night and at some point I said that the only person I was going to talk to that day was the book. I said it to a girl on the phone who I have never met.

Nobody asked me to sit there.

I do not remember the last time I was on a bus. If I am on one I am standing in the corner of it and I am not looking at anybody.

I am trying to act half dead.

At night I am at the desk and the desk is in the corner and I am behind it. The dormition of the Theotokos is on the wall in front of me. Mary on the bed, the apostles standing over her, Christ holding her soul like a small child. The AC is playing its music. It has been thirty six hours since I slept and I am not tired.

There are crumbs on the desk. I touch one. The other one moves away from my hand and then it stands still and I eat the crumbs.

The music is in my ears and the hands are above my head but they are not, it is the lyrics.

In Seraidi I finished the day at the hotel. They had the music going. I got up and I walked away from it, far enough that it was only a sound, and I sat down on my own and looked at the view.

The view was better from where I was.

My brother was not home. I heard a voice and I turned around and there was nobody. The kitchen was empty. The window was open.

I like being alone.
