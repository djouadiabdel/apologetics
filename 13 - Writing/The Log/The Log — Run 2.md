# The Log — Run 2

Six sentences a day. Things that happened, not what was on my mind.

- No explaining, no confessing, no meaning. A camera in the room would have caught it.
- Things said count. Run 1's strongest day was almost all `I said / she said`.
- They do not connect to each other. They are not going anywhere.
- Stop the moment two of them start reaching for each other. Some days that is four, some days twelve.
- When I want to close the file right after writing something that exposes me, that is not the end. Three more lines, and they are more facts, not commentary on what I just wrote.
- Do not reread. Close the file.
- If a confession pushes while I am writing, it goes in `The Log — Sidecar.md` and does not count toward the six.

Six days: 21 to 26 August 2026. Reread cold on the 27th, not before.

**Sunday 23rd, before writing that day's six: pick the charge.** Read what is here so far and find the line I would argue with. Not the best line, the one that makes me want to say *no I wasn't*. Everything after that sorts itself.
