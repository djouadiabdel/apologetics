# The Log — Run 1

Six sentences a day. Things that happened, not what was on my mind.

- No explaining, no confessing, no meaning. A camera in the room would have caught it.
- They do not connect to each other. They are not going anywhere.
- Stop the moment two of them start reaching for each other. Some days that is four, some days twelve.
- Do not reread. Close the file.
- If a confession pushes while I am writing, it goes in `The Log — Sidecar.md` and does not count toward the six.

Six days: 13 to 18 August 2026. Reread cold on the 19th, not before.
