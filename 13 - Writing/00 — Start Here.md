# Start Here

Where everything in `13 - Writing` lives, and what each folder is for.

## The daily practice

- **`The Log/`** — six sentences a day, camera only. `Run 1` (13-18 Aug), `Run 2` (21-26 Aug), and the `Sidecar` where confessions go so they stay out of the log. This is the writing when there is no project. Everything else is built out of it.

## Raw material

- **`Material/`** — the stockpiles. `The Lines — The Stack` is the big one, everything worth keeping from 93 days of the record. Also the older `Lines`, `Harvest`, `Seedbank`, `Graveyard`.
- **`Record/`** — the Instagram export, 20 May to 20 Aug 2026, parsed. `The Record` is the day-by-day index. `Record/Log/` is 18 threads converted to log prose. Times are Algeria, corrected.
- **`Dumps/`** — numbered raw dumps.

## Work in progress

- **`Story Plans/`** — thirteen stories, one file each: the charge, dated evidence, where the floor goes. `00 — Index` says which are ready. `04 — The Corner` has a full spine and is the one to write.
- **`Drafts/`** — pieces being written. Two Free People, drafts 1 to 3.

## Finished and long-form

- **`Short Stories/`** — standalone finished pieces.
- **`The Crowning/`** — the novella and everything around it. Chapters live in `The Crowning — Novella/`, workshops and sourcebooks alongside.
- **`Web novel/`** — the Ippo adaptation, anime analysis, the older `Short Stories Of her`.
- **`Meg/`** — the 2023 material.

## How it works

- **`Method/The Machine`** — claim, defend, trapdoor, don't move.
- **`Girard/`** — the story engine and combinations.

The order runs: log daily → harvest into `Material` → a charge becomes a file in `Story Plans` → spine it → write it in `Drafts` → publish.
