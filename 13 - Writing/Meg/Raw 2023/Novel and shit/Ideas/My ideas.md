### 1. The Fucked-up Mind = La Maladie Ontologique (Ontological Sickness)

You want to write about the fuckedupness of the mind? Girard gives it a name: **La Maladie Ontologique (Concept 14)**.  
When human beings stop worshipping God, the religious impulse doesn't just disappear. It gets redirected horizontally. We start worshipping each other. This is **Deviated Transcendency (Concept 10)**.  
Because we are empty inside, we look at another human being and think, "They have the secret to existence. If I can be like them, if I can have what they have, or if I can destroy them, I will finally be complete."  
This is why people become stalkers, why people stay in abusive relationships, why we hate the people we secretly admire. It is idolatry. The mind becomes a hellscape because it is trying to extract divine salvation from a flawed human being. That is the pain you want to write about.

### 2. The Sadism and the Masochism (The Dostoevskian Underground)

You want to write horrible, sad stories? Read Chapter 8 and Chapter 11 of Girard.  
He explains that when a person is deeply sick with this mimetic disease, they actually seek out rejection. **(Concept 33: Masochism as Intensified Mediation).** They think that if someone treats them like garbage, that person must be a God. And conversely, if they become the Master, they become sadistic **(Concept 35)** to prove their own divinity.  
This is the psychological horror you want to write. It’s not about monsters or ghosts; it’s about a man who destroys the woman who loves him because her love makes her seem "weak," while he crawls on his knees for a woman who spits on him because her cruelty makes her seem "divine."

### 3. Theology and the Road to the Messiah

Girard’s entire book is secretly a theological masterpiece. He proves that the "Romantic Lie" (the idea that I am my own God) always leads to the **Apocalypse (Concept 44)**. When everyone is trying to be God, society tears itself apart in violence and envy.  
Girard's ultimate conclusion **(Concept 48 & 54)** is that the only way out of this psychological hell is the Christian realization of Agape (Grace) and the imitation of Christ. Christ is the only Mediator who does not become a Rival. The "Road to the Messiah" is the story of humanity exhausting every other fake idol, bleeding itself dry, until it realizes it needs a Savior from outside the triangle.

### How to use this to understand your own pain and cry out loud:

You said: "I want to get my pain and be able to understand it to then cry out loud about it."

Girard says in **Concept 51 (The Universal Self)**: The most particular autobiography produces the most universal recognition.

If you want to make people feel pain, you cannot write vaguely about "sadness." You have to write the most specific, humiliating, pathetic, secret pain of your own life. You have to write the things you are too ashamed to tell your friends.


- **Write about your "Skandalon" (Concept 47):** The Biblical stumbling block. Write about a person or a goal in your life that was simultaneously an obstacle and a magnet. Someone who destroyed you, but you couldn't look away from them. Use Girard's French to describe it: Pourquoi cette fascination haineuse? (Why this hateful fascination?)
    
- **Write about your "Souterrain" (The Underground - Concept 46):** Write about a time you sat in your room in Algeria, feeling completely isolated, grandiose, thinking you were better than everyone else, but simultaneously hating yourself and desperately wanting their approval. Write the ugliness of that paradox.
    
- **Write about your "Mensonge Romantique" (Romantic Lie - Concept 7):** Write about a time you convinced yourself you were acting out of pure love, or pure faith, but deep down, you were just performing for an audience.
