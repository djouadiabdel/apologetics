# What Meg actually is

The draft is from December 2023. You wrote it as a productivity book with two women in it. It is not that.

## The split

Meg teaches. Meg gives, explains, hands over the system, and is the one who knows. Faith receives, uses what she was given, gets her life working, and then thinks this:

> Faith kept realizing that all the things that she thinks that Meg taught her are common sense, are things that she could of picked on her own, so why did she bother at all to be with her. I don't need Meg.

And this:

> I bet it was that she just could manipulate me a little bit and be with me, she does not mean anything that she said to me, just whatever it takes to make the prey fall for you.

Two and a half years later, in "1. Useless", you wrote the same thing without the costume:

> I taught her things. That was how I loved. I gave. I told her which books to read and she read them. I told her things about herself she did not know and she believed me. I was her sky. I did not know yet that a sky is also a ceiling.

Meg is you. Faith is also you, standing where she stood, saying what you were afraid she was saying. You split the wound across two women so you would not have to sign it. That is why the book has two protagonists and no antagonist.

The 2023 draft is the version written before you knew. That is its whole value. Do not fix it into hindsight.

## The productivity notes are not contamination

Day One is a man who wakes cold, cannot get through the day, and by paragraph six is designing a ten hour focus system, a cursed tool, a way to split one room into two rooms so his brain will obey.

You read that as the book going off topic. It is the book. A man builds that much machinery for one reason. Keep every gear of it. Just stop pretending it is advice.

The one place to cut is the raw study material that bleeds in mid scene, the Stage One and Stage Two teaching notes at the end of 13.md. Those are lecture notes, not the man. They go.

## Alberto arrives early

He is already at the end of 13.md with a woman he calls his toy:

> she found a man who cared about her, she wanted to live this life with him and he wanted to toy with her.

Same logic as the Ippo material. Conquest as a way of being real. He was in the draft two years before you named him. See [[Hajime no ippo]].

## What makes it peak

Three changes.

**One.** Let the split be deliberate. Meg and Faith are one person insisting they are two. The book ends on the sentence where the insisting stops. You do not explain this anywhere. You just stop alternating.

**Two.** Take the reunion seriously. In "1. Useless" the body refuses the plan the mind made in the dark. Give one of them a speech prepared on the walk over and do not let her deliver it. Nothing else in the draft is that strong.

**Three.** Cut the philosophy where the scene already did the work. The three days apart are good. The four paragraphs explaining what meaning is are the same four paragraphs every time, and the mud already said it.

## Files

`Raw 2023/` is untouched, moved out of the archive. `Day One.md` and `The Three Days.md` are rewritten at target voice. The other nine days are still raw.

Voice rules in [[reference-bukowski-voice-benchmark]] terms: mean sentence near twelve words, one in five under five words, paragraphs around forty, no em dashes, and never name the feeling the prose already produced.
