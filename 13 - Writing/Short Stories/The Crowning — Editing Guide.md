# How to edit the novella

You edit these chapter files like ordinary Obsidian notes. There is no colour tool to learn.

The coloured boxes already exist. They are comments placed **after** the story, not part of the novella. Obsidian colours them automatically.

If you see plain text such as `[!danger]` instead of a coloured box, you are probably in Source mode. Switch the note to **Reading view** or **Live Preview** to see the colours. You can also ignore the colours completely—the words CHANGE, CHOOSE, ADD IF TRUE, and KEEP contain the full instruction.

## The simplest possible process

1. Open [[The Crowning — Novella]].
2. Choose one chapter.
3. Read only the story above `## Author pass`.
4. Change anything you want directly in the story:
   - delete a word
   - replace a phrase
   - add an ugly or honest sentence
   - move a paragraph
   - cut an entire scene
5. Read the four boxes at the bottom.
6. Follow the useful ones and ignore the false ones.
7. Keeping a passage unchanged is a valid decision. The boxes are prompts, not proof of unfinished authorship.

## What the four boxes mean

> [!danger] CHANGE
> I found a passage that may be too composed, too explanatory, or insufficiently yours. You may rewrite it directly. This is advice, not an order.

> [!warning] CHOOSE
> There are two legitimate versions of the book here. Only you can choose. Alter the prose only if your choice requires it.

> [!question] ADD IF TRUE
> This is asking whether you possess a real detail the manuscript lacks. Add it only if it is true. If nothing comes, leave the prose alone.

> [!success] KEEP
> This is a reminder that a clue or event is structurally important. You can rewrite every word while preserving what happens.

## Example

Suppose the prose says:

> I put my hand against the door and understood that it was cold.

You can simply edit it in the chapter:

> I put my hand on the door. It was cold. I left it there too long.

You do not need to highlight the new words or make them red. The history of the original is safe in [[The Crowning]], so the workshop copy is where you are allowed to damage, test, add, and change things.

If you dislike both versions, delete the sentence and write your own.

## Three passes for each chapter

### Pass one — Mine

Change anything that you would never naturally say. Add the facts you are embarrassed to add. Do not worry about elegance.

### Pass two — The book

Check the green box. Make sure the physical clues and necessary events survived your first pass.

### Pass three — Sound

Read the chapter aloud. Fix only what your mouth refuses. Do not make every sentence beautiful again.

## How to bring a chapter back to me

After editing, you can say:

> Read my workshop version of Chapter III. Preserve my changes. Tell me what became stronger, what broke structurally, and ask me before rewriting it.

That lets your prose remain the authority. I can check the physics and continuity without silently replacing your decisions.

## Important safety rule

Work only in the files inside the `The Crowning — Novella` folder. The continuous [[The Crowning]] file is the preserved manuscript. We merge a finished chapter back only when you explicitly ask.
