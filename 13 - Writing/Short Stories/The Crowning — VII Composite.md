# VII composite

Workshop only. Composite of the current chapter and the cold pass. This covers the four-second event through the crown diagnosis; it is not yet a replacement in the chapter file.

Then the nearest room opened.

In the nearest room there was a man and somebody with him.

He was not a better man than me. I looked. I looked hard and for a long time. I want to say I was searching for a reason, and there was not one. I have gone over it since. There is still not one.

One thumbnail was black at the edge. That was what I found.

She came behind him while he worked at something in his hands, a strap or a buckle. She put her palm flat against the back of his neck, under the hair, and left it there.

He did not stop what he was doing.

He did not stop.

He kept working at the strap. He said something over his shoulder that was not about her and laughed at the end. Her hand remained and then went away. He never looked up.

Four seconds.

Four full seconds. I counted them because I count every one I have been given. There have been eleven and I know the dates and I could feel you behind my right shoulder and I lowered my head a little not enough to ask for anything there is an amount a man can move and still say he was only changing how he stood and the air reached the place below my hair and he received four and did not look up and did not know and will not remember it tomorrow and it cost him nothing it cost him absolutely nothing he did not even have to be present for it he was somewhere else the whole time and she gave it to him anyway and will give him another tonight and he will not notice that one either and I stood in the dark and wanted it so badly that my mouth filled and I swallowed and it came back and my hands went over the wall and I would have taken it from him I would have taken it out of the air I would have entered the room and taken it and I knew in that second that I would have and something in me that had been standing for a very long time went out from under me

and I called it running.

There is nowhere on that floor to run away to. I went along the wall with one shoulder striking it and my hands over my ears, which did nothing because this is not a floor you hear. At the first divider my foot landed badly. At the second I missed the floor and went down far enough to catch myself, then got up and continued.

The thing on my head moved when I ran. Not the way a weight moves. It went with my head and then went on going, half a beat late, lifting at the back the way a light thing lifts in disturbed air. I heard its edge touch my hair.

At the far end my knees struck stone.

There was spit hanging from my mouth. I wiped it with the back of my hand before I looked toward you. The hand left my mouth and went to the place I had opened to the air.

The thing was exactly as warm as the skin beneath it. Exactly the temperature of the place where her hand had rested on him.

I decided that proved the heat was spreading.
