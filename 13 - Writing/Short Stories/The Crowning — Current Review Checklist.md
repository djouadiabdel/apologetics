# Current review checklist

This is a menu, not unfinished work. Pick any chapter, read the named passage, and change it only if something truer arrives. “I kept it” is a complete answer.

When you report back, give me the chapter, the old line, and your new line—or tell me you kept it.

After she puts her hand on the wall instead of in his, what does his unused hand physically do? Add one literal movement only if you can see it. Do not explain it.
