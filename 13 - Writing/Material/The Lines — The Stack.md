## What this file cannot know

The parser reads a sender field and a string. It cannot tell my own writing from a quote, a paste, a lyric, or a bit. Cross-checking every line against hollow-sky and the vault caught one (the orange). It cannot catch **other people's words in my mouth**, because those leave no trace in my files. Aya's sentence was found that way only because I recognised it.

So this stack is unverified on authorship until I read it. Anything here that is a quote, mark it.

Not farmed: every thread under 700 words.
