Raw material from the night of 13-14 August. Nine feelings, each with what was actually said, the Girard hook, and a situation.

**Rules carried over from [[Girard Story Engine]].** The concepts on this page are author-side only. No character names a mechanism, no narrator understands the structure, and if a reader can lift the concept out as a stated idea the story failed. 2,000 to 2,500 words, first person, one person who does not know what is happening to them.

**Guard.** The woman on the page is not her. Same ruling as The Book of Elara. If a piece starts becoming a report, stop and change something structural, not just the name.

---

## 1. The word that made it cheap

**What happened.** Asked about situationships at random, she answered *"well a situationship is us."* He defined it himself: nothing official, short term, *"a random swing hookup as they do outside but for poor people,"* *"like that car you rent because you can't afford or you don't want."* She refused it twice: *"More like not related to a person.. cuz both gyal and man are free !!"* and *"Deeper than that."*

**The live thing.** He supplied the insult and then took the wound from it. His own sentence has two clauses in it and he assumed he was the first one.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#1. Stage one: desire is borrowed]]. The spec sheet he graded the thing against was never his, and the cheap definition came from watching other people.

**Situation.** A man explains to someone what a thing is worth, using a comparison he invents on the spot, and spends the rest of the night inside the comparison. The listener corrects him once and he does not hear it.

## 2. Believing an accurate thing

**What happened.** She says I love you more. Kisses, voice notes, replies to every message ever sent. His question at five in the morning: *"what is my fault now for thinking that a girl saying to me i love you more is truly interested in me?"*

**The live thing.** He read the signals correctly. What did not follow was availability. Interest and unavailability sat in the same person the whole time and nothing in the messages distinguished them.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#2. Stage two: the only variable that matters is distance]]. Nothing was misread. The distance changed.

**Situation.** Someone assembles a complete and correct case, presents it, and is told the case is right and the conclusion does not follow.

---

## 3. Asking permission

**What happened.** Monday 10 August. He came to her crying because he missed her, wanting to be told it was not his fault that he misses her, that it hurts, that it is okay to love missing her. She answered: don't feel that, don't tell yourself bad things, it won't help, nothing helps. He stopped crying and hated himself for days. Silence until 18:00, one message, she replied around 20:00.

**The live thing.** He did not ask for comfort. He asked her to authorise a desire. The moment she was placed as the one who licenses wanting, she became the gate on it.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#5. Stage four, symptom two: master and slave]] and the deviated transcendence material in [[GIRARD'S DECEIT, DESIRE AND THE NOVEL — COMPLETE CONCEPT EXTRACTION]]. This is also the engine already running in [[The Crowning — Girard Sourcebook]], where the narrator descends explaining himself to a woman who never speaks.

**Situation.** A man goes to a specific person for a ruling he could get nowhere else, and the refusal is not cruel, it is administrative. She simply does not issue those.

## 4. The dedication coming down

**What happened.** The front page of the novella said *"For Ari, who lights my world"* and a note about a prayer answered, grace, not earned. Hours after the situationship word he wanted it gone, along with every love-related story on the site. He did not delete the stories. He replaced the dedication with sixteen verses of regret.

**The live thing.** The note was a claim about his own standing, not only about her. When the status stopped being co-signed, everything resting on it went at once. He converted a public claim into a private hint and left the book intact.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#3. Stage three: double mediation, the closed circuit]]. Each one holds the other's regard and only one can hold it at a time.

**Situation.** Someone quietly removes a dedication from a finished book and replaces it with something nobody will decode, then cannot stop checking whether the page looks different.

## 5. Going general when she went personal

**What happened.** Twice in one night. She said kind people do get helped, he answered *"they help others they don't get helped."* Later she said the horror is that it might happen again, he answered that human beings suffer when they are alone. After the second one she closed the subject with thanks for satisfying her curiosity.

**The live thing.** The general sentence is the ask, in disguise. It arrives exactly when the other person has just been specific.

**Girard.** [[Girard Story Engine]], the rule about narrators who explain. See also the operational law in `The Crowning — Revision Map`: a general sentence produced as a defence is evidence, one that summarises a scene is authorial.

**Situation.** Every time the other person says something true about themselves, the narrator answers with something true about everybody. Write it so the reader counts the misses and the narrator never does.

---

## 6. Being handed the worst thing

**What happened.** She asked about suicidal thoughts, prefaced with respectfully I'm curious, saying it was for herself. Then in the third person, a past attempt, then *"The gap after doing it .. but ur alive .. but what it happens again thatts horror."* At the end, thanks for satisfying my curiosity, and the cover story went back on.

**The live thing.** He got the thing he had been asking for all summer, to be needed for something specific and effortful, in a currency he would never have chosen. And the door closed politely afterwards.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#7. Stage five: the person who is not in the room]]. Something is being carried that was never introduced, and it shapes everything without appearing.

**Situation.** A person is trusted with the one thing that matters and discovers that being trusted with it changes nothing about their standing. Keep the narrator sure that it will.

---

## 7. Contempt as anaesthetic

**What happened.** At midnight she was grace and an answered prayer, printed on a public page. By five in the morning: *"she is just a useless person to me,"* *"she isn't even a good friend,"* *"because of her I stopped the psalters and everything else."*

**The live thing.** The last one is false and checkable. The schedule is dated, six weeks, running to the 26th, and nobody took it away. Contempt arrives fast, works immediately, and bills later.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#8. Stage six: the fake exits]]. Devaluing the model is one of the exits that is not an exit.

**Situation.** A narrator lists everything the other person cost him. Each item is checkable and the reader can check them. He cannot.

---

## 8. Grief with no licence

**What happened.** Nothing was named, so nothing ended, so there is nothing to mourn and no permission to mourn it. This is the verse already on the front plate: the delight of your eyes taken at a stroke, and you will not mourn, and you will not weep, and your tears will not come.

**The live thing.** The undefined thing has no funeral rite. This is the deepest idea the word situationship gave him and it is the one worth a whole story.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#6. Stage four, symptom three: Flaubert's law]].

**Situation.** A man attends a funeral for someone he is not related to, and the whole story is about where he is allowed to stand in the room.

---

## 9. Two currencies

**What happened.** He sent spoken goodnights, voice notes, warmth said out loud. She answers at night in writing. On 13 August he sent *"goodnight sweety"* and nothing else. She kept the conversation going anyway.

**The live thing.** He was paying in the live, spoken, unkeepable register and being answered in the filed and burnable one. See [[feedback-addressee-may-read-it]] logic, two women, two grammars, running in real life instead of on the page.

**Girard.** [[Girard in a Relationship — The Whole Thing#4. Stage four, symptom one: coquetry]]. Author-side only, and not a verdict on anybody real.

**Situation.** Two people write to each other for a year in different media and neither ever mentions it. One keeps everything. The other one speaks and keeps nothing.
