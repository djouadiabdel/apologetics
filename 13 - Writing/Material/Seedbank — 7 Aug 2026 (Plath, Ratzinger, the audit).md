# 🧨 Seedbank, night of 7-8 August 2026

One sitting. Bell Jar ch. XI-XIV, Ratzinger's *Eschatology* ch. VII, the seven capital vices, and a conversation with Ari. Everything below came out of that night. Nothing here is finished.

---

> [!abstract] The best idea in this file, so it goes first
> **The night itself is the story.** A man spends eight hours working out, at speed and with real understanding, that a person is constituted by relation, that we are ourselves only as being in others, that someone else can hold a true thing about you when you cannot hold it yourself. He gets it. He is thrilled by it. Then at three in the morning he asks a girl to say four words, she doesn't, and he cries.
>
> The theology is exactly about the thing he can't get. He has the whole apparatus and it doesn't reach the floor.
> Nothing needs to be invented. The shape is already there.

## 1. Images that are already story-shaped

**From Plath**
- The body as an adversary that saves you against your will. Hands going limp on the cord. The water spitting her up *"like a cork."*
- Cooking the hotdog correctly by watching everybody else, then burying it in the sand when nobody is looking.
- Weeding the dead flowers out of the vases, then the dying ones, and laying them in a basin *"cold as a tomb"* before she can enter the room.
- Buying a black raincoat with the last money in the account, from a salesgirl who explains it is only showerproofed. Buying your shroud and being upsold.
- A breakdown told entirely as capacities lost with counters attached. Seven nights, three weeks, reading gone, writing gone, the handwriting itself. No scene for the turn.
- Under the machine: *"I wondered what terrible thing it was that I had done."* The victim looking for the crime after the punishment.
- Putting the chair back in the exact spot before going down to the cellar.

**From Ratzinger**
- Purgatory is the rehab, not the surgery. The bone is united and the man will not put the leg down.
- A fire that is a person. Being burned by the arrival of love rather than by punishment.
- **Reperfusion injury.** Blood returns to starved tissue and the return of the good thing is what damages it.
- *Existenzzeit.* A duration that cannot be measured on a clock and is not eternal either.
- A version of you that exists only inside someone else, that you have no access to and cannot inspect.
- **Chimerism.** A man whose blood is manufactured by a stranger's cells. Anonymous donor, never meet, never learn his name, inside him for the rest of his life.
- Péguy: *J'espère en toi pour moi.* I hope in you for me.
- Someone holding your hope because you cannot hold it while you are the one on the scales.
- **Acedia**, the noonday demon. Not laziness. The monk at the fourth hour checking the sun, going to the window, leaving, coming back, reading two lines, imagining other cities and kinder brothers. The remedy is not to cheer up. The remedy is **stay**.
- *'avon*, crookedness. Sin as a **shape**, not an act.
- Athanasius: made from nothing, so turning away is turning back toward nothing. Corruption is not the penalty, it is the direction.

**From this month**
- A person who replies beautifully and never begins.
- Asking for one sentence and receiving twenty others, all warm, none of them it.
- A limit placed inside a gift and handed over together. *For now.*
- The audit. Counting seconds, read receipts, minutes of a skincare routine.
- Settling the verdict by four in the morning, alone, with no new information, and calling it knowing.
- Never being wrong afterwards because you never let anything get as far as afterwards.
- A fear that survives the removal of its own stated cause, and moves on to the next one without pausing.
- Wanting a title that would change no behaviour at all, because the thing wanted is a guarantee and not a behaviour.
- Being thanked for your words by someone who sleeps happily because of them.
- Two cities, three hours of road, and a family that will not let her travel.

## 2. Premises, not images

1. **Donor.** A man receives marrow from an anonymous donor and cannot stop thinking that a stranger is making his blood. He wants to find them. They will never meet and the cells will outlive the search. Intercession as fiction, with nothing religious in it.
2. **The Fourth Hour.** A man who decides every night how things will end, by four, and is never wrong, because he closes everything before it can reach afterwards. He has a word for this and the word is careful.
3. **The Report.** A footballer cleared to play who cannot plant on the leg. The whole story is the gap between the film and the trust, and nobody in it can supply the trust for him.
4. **Noonday.** Evagrius's monk transposed to a sixth-year medical student in Khenchela who cannot stay in the room. Checks the time, goes out, comes back, opens a folder, closes it. The story ends with him staying.
5. **Chimera.** After a transplant a woman's blood type converts to her donor's. Her family treats it as a medical curiosity. She treats it as a theological event and nobody will discuss it with her.
6. **The Count.** Expansion of *The Other Thing*. A man keeping a private ledger of what he sends and what arrives in its place, which is always good and is always the other one.
7. **The Flowers.** The maternity-ward scene relocated to an Algerian hospital. A volunteer who cannot enter a room full of new mothers until she has sorted the dying flowers from the dead.

## 3. Forms and moves worth stealing

- **KJV narration wrapped around a blank-verse scene.** Invented in *The Book of Elara*, 6 Aug. That hybrid is mine and nobody else is doing it. Reusable for any subject where the frame must sound older than the argument.
- **Breakdown as inventory.** Plath ch. X-XII. Give only lost capacities with numbers and refuse the causal scene entirely.
- **Hard cuts with no transitions.** Plath ch. XIII: beach, hospital, graveyard, cellar, no time markers. The reader supplies the passage of time or doesn't.
- **The clinical case carrying the metaphysical claim.** *As Tolerated*, and it works better than any essay would. Find the ward version of the idea before writing the idea.
- **State the strongest objection to yourself first, twice.** Ratzinger does this with Gnilka and then with his own substitution problem, and it is most of why he sounds unanswerable. This is the move my apologetics writing usually skips.

## 4. The six mental models, for fiction

1. **Person vs nature.** Test: can this character do something out of character that is still recognisably them? If no, it is a nature with a name.
2. **Every real scene contains an address and a response.** Things that merely happen are weather.
3. **Defective permeation.** A real decision plus a long lag in which the old pattern keeps firing. Arcs are not switches.
4. **Characterise by what someone is in three other people**, not by inventory. Characterisation with zero description.
5. **The strongest tragic ending is declining, not being destroyed.** And accepting has to have been genuinely available.
6. **Romance runs on unequal custody.** Each holds a version of the other that the other cannot reach. Misunderstanding becomes structural, not contrived.

## 5. Confirmed about my own writing, from the same night

- The pieces that work have an **addressee**. *The Third Word* has an angel who never answers. *As Tolerated* has a patient who refuses. *Nothing Is Wrong* had nobody in the room, which is exactly why its ending went slack.
- Fix applied to *Nothing Is Wrong*: cut the dead simile, cut the stacked disavowal, and replace the closing verdict with present-tense wanting plus a scheduled repetition. The narrator sees it clearly and books the next one for Friday.
- I skip descriptive passages by choice and the events are hidden inside them. This is true in French **and** in English. Slow down where a paragraph is all objects and no reasoning.
- **Description is never decoration. Every object is an argument.** Gordon's beige windowless waiting room. Pink marble like tinned salmon. Graves like an oblong bathtub full of dirt.
