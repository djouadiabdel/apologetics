# Magnesium

I take 400 at ten and I am down by eleven. Not in my bed. At my desk.

It took me a while to get the dose right. Glycinate, not oxide. Oxide does nothing except send you to the bathroom. I read the literature on it, and there is more of it than you would think.

I was buying the same brand before. It did not work then. I assumed the dose was wrong, so I went to 600, and then to 800, which is enough to be an actual problem, and it still did not work. I remember standing in the kitchen doing arithmetic about my own body at two-thirty and feeling reasonable about it.

The dose was never the issue. I was reading a message again. That was most nights. You read it once for what it says, then again to see whether it says the thing you thought it said, then a third time to put the hour it was sent against the hour she was awake and see what the gap comes to. You can run that until four. I have a fair amount of data on it.

Now I am up at four maybe twice a week and it is because I chose it. I have learned things by heart at four, in a language I am still bad at, lines I will still have in twenty years. I have argued with a Frenchman who has been dead eleven years and lost. At that hour nobody needs anything from you and nothing you do has to be explained to anybody. I would not give it back.

I am the only one awake.

The hours are the same hours. I worked it out once, out of curiosity, and it is almost exactly one to four, which is what it was at the worst of it.

I thought that was funny. I said it out loud to someone and they did not laugh.

I still keep the magnesium in the drawer. I sleep fine now. It is cheap, and I would rather have it there than not.

I started taking saffron last month. For mood, they say. I did not tell anyone I had started.

It makes the dreams vivid. That is in the literature too. I read the literature.

She is in most of them. I have not stopped taking it.
