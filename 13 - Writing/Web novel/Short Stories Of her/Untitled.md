I have a story where the hurt is a certificate.

It goes like this. I got hurt because I am good. The other one does not get hurt because he is not. He takes what is in front of him and he sleeps fine and nothing follows him into the morning, and the reason nothing follows him is that there is nothing in him for it to catch on. My pain proves I have an interior. His comfort proves he lacks one.

I spoke of that out loud but sang it to no one. I have thought it in some form nearly every week for years.
