# Good With Actions

I am not good with words. Everyone who says it says it the same way, a little proud, and what they mean under it is that the account is closed and nothing further is owed.

I do not need to be told. I want that down first, before the rest, because the rest is going to look like something else.

The dream starts at ten past four on the road to Batna. My mother has two bags and one of them is not hers, it is full of things for people in Alger who did not ask for them. The airport is fifty minutes out. She is awake and I am driving and neither of us says anything, which is how we have always travelled.

I message you at the lights by the roundabout. You say you are in Alger until Sunday.

I read it twice. The light changes and I have already decided to get on the plane.

That is the whole engine of the dream and it took nine seconds. My mother asks me nothing about it. She moves her bag onto her lap to make room for mine and that is her entire comment on the matter.

You are at the gate in Alger with your hair up. You are laughing before I reach you, at what I do not know, and I never find out.

By evening we are at Tunis and the sign over the road says Carthage. I am not surprised by that in the way a man awake would be.

The father there is a good man. I stood at the font for his son four years ago and held him over the water with both hands and the boy was heavy and furious and would not stop kicking.

Water on its own is a wash. Augustine says take away the word and what is the water except water, and the word comes to the element and it becomes the other thing. Not a better wash. The other thing.

He did not mean it as advice about marriage. I am taking it as that.

You want to know why I will not let this go, so here it is, and I am aware I have had all day to build it.

Chrysostom tells a husband to say to his wife, I have taken you in my arms, and I love you, and I prefer you to my life itself. He says do not call her by her name only, call her with love. He is preaching in a city where a husband fed a household and guarded it and worked himself into the ground for it. Those men are sitting in front of him. He tells them it is not enough. Go home and say it out loud.

Sixteen hundred years and nobody has needed to write the opposite homily. Nobody has ever had to tell a man to stop talking and buy bread.

And the law is worse than the homily. Consent makes the marriage. Not the house, not the bed, not the years. Two people say a sentence each in front of a witness and it is done, and if they never say it, then everything else they do together is a long friendly arrangement with good food in it.

You are writing in your notebook while I say this. You always write while I talk. I have never once been able to see what you put in it.

She was good with actions. That was the phrase and she said it about herself the way you would state a qualification.

I can give you the actions. She cooked. She was there. She did the other thing and she was generous about it and neither of us pretended it was small. She came when I called at bad hours. Not one of those is nothing and I will not sit here and call it nothing.

But I could not have told you, then or now, what she thought I was. Two years, and I could not produce one sentence out of her mouth with me in it. Every single thing she gave me could have been given to a man who happened to be standing there.

A deed can be aimed at anybody. Only a word can have my name in it.

And the research is not on the other side of this, which surprised me, because I expected the research to be on the side of the quiet ones.

There is a whole field on what they call responsiveness, which is not what you did but whether the other one came away believing you understood him. That belief is the thing that predicts everything. Not the act. The landing.

They have a category for the partner who listens well and says little. Passive constructive. Quiet but interested. It sits in the tables next to the bad ones, and people on the receiving end of it report their partner as less responsive and report themselves as less satisfied, which is to say that being silently loved reads, from inside, as being tolerated.

And the love languages thing that everyone repeats, the one where you announce which one you are and are excused from the others, has been tested properly now and it does not hold. People do not reliably have one. Matching to it predicts nothing much. What did come out of the data, twice, in samples of hundreds, is that words of affirmation predicted felt love better than whatever the person had named as their own primary language.

So the man who says I am not good with words has picked the one to skip that the numbers say is load bearing, and he has been allowed to say it at parties for thirty years and be nodded at.

You put the pen down and you said, and if someone means it and cannot get it out.

Then he is a man with a debt and no currency, and I have never said he is a liar. I said the debt is real. He does not get to close the file by announcing his handwriting is bad.

The wedding is on the Sunday and I want to get it down while I still have it.

It is hot and the doors are open and you can hear the sea through them, not see it. My mother is there, which is not possible, since she was in Alger, and in the dream nobody raises this. She has her hands folded in front of her the way she does in a place she has decided to approve of. The father's boy is eight now and he is carrying something he has been told twice not to drop.

The oil goes on and it is warm from being held. There is singing and I do not know the words and I stand in it. The crowns are heavier than they look and mine sits badly and you laugh at it, in the church, out loud, and the father waits for you the way you wait for weather.

I am happy. I want that written plainly because I have not written it before and I do not want to be clever about it. I was happy the whole hour.

Before the crowns I asked the father whether anyone was going to ask me to say anything.

He said the rite does not require it.

I asked him to ask me anyway.

He looked at me for a moment and then he did, and I said it, and my voice came out level, and then you said yours and you used my name.

It went in. I felt it go in. And it did not stay, and I stood there under the crown with the oil still wet at my hairline doing arithmetic on when it would be reasonable to hear it again.

I asked you before the oil had dried.

You said it again. You did not ask why, which is worse than asking, and you put your hand flat on my chest while you said it as though you were checking whether the first one had arrived.

Nothing further was owed. I asked anyway.

I woke at ten to six and lay there and did the accounting.

I have built a doctrine out of Augustine and Chrysostom and canon law and four decent studies. Every load bearing wall of it is there to make it acceptable that I need to be told. And told again. And told at the same hour tomorrow.

The doctrine is still true. That is the part nobody warns you about. I did not go looking for it because it was true.

And I am not taking any of it back. If you say it to me I am going to ask you to say it once more, and I know what that is now and I am going to do it anyway.

The word comes to the element. That is all that happens.

# Working notes

Claim is the second line. *I do not need to be told.* The charge nobody made. Everything after it is the defense and the defense is what convicts.

Trapdoor is behaviour, not analysis. *I asked you before the oil had dried.* One line, no crumble, and the wedding is still good on both sides of it.

The father not requiring the words and me asking him to ask is the piece in miniature. The rite does not need my sentence. I needed to be asked for it. That is in before any of the argument lands, so a reader who is paying attention has me three pages early.

Echo carried over: *nothing further is owed* in the opening, *nothing further was owed, I asked anyway* at the turn. Same words, now about me. Same move as the thigh in As Tolerated.

*Being silently loved reads, from inside, as being tolerated* is a deliberate hook back to As Tolerated. Nothing in either points at the other.

The one before is never named and never argued with. Her actions are listed straight and none of them is called small, because the piece dies the second it becomes a case against a woman.

Don't move: the doctrine stays true and I keep doing the thing anyway. No conversion in the last paragraph.

## Sources, in order of appearance

**Augustine**, *Tractates on the Gospel of John* 80.3. *Detrahe verbum, et quid est aqua nisi aqua? Accedit verbum ad elementum, et fit sacramentum.* Take away the word and what is the water except water. The word comes to the element and it becomes a sacrament. Said about baptism, borrowed here.

**John Chrysostom**, *Homily 20 on Ephesians*. Say to her, I have taken you in my arms, and I love you, and I prefer you to my life itself. Also, never call her simply by her name, but with terms of endearment, with honour, with much love. Preached to men who were already providing.

**Ulpian**, *Digest* 50.17.30, taken into canon law. *Nuptias non concubitus sed consensus facit.* Not intercourse but consent makes a marriage. Still the operative principle in the Latin canon on matrimonial consent.

**Perceived partner responsiveness.** Reis and Shaver's construct, meaning the other one's belief that you understood, validated and cared. It predicts intimacy better than the support actually delivered. Crasta et al. 2021 in *Psychological Assessment* built the current measure and its two dimensions are Responsiveness and Insensitivity, which is the whole argument in two column headings.

**Passive constructive is a real category and it does badly.** Bosisio et al. 2022, *Journal of Social and Personal Relationships*, 151 cohabiting couples filmed in a lab. Perceiving your partner as passive constructive, quiet but interested, was linked to lower sexual satisfaction, not higher. Tavares et al. 2025 in *Journal of Family Psychology*, 240 couples over 21 days of diaries, found the same thing on the responsiveness measure. The quiet supportive partner is not neutral in the data.

**Love languages do not hold up.** Impett, Park and Muise 2024 in *Current Directions in Psychological Science* went through the three core claims and none of them is supported, and they propose a balanced diet rather than a language. Flicker et al. 2024 in *Journal of Marital and Family Therapy*, n = 696 preregistered, found that satisfaction with your partner's primary love language predicted nothing better than the others, and that **words of affirmation and quality time predicted felt love and satisfaction better than the person's own stated primary language.** Replicated in Flicker et al. 2025, n = 499, where verbal affirmation came out as one of the most robust predictors.

That last finding is the one worth keeping. The folk theory exists to let a man opt out of the one thing that turns out to be load bearing for everybody.
