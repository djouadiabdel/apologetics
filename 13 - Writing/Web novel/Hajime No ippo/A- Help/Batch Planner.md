**A few chapters after that — The name drop**

Fujii mentions him by name in conversation with Mari. Casual. "Ah, [name] wrote something interesting about the last fight." Mari responds with something that reveals she's been watching him too — maybe skeptically. This tells the reader that the existing cast is already aware of him as a person, not just a presence. The reader is now where Fujii and Mari are — noticing him, forming an opinion, waiting to see what he becomes.

**Then when you're ready — The door opens**

His first real scene. And because you've been planting seeds, the reader doesn't feel ambushed by a new character. They feel like they're finally getting to meet someone they've been glimpsing in the periphery.


# THE BATCH PLANNING GUIDE

## Use this after finishing a batch of chapters. Paste it to Claude with your notes.

---

## STEP 1: WHERE ARE WE?

Fill in the blanks. Keep it messy. One or two sentences each.

**Last chapter I finished:** Chapter ___

**What happened (just the events, no analysis):**

**Which character changed their relationship to something between the start and end of this batch?** (If nobody did — that's fine, but it means a soul chapter is overdue.)

## STEP 2: MAP THE ACTIVE TRIANGLES

A triangle = someone wants something THROUGH someone else. Format: [Character] → [Mediator] → [Object] The mediator is the person whose existence makes the object desirable or painful.

**List every triangle currently running in the story:**

Example:

- Itagaki → Ippo → Japanese Title
- Itagaki → Imai → Inter-High dignity he never had
- Ippo → Miyata → "the promised fight" / recognition
- Kamogawa → his own past → Ippo's career

Your current triangles: 1. 2. 3. 4. (add more if needed)

**Which triangle is the LOUDEST right now?** (The one the reader is most aware of, the one driving the current chapters.)

**Which triangle is SILENT but ticking?** (Something seeded earlier that hasn't detonated yet. The blond figure. A character who hasn't reacted to something they should have.)

## STEP 3: WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

## STEP 4: THE SOUL CHAPTER CHECK

For the chapter(s) you marked as MOVE, answer these:

**What triangle is changing?**

**What is the character's body going to do BEFORE their mind catches up?** (This is your entry point into the scene. The body knows first.)

**What is the one image or moment that made you think "this chapter matters"?** (This is the seed. Protect it. Build around it.)

---

## STEP 5: THE VIBE CHAPTER CHECK

For the chapters you marked as HOLD, answer these:

**What's the FUN of this chapter?** (Not the plot purpose — the fun. What moment are you looking forward to writing? If there isn't one, find one before you start.)

**What is one sensory detail that grounds the reader in this world?** (A sound, a smell, a texture. Just one. This is your opening line seed.)

## REMINDERS

- Soul chapters are NOT always the "big" chapters. A quiet chapter where Kamogawa touches a glove and realizes something can be a soul chapter. A title fight where nothing shifts internally can be a vibe chapter.
    
- The ratio is flexible. Sometimes you get 5 vibe chapters between soul chapters. Sometimes two soul chapters land back to back. Follow the triangles, not a formula.
    
- You don't have to fill this perfectly. Messy is fine. The point is to THINK about the triangles before you draft, so the drafting is free.
    
- If you can't tell whether a chapter is soul or vibe — draft it as vibe. If something opens up while you're writing, let it become soul. The category lives in you, not in the outline.
