# Heart

The word is heart. Nobody in this gym has ever defined it. They watch a boy take four to give one, and they need a word for why that is admirable instead of a thing you would stop, and heart is the word they have.

I taught them the word. I want that down before anything else.

He is twenty three. Fifty one rounds this year. Two nights ago he was hit ninety four times, and at the end of it he was standing with both hands up and eleven thousand people were on their feet, and I was the only man in that building doing arithmetic.

He will not slip. That is the thing. He can. I taught him and he does it clean on the pads and he does it clean in sparring, and then the bell goes and he walks forward into it, because walking forward is the only way he has ever been sure he is in the room.

And he does it well. Nobody says that part. He has a technique for taking it. He turns his head a quarter of an inch at the last moment so it lands on bone instead of on the hinge, and he does it without thinking now. Nine years of practice. It is a real skill and I am the one who gave it to him.

Here is the mechanism and there is no other side to it.

The tissue does not repair. Every one is permanent and they add. A man is not concussed and then well again, he is concussed and then carrying it, and the reason the twentieth one takes something from him is the nineteen underneath. For years there is nothing to see. Then there is something to see, and by then you are not deciding anything, you are being told.

I have known this for thirty years. I am not a man who does not know.

The doctor came to the gym on Thursday and sat where the boys sit and said what doctors say, and he was right, and he was polite about it, and I listened with my arms crossed like an old fool defending his house.

I told him the boy would not stop.

He said, that is not what I asked you.

I have gone over that four times. It is a small sentence and I cannot get past it.

The boy came in on Friday with his eye still closed and asked me what I thought of the third round, and I told him the truth about his feet, because his feet were bad in the third and he knows it, and we worked on his feet for an hour.

There are six other things I could have told him about and I picked his feet.

He is the easiest person to teach I have ever had in here. That is not a compliment to me. He believes what I say in the way children believe weather, and if I told him tomorrow that the style was killing him he would stop that afternoon and never ask me a second question about it.

So it is not that he would not stop.

It is that I have not said it.

I said a version of it once, in February, badly, half of it, the way you say a thing so you can say later that you said it. He listened and then he said something I have not been able to answer since.

He said, if I stop doing it that way I do not know what I am.

Which is the correct sentence. I have no reply to it that is not a lie, because I have lived on that same sentence since I was nineteen and it is the only thing I have ever been able to teach anybody.

There was another one. 1979. He was better than this one. He is alive and he lives with his sister and he is fine, and I use that word the way the gym uses heart.

I told myself for a long time that the science was not there yet, that nobody knew, that I did what any man would have done with what was in front of him. That is true. It is also the shape of an excuse and I know the shape, I have heard it from men in this room about worse things and I made my face when they said it.

Here is what I have not told anybody.

I did not pick this boy because he could punch. I found out about the punch later, weeks later, and I was as surprised as anyone. I picked him because I saw a boy get up.

He had been held down by three of them and he got up, and I stood across the road and something in me said, there he is.

For nine years I have told people I saw talent. What I saw was a boy who did not know how to stay down, and I have spent nine years making sure he never learns.

He will be here at four. He is always early. He will ask me how I thought it went and he will mean it, he will actually want to know, and he will stand there with his gloves under his arm waiting for me to tell him.

Tomorrow I am putting him in with the tall southpaw, because he needs the work.

The word is heart. I will use it again tomorrow.
