# Same Script

**The charge.** *Same script.* His own two words, typed under a forwarded screenshot.

**Whose words this is built from.** Hers and Khadija's, roughly evenly, and his in between. He never says the thing the piece is about.

## The finding that makes it a piece

He says the women are running the same script on him. The record says he ran the same script on them, and that one of them broke it.

**Good boy / good girl. Eleven times to one, eleven times to the other.** Counted across both threads. The same phrase, the same number of times, to two women who do not know each other.

Other moves, counted: pet name (mommy/mama) 52 to Ari, 12 to Khadija. Claiming (*my girl*, *be mine*) 10 and 8. Asking for a verdict 2 and 10. *I love you* 6 and 2.

## The difference, which is real and is the whole second half

Khadija's register is **interest**. Her own words, repeatedly: *i already am interested in the way u write*, *nice interest u have*, *are u interested in Islam*, *so u chose the most unwanted subject in the world and said to urself ye I'm interested*. Grep her side for care words and what comes back is the word *interested*.

Ari's register is **care**. *what did u eat*. *miss u already*. *i hope ur doing amazing slept well and feel well*.

## Where the floor goes

**1 August, 10:11 to 10:19. Eight minutes, verified line by line.**

- 10:11 — *Heloooollllloooooooo and good morning, i hope ur doing amazing slept well and feel well*
- 10:12 — *Anddd these messages omg, i read them multiple times, I'll answer everything*
- 10:12 — *Sorry for replying late, granny is make the use of me lol (her sis passed away mskina)*
- 10:13 — *I don't even know how to begin because your words reached places in me I thought had gone quiet*
- 10:15 — *Thank you for trusting me with your heart like this. I read every word, and I promise none of it was taken lightly. You made me feel seen and cared*
- 10:16 — *And kinda likkke*
- 10:18 — *it isn't because I don't appreciate you I truly do. But right now, I need to choose healing instead of starting another relationship*
- 10:19 — *The fact that ur texting me at 4:00 am then at 6:00am i feel some pain*

She refused him with more care in eight minutes than most people manage in a year, and she noticed the 4 a.m. and the 6 a.m. and said it hurt her.

**What he did with it.** He copied out the one sentence that was the refusal, sent it to a different woman, and wrote **same script** underneath. The seven minutes around it did not go with it.

That is the floor. He is the one who flattened her into a script, and he did it by quoting her accurately.

## The shape

Both registers run in parallel, unlabelled, so the reader sorts them. The eight minutes arrive whole and undivided, one block, late. Then the forward, then the two words. Nothing reflective after.

## Title

***Didymus***. The Twin. The one who was handed the thing by everybody who had seen it and would not have it without putting his hand in.
