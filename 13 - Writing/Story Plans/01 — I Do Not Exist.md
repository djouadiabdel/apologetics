# I Do Not Exist

**The charge.** *Idk I don't think I have a personality do you? Do we? Idk.*

**Whose words this is built from.** Mostly Khadija's, and that is the problem with it. She says the sharpest version of it and I agree with her afterwards. If this gets written, the narrator has to be doing something while she talks, or it becomes her essay with my name on it.

**The form.** Two people agreeing they are empty, and continuing to talk every day for two more weeks.

## Where the floor goes

He says he isn't there, and then needs one specific person to say he is. The published story already holds the only moment he existed and it took someone else's mouth to do it.

## Open

The balance. Her lines are better than his on this subject and the piece has to survive that.
