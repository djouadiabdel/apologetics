# The Father

**The charge. Settled 21 Aug: *father is father regardless.*** His own sentence, typed to a girl who was asking whether he was really the father she was confessing to. He said it about himself while playing the role. It acquits his father and convicts him in the same five words, which is what the plan was reaching for with *he's fine, he's just like that* (that line survives as an insistence).

**Drafted.** `Drafts/The Father/Draft 08.md`, titled ***The Confessor***.

**Whose words this is built from.** Mine, entirely. He never appears in his own voice, only in reported speech and in what he did.

## Corrections from the verification pass, 21 Aug

Five of the eleven evidence lines were read wrong or thin. All are replies, which is the standing failure mode.

- **The hammer is a reply.** She was telling him her own father caught her with a boy, refused to hear it, hit her, and she packed and went to her grandmother's. Forty seconds before, he had typed *nooo i don't wanna see u being hit.* Then he answered her beating with his own, flat, and she laughed. It is not an opening piece of evidence, it is a matching move.
- **The water is a reply.** She said she drinks one cup a day when she remembers. He answered with the water ban and changed the subject to Georgian chant inside the same minute. She later said she buys her own water too, to challenge herself.
- **The *fuck off* line is a reply, and it is the centre of the piece.** She had just said she cried at school, went home, told her dad, and *he hugged me*. He answered: *If I tell my dad anything he would just say fuck off LOL.* Then, unprompted: *Good dad good dad.*
- **The blond-hair line is a reply.** She was arguing that men here do as they like and she gets called a whore for less. He supplied his father as her evidence, then told her he liked how she thinks.
- **The clinic and taxes fight is already spent.** It is in *The Man of God* at fragments 39 and 40, with the thirty patients. So is *ydirli 4*. Both are out of this piece.

## Found in the mine, not in this plan

- **He is the father.** Three women call him Father, papa or padre. One says it *as in priest* and sends him confessions. He answered one of them *father is father regardless*, which is now the claim. Another sent him a message calling him a selfish liar, a fraud psychiatrist and a bad father; he pasted it to a friend and typed *iam laughing*, then *am crying*, then *dude*.
- **The duel.** 30 July, to a stranger who had said she freezes: *Father and son, the son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.* And before it, *Even when the son is better, he can't win.* Unprompted, about swordsmen, to a woman he does not know.
- **The conversion.** *My father didn't really care, my mother was worried.*
- **Khenchela.** He can buy anything in that town and nobody will sell to him, because they know his father. The inverse of the supermarket line that went to *The Corner*.
- **His own funeral.** 17 Aug, joking about her meeting the family: *take good care of my father*, then *I am just dying*, then *Not a single one would give a fuck about me.* This is what the grandfather's funeral pays off against, and it is the last move before the claim returns.
- **Twice, two weeks apart, to two different women:** *she only cooks for my father.*

## Where the floor goes

*I don't think my father cried when his dad died, I remember that day pretty well all of it.*

He remembers every detail of the one day his father did not weep, and says so in passing while talking about whether men cry.

## The shape

The hammer goes early, reported flat. Then the water, the clinic, the taxes, the quiet pacing, and it lands on the funeral.

## Moved out, 21 Aug

**The supermarket went to The Corner.** I had read it as the father not being recognised as a father. The transcript says otherwise: Athina was saying she goes out so rarely that people would think her new to her own street, and he answered with the shopkeeper never having seen him. It is evidence about the son being unseen, not about the father, and it belongs where it now is.


## What the expansion pass added, 21 Aug

Draft 4 was 94 fragments and 1,295 words. Draft 8 is 120 and 1,748. Nothing was invented and no fragment got longer for its own sake. Every addition was already typed, sitting next to a line that had been extracted as a summary of it.

**The hammer night had a second half.** Fifteen minutes after telling her about the hammer, he told her that he and his father had bought a car, that he is sitting comfortable, and that he spends how he likes. Her side of that night: a boy she does not know messaged her, her father saw it, refused to hear her, hit her, dragged her in the alley. She gathered her things and went to her grandmother's and her mother came to get her back. She is in her BAC year. He told her he is in his sixth and that one more makes him a specialist in France.

**The mascara scene had a killer adjacent fact.** Straight after *good dad good dad*, she typed that her father had also told her she put too much blush on that day. Then he told her people of her calibre are rare, that she had done nothing wrong, that she could have done something and been justified, and that the Logos of the world was never a violent one and he is writing something about that. Collapsed into one block.

**The floor's damning half was seven minutes away.** After *I don't think my father cried when his dad died* and her reply that crying does not make a man weak, he typed: *I still am unable to talk about how I feel.*

**The duels are the same woman, three days later.** 27 July she gets the funeral. 30 July she gets *father and son, the son learnt to always be afraid of his father and thus loses the duels.* Moved to sit after the floor for that reason. She then says she tells herself, who are you to be afraid, and he answers FUCK IT WE BALL.

**The priesthood.** In the same conversation as his own funeral, sixteen minutes after *not a single one would give a fuck about me*, she asked about the name on the site and he said there is no wife of his yet, and that it depends on whether he chooses the priesthood. That is now the last move before the claim returns.

**Three collapse blocks:** the 1 a.m. consultations (84 words), the mascara aftermath (97), the confessions arriving (110).

**Cut as invented:** *I asked her what she was going to wear*, *she was seventeen when she stopped talking to hers*, *not her too*. None of them were in the record. The real next line was *did he call u* and it is now the last thing he does.
