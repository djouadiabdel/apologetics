## Evidence

Everything since:
- The 0-99 peg roster, the 52-card people, the psalm memorisation calendar, Hugh of St Victor, ruminatio.
- The camera as the exam. One take, cold, pass or fail.
- **15 Aug, to Khadija:** *i mean i never watch my videos back, i just use it as a tool to really get to experience the knowledge deeply.*

## Where the floor goes

He qualified once, at fourteen, and did not go. He has spent the eleven years since building a private memory system with an examination he administers to himself, alone, in a room, with nobody watching, and calls it the test.
