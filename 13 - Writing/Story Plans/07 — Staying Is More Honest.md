**The charge.** Staying is more honest than leaving, because only the one who stays can keep looking.

## The flaw, which is the story

She meant honest as *your action matching your inside*. He answered as *access to evidence*. He switched the sense of the word and won an argument she was not making, and conceded the real objection in passing (*even when looking at it for eternity creates biases*) without stopping.
