# The Corner

> **Titled *The Man of God*, 21 Aug.** After Alexis of Rome, who came home and lived seventeen years under the staircase of his father's house unrecognised, fed scraps by the household, identified only after his death by a letter still in his hand. The title is what they called him without knowing who he was. Fragment 65 is *this is your son? He said he had never seen me.*

*He wants this one. Draft 14 in `Drafts/The Corner/`.*

**The charge.** *I like being alone.*

**Whose words this is built from.** Mine, and camera only. No argument in it anywhere.

**The form.** Position. The same body in the same place across twenty years and four rooms, reported flat, never explained.

## Where the floor goes

*trying to act half dead.* He supplies the reason for the corner in five words and never returns to it.

## Why this one is ready

Every line is his, every line is camera, and nobody has to be quoted for it to work. It is the only story on the slate that owes nothing to anyone else's sentences.

# The spine

Claim stated flat, seven rooms in order, trapdoor at five, then two more rooms after it. Restate the claim.

**Open.** I like being alone.

**1. The bus.** *(16 Aug)* Standing in the corner of it. Cannot remember the last time before this one. Acting half dead.

**2. The room, as a kid.** *(20 Aug)* Walking the length of it. Has done this since he was small and never gets tired of it. Keeping the steps quiet so his father doesn't come.
> *insistence:* I have always liked the quiet.

**3. Primary school.** *(19 Aug)* Remembers nothing about being there. Remembers being abandoned there.

**4. Home alone, June.** *(11 Jun)* The only one in the house. The book is the thing he talks to.
> *insistence:* Nobody asked me to sit there.

**5. His room at night.** *(The Log, 14 Aug)* The corner again. The dormition of the Theotokos in front of him. The AC playing its music. Thirty six hours without sleep.

**TRAPDOOR.** *trying to act half dead.* Five words, said in passing to a woman he barely knows, about a bus. He does not come back to it and neither does the piece.

**6. Seraidi.** *(18 Aug)* A hotel, a full day, music playing. He walks away from the music on purpose, sits down far from it, and looks at the view.
> *insistence:* The view was better from where I was.

**7. The kitchen.** *(The Log, 13 Aug)* Brother not home. A voice, and he turns, and there is nobody. The kitchen empty. The window open.

**Close.** I like being alone.

## What each room needs

One other person in it or one clear absence, and one thing that changes between entering and leaving. Rooms 3 and 7 already have both. Room 6 has the music leaving.

## What is not allowed in it

No explaining why the corner. The five words at the trapdoor are the only reason the piece ever gives, and they arrive as a throwaway in the middle of a sentence about public transport.
