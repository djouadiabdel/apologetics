# Her Sentence In My Mouth

**The charge.** *I do not talk about her any more.* Kept as written. There is no verbatim version in the record, and there does not need to be: it is the thing he would defend, and the record is the refutation. The sharper version the mine produced is that he tells everyone except her. His own line, 14 Aug: *i didnt tell aya all those things.*

**Drafted.** `Drafts/Her Sentence In My Mouth/Draft 04.md`, titled ***The Evangelist***. The position, not the subject: one who carries an account of a person to people who never met her, repeats it verbatim to different audiences, and never delivers it to the subject.

**Whose words this is built from.** One sentence of Aya's, and three months of me saying it.

## Verification pass, 21 Aug. Three of six evidence lines were wrong.

- **The six-years line is a different woman.** *I don't remember what she wanted last time we talked 6 years* (10 Aug) sits inside a roster conversation where he is listing girls with dates. Forty-eight minutes later he gets to *then the last girl is [her] who's been a hmmm*. Six years back does not reach her. Cut from the piece.
- **The *never wrote it* line is about the novella, not about her.** *i dont know what she wanted. i never wrote it because i have never seen it* (14 Aug) is him answering a question about a character in his own book. Kept, but placed as what it is. The admission arrives as literary criticism of his own work.
- **The *no wonder you left me* line is about a different woman and it is a joke.** He had just asked his friend to change his profile picture to her photograph, typed *first time laughing normally in a while*, then apologised to the picture, then typed a twelve-letter laugh. Kept, marked as another woman.

## What the mine added

- **He says her sentence three times, not twice, plus once as a joke.** 11 Jul he attributes it, then retypes it clean sixty seconds later. 18 Jul it becomes *the bitch will say huh you got everything from me and i benefited from nothing*, with xDDDD in front. 2 Aug he types it cold to someone he has known three weeks.
- **On 2 Aug he is not quoting from memory.** One line after typing it he wrote *i am reading what she said. like said.* He has the message open.
- **There is a second sentence of hers, with a day attached.** *Maybe in another life*, a Sunday. The benefit sentence was a Monday. He and his friend were trading days of the week.
- **The floor is better than the plan.** He typed her sentence, got xDDDDDDDDDDD, and typed it again one minute later with the first half restored. The reply was a reel of a woman arriving home in Sacramento.
- **The record moves in his mouth.** 9 Jul: *I remember forcing Aya to find a girl so they go out and idk.. do stuff.* 14 Aug: *she wanted to find a girl for us*, and *she was literally looking for a girl for me too.* The two versions now straddle the trapdoor and the piece never points at the change.
- **He said the end had to be made acceptable.** 1 Aug: *Aya had to be a cruel bitcha for me to even accept the end*, then *irl irl irl*, then *who said I will move on.*
- **Five days after the double-saying** he told a girl he was arguing with that he is having the best time of his life since the breakup.
- **The 2023 files are in the piece.** `Aya.md` is a dated ledger in which every entry is something she said about him. `Asks for Aya.md` is titled asks and contains three things he wants to be shown and no request. Both quoted directly for the first time.

## Overlap avoided

*Useless* is already published and inverts the fact: it has him calling her useless, the record has her calling him useless. *A Supply* already holds the archive-is-inadmissible territory (*two thousand messages and not one of them admissible*). This piece stays on transmission rather than on the archive, which is what keeps it distinct.
