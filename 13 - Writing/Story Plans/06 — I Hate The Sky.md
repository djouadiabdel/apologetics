That is worse than the plan assumed and it is the trapdoor.

**The charge.** Four words, 12 Aug, to Khadija, dropped in the middle of something else and never returned to:

> Bcz I hate the sky kinda idk

## Why this is the big one

- *I was her sky. I did not know yet that a sky is also a ceiling.* — from *Useless*, his best line.
- The site is called **The Hollow Sky**.
- Khadija, 20 Aug: *Vacuum. No matter how much we suck it was never ours to last. Hollow no matter what.* She arrived at the word on her own, having never seen the title.
- Me, 16 Aug: *He just came down here to try to save me whom i am the worst.*
- Me, 10 Aug: *I don't deserve the lamb's perfect blood.*
- Me, 16 Jul: *I am the worst of the worst sinners.*
- Ghad, 17 Aug: *I'm talking to god not u.*

## Is it really not ready?

The material is four words and everything else is circling. That is the argument for waiting.

The argument against waiting: the rule that says don't name the feeling assumes the prose already delivered it. He has never said this one out loud, not once, in a year of writing around it. On material he has not said, silence is not restraint, it is avoidance.

**So the condition is not time. It is that he says it plainly once, in the ugly draft, and lets the piece be wrong about it.** He may name it. He may not be right about it.
