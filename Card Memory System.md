# 🃏 Card Memory System

> [!abstract] The design, in one line
> A **single card = a person.** A **pair of cards = a PERSON standing in a SETTING.**
> Person comes from the **numbers (ranks)**. Setting comes from the **suits (shapes)**.
> The people are done. The suits are the new muscle — **drill them until they hurt.**

---

## 0. Burn-in layer — THE SUITS (do this first, every day)

The number-person is already easy. The **suits** carry the whole new load, so make them reflex.

### First suit = the WORLD (the *where* / the stage) — the ONLY thing you must memorize

| Suit | World | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| ♠ Spades | **Black Clover** | spade = a black clover leaf, grimoires & dark magic |
| ♥ Hearts | **Church / Christian** | heart = love = the church, cross on the altar |
| ♦ Diamonds | **Adventure Time** (Ooo, Tree Trunks' land) | diamond = the candy-gem kingdom |
| ♣ Clubs | **Dark and Darker** (the ruins/dungeon map) | club = wooden weapon, torchlit dungeon full of loot |

### Second suit = the PROP (the *what* in the scene) — basically FREE

> The prop is just the **pip symbol itself**, held up as a 3-D object. You already know these shapes — no memorizing.

| Suit | Prop |
|---|---|
| ♠ | the **spade shape**, a little **demon** lurking under it |
| ♥ | the **cross** |
| ♦ | the **diamond ring** |
| ♣ | the **club / cudgel** |

### The 16 settings at a glance (World × Prop) — this is your drill grid

Read **left-to-right**: first card builds the room, second card is the thing in it.

| 1st ↓ \ 2nd → | ♠ demon-spade | ♥ cross | ♦ ring | ♣ club |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **♠ Black Clover** | grimoire hall + demon-spade | + a cross | + a ring | + a club |
| **♥ Church** | church + demon-spade | + a cross | + a ring | + a club |
| **♦ Adventure Time** | Ooo + demon-spade | + a cross | + a ring | + a club |
| **♣ Dark & Darker** | dungeon + demon-spade | + a cross | + a ring | + a club |

> [!tip] Drill target
> Flash any two suits → the *room + object* fires in under a second, no thinking. Only 4 worlds to burn in; the props are the pips.

## 1. The pair formula

**Pair = PERSON (from the two ranks) + SETTING (from the two suits).**

1. **Person** ← read the **two ranks** as a 2-digit number → your 0–99 peg (§4).
2. **World** ← **first** suit (the table above).
3. **Prop** ← **second** suit (the pip).
4. Fuse into **one image** at a locus.

> [!example] Watch it turn — a pair and its shadow
> **4♣ 7♦** → ranks 4,7 = **47 Rika** · suits ♣→♦ = *Dark & Darker dungeon* + *diamond ring*
> → **Rika in the ruins, clutching a diamond ring.**
>
> **7♦ 4♣** (the shadow) → ranks 7,4 = **74 Kira** · suits ♦→♣ = *Adventure Time* + *club*
> → **Kira in Ooo, swinging a club.**
>
> Same two cards reversed → a totally different scene. **No blur, ever.**

> [!note] The Reversibility Law
> The image must let you recover **both exact cards**. Person = the ordered ranks. World = 1st suit. Prop = 2nd suit. Every bit is in the picture.

> [!warning] One thing to keep straight
> A **single card** reads as *suit = tens, rank = ones* (§4b). A **pair** reads the *two ranks together* as the number and sends the *suits* to the setting. Same roster of people, addressed two ways depending on whether you're doing one card or a pair.

## 2. Courts break the number machine → 3 buckets

Courts (J/Q/K) aren't digits, so they don't feed the 2-digit number. Three clean cases:

1. **Number × Number** → the fused **person-in-a-setting** image (§1). *The main case.*
2. **Court × Court** → just **two of your 12 court-characters in one scene** (their suit is baked into the character — no setting needed).
   - *e.g. K♥ + J♣ = Jesus and the Joker together.*
3. **Number × Court** → the **court-character** + the **number card's own single-card person** (§4b), linked in a scene. **Both faces already exist → zero new memorizing.**
   - *e.g. K♠ + 5♦ = Dante headlocking "35 Meliodas."* (5♦ single-card = 35)

---

## 3. Runtime mindset (why this feels slow now, and won't later)

- **You do NOT compose at speed.** At runtime you *recognize* one pre-fused image, instantly — same as you don't "compute" a single card, you just see the person.
- The suit rules are a **learning scaffold + repair kit**, not a live calculator. When you blank, the rule *regenerates* the image (self-healing).
- **Drilling fuses** person+world+prop into one chunk. First few reps of a pair are slow; then it's one picture.
- **You can start running decks now** — compose-on-the-fly as the slow fallback, and reps bake the common pairs into instant images.
- This is the pros' method: **Ben System** (2704 images), **PAO**. Your system is a legit variant.

## 6. Drilling plan

1. **Suits first (§0).** Flash two suits → *room + prop* fires instantly. Only 4 worlds to burn in.
2. **Single cards (§4b).** Flip one card → say the person aloud. Aim for *instant*, not fast. Cycle the ~6 you stumble on.
3. **Pairs.** Draw two cards → build the fused *person-in-setting* image (§1). Slow at first; reps fuse it.
4. **Run decks.** 2 cards per locus → 26 loci. Compose-on-the-fly is the fallback; speed comes from reps.

## 7. Retired ideas (do not resurrect)
- **Person–Object (P-O):** pair = 1st person + 2nd object. Dropped — the *setting* design (§0–1) replaced it.
- **CVCV word system** ([[2704 Two-Card System]]): each card = a syllable, pair = a word (tapi/pita). Dropped — required composing on the fly, too slow. The word note is **superseded** by this one.
