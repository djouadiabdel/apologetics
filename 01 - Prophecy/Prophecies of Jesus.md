### **Lineage and Origin**

- **Genesis 3:15:** Born as the "seed of a woman" (a human male) who will crush the serpent's head.
    
- **Genesis 21:12 & 22:15-18:** A descendant of Abraham and Isaac through whom all nations will be blessed.
    
- **Genesis 49:10:** From the tribe of Judah; the "scepter" (royalty/ruling authority) will not depart from Judah until He comes.
    
- **Numbers 24:17:** A "star out of Jacob" and a "scepter out of Israel."
    
- **1 Chronicles 5:2:** Affirms the Messiah comes from the tribe of Judah.
    
- **Isaiah 11:1:** A rod and a branch growing from the stem of Jesse (David's father).
    
- **2 Samuel 7:16:** A descendant of King David whose throne will be established forever.
    
- **Jeremiah 23:5-6:** A righteous Branch raised to David, a reigning King called "The Lord our righteousness."
    
- **Ezekiel 34:23-24:** A shepherd over the people, referred to as "my servant David."
    
- **Ruth 4:12-17:** A direct descendant of Boaz and Ruth.
    
- **Micah 5:2:** Born in the specific town of Bethlehem Ephrathah, yet His origins are "from everlasting."
    
- **Isaiah 7:14:** Born of a virgin and called Immanuel ("God with us").
    

### **Nature and Identity**

- **Isaiah 9:6-7:** A child born and son given who is called "Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace."
    
- **Proverbs 30:4:** Identified specifically as the Son of God.
    
- **Psalm 2:7:** God declares, "You are my son, today I have become your father."
    
- **Deuteronomy 18:15-19:** A prophet raised up like Moses whom the people must listen to.
    
- **Psalm 110:1:** The Lord seated at the right hand of God.
    
- **Zechariah 6:12-13:** Will hold the dual roles of both Priest and King.
    

### **Ministry and Actions**

- **Malachi 3:1 & Isaiah 40:3:** Preceded by a messenger/forerunner (a "voice calling in the wilderness") preparing His way.
    
- **Isaiah 35:5-6 & Isaiah 42:6-7:** Will perform specific miracles: opening the eyes of the blind, unstopping the ears of the deaf, healing the lame, and freeing prisoners from darkness.
    
- **Zechariah 9:9:** Will make a kingly, humble entrance into Jerusalem riding on a donkey (a colt).
    
- **Haggai 2:6-9 & Malachi 3:1:** Will visit and minister in the Second Temple (meaning He had to appear before its destruction in 70 AD).
    
- **Daniel 9:26:** Gives a specific mathematical timeline for when the Messiah will appear and be "cut off" (put to death).
    
- **Joel 2:28:** Will baptize people with the Holy Spirit.
    
- **Isaiah 49:6:** Will be a light not just to Israel, but to the Gentiles, bringing salvation to the ends of the earth.
    
- **Psalm 2 & Genesis 49:10:** Will ultimately have worldwide dominion and the obedience of the nations.
    

### **Betrayal, Rejection, and Suffering**

- **Psalm 118:22:** The "stone the builders rejected" who becomes the Chief Cornerstone.
    
- **Isaiah 53:2:** Rejected by His own people; having no "beauty that we should desire him."
    
- **Psalm 69:4:** Hated by the people without a cause.
    
- **Zechariah 11:12-13:** Betrayed for an exact price of 30 pieces of silver, which is later thrown into the house of the Lord and used to buy a potter's field.
    
- **Isaiah 50:6 & Isaiah 53:10:** Whipped, beaten, crushed, and made an offering for sin.
    
- **Psalm 22:** Details the specific manner of His execution: mocked by crowds, bones out of joint, dehydrated, and hands and feet pierced.
    
- **Zechariah 12:10:** The people will "look on me whom they pierced" and mourn for Him.
    
- **Amos 8:9:** The sun will go down at noon and darken the earth in broad daylight during His death.
    

### **Resurrection**

- **Psalm 16:8-10:** God will not abandon His soul to the realm of the dead.
    
- **Isaiah 53:10:** After being an offering for sin, He will "prolong his days" and see His offspring (resurrection).

### **Typological Pictures of the Messiah**

_(The transcripts note that beyond direct verse prophecies, the Old Testament uses historical events and people as "pictures" foreshadowing Jesus)_

- **Exodus 12:** The Passover Lamb (sacrificed to save the people from death).
    
- **Exodus 16:** The Manna from Heaven (the bread of life).
    
- **Leviticus 16:** The High Priest who enters the holy place, but offering His own blood rather than goats/calves.
    
- **Numbers 20 & 2 Samuel 23:2-3:** The spiritual Rock that brings forth living water.
    
- **Numbers 21:8-9:** The Bronze Serpent mounted on a pole (looking to the one "lifted up" brings life).
    
- **Joshua 5:13-15:** The Commander of the Lord's Army.
    
- **Judges:** The ultimate Judge and Lawgiver.
    
- **1 & 2 Kings:** Foreshadowed by Elisha multiplying bread to feed a multitude.
    
- **Ezra & Nehemiah:** Typified by Zerubbabel (rebuilding the temple) and Nehemiah (rebuilding the walls of salvation).
    
- **Esther:** Typified by Mordecai's intercession.
    
- **Job:** Typified in innocent suffering followed by great blessing.
    
- **Song of Solomon & Hosea:** The faithful Bridegroom pursuing His bride.
    
- **Jonah:** Typified by spending three days and three nights in the belly of the fish (the heart of the earth).
    
- **Nahum:** The Avenger of God's people.
    
- **Zephaniah:** The Restorer of the remnant.
