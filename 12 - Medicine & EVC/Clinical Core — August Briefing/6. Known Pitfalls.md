Things to refuse in August even if they sound tempting:

- **Don't submit to clinical tracks.** Humanities/épistémologie only. Pure clinical tracks will desk-reject philosophy-flavored work before review.
- **Don't write loose analogical Girard.** Every conceptual move must name a mechanism. "Le bouc émissaire ressemble au patient désigné" is not an argument — "le mécanisme de désignation par convergence mimétique recouvre la fonction homéostatique du patient désigné en thérapie familiale systémique" is.
- **Don't skip the méthode section.** Even for a conceptual paper. Especially for a conceptual paper.
- **Don't use English-flavored French academic register.** Read 5-10 model abstracts before drafting. The register is heavier, more nominal, more impersonal than English.
- **Don't try to ship three abstracts at once.** Pick one Core. Polish it. Ship it. The second abstract is for the next cycle.
- **Don't assume I need a clinical vignette from my own rotations.** Literature-based cases (drawn from published French psychiatric case reports) and purely conceptual papers are both valid in humanities tracks. My psychiatry rotation was unremarkable and that's fine.
- **Don't let me skip the reviewer-quiz step** (see Section 8).
