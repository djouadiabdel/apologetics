# Congresses are out. Journals are the route.

**Why the congress branch is dead, at least until I am in France with someone paying:**

Verified against the official Encéphale 2027 call:

- **Presenter registration is mandatory** and must be completed by 9 December 2026. "L'inscription au congrès du présentateur est obligatoire."
- **"Les frais d'inscription, de transport et d'hébergement sont à la charge des auteurs."** Registration, travel and accommodation are on me.
- **There is no humanities track in 2027.** The thirteen categories are épidémiologie, bipolaire et dépression, anxiété, troubles du comportement, personnalité, schizophrénie, addictions, pédopsychiatrie, neurodéveloppement, sujet âgé, urgences, innovation thérapeutique, médico-légal. No épistémologie, no histoire, no psychopathologie. My own pitfalls file forbids submitting conceptual work to a clinical track.
- Deadline was 7 October 2026, not mid-September. Irrelevant now.

The old version of this file assumed a free e-poster with no travel. That was wrong. **Every congress requires the presenter in the room**, so the whole branch is out of reach from Algeria and stays out until the FFI years.

Nothing of value is lost. A journal article beats a poster on every axis I care about: no fee, no flight, longer form so the argument survives intact, permanent indexed reference instead of a line in a supplement, and no January in Paris.

# Primary target: L'Évolution Psychiatrique

- Publisher Elsevier, ISSN 0014-3855, in French, 4 issues a year.
- **No publication fee for standard articles.** The $2,720 APC applies only if I opt into open access, which I will not.
- Submission via **editorialmanager.com/evopsy**. Word files, no PDF.
- Instructions: `levolutionpsychiatrique.fr/la-revue/soumettre-un-article/`
- **This is also where the épistémologie seminars happen.** Falissard gave one there. It is the room I actually want to be in, not the poster hall.
- Highest prestige of the three, so this is the aspiration, not necessarily the first submission.

# Second: Perspectives Psy

- 4 issues a year, psychodynamic and humanities-friendly.
- Submission through the **MMS electronic system** at `perspectives-psy.org`, tab "soumission électronique (MMS)". First submission requires a profile with identity, address, profession, expertise.
- Anonymous reading committee, **opinion returned within two months.** That turnaround is the fastest of the three and makes it the best place to take a first shot and learn the register.
- Requires cover letter, structured text with abstract, APA bibliography.

# Third: PSN (Psychiatrie, Sciences humaines, Neurosciences)

- Best conceptual fit for Girard-flavoured work.
- **Careful: it moved publishers.** Springer archived it and no longer takes submissions. Since 2012 it is published by **Éditions Matériologiques**. Submissions go through them, contact `arnaud.plagnol@iedparis8.net`.
- Smaller and harder to navigate than the other two, so confirm it is still active before spending a Core on it.
