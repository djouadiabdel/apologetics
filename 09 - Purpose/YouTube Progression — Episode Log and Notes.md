## Episode 2 — James 2:1-13, and the 25-minute problem

You shot 25 minutes trying to say everything, and it did not feel organized enough to cut to 10. That is not a discipline failure. It is a **thesis-count failure**: the reading plan for this passage contains at least three separate video-sized theses, and you tried to deliver all three in one sitting. Of course it ran long and felt loose. It is three videos wearing one recording.

The good news is the reading plan already sorts them for you. The passage has two halves (2:1-7 and 2:8-13) and the material clusters into three clean ideas. Proposed split:

**Part A — The man with no face (2:1-7).** The scene. The ring is a sign, not a thing (Hilary of Arles). *Prosōpolēmpsia* = receiving the mask, the gold ring as a Roman persona you wear on your hand (Zizioulas). The assembly builds a courtroom and seats people by rank. Close on 6d: the sin of face-receiving produces a man with no face. This is the Zizioulas vein, the deepest one, and it stands alone.

**Part B — Why God is not fair (2:5).** The Chesterton swerve. Leviticus 19:15 says do not favor the poor *either*, and then 2:5 says God chose the poor. That is a counter-partiality, and it is the honest objection your viewer already has. Resolve it with the choosing that constitutes rather than detects (Augustine at 2:5, Zizioulas on love originating value). One idea, one paradox, one release.

**Part C — Guilty of all, and mercy that boasts (2:8-13).** The law is one because the neighbor is one. Adultery and murder as the two archetypal mimetic crimes (Hilary again). *Katakauchatai*: mercy does not outweigh judgment, it wins on judgment's ground and taunts (Chesterton on dividing the crime from the criminal). End on the open loop into Episode 3: if the law is a relation and not a ledger, then the "works" of 2:14-26 were never ledger entries.

Three videos, each with one thesis, each 8 to 10 minutes, and Part C hands the baton to the faith-and-works episode for free. The 25 minutes you already shot is most of the raw footage for all three. This is a cutting job, not a re-record.

**The lesson to carry forward, in one line:** one video, one thesis. If the reading plan has three findings you love, that is three videos, not one long one. The length problem is almost always a count problem.
