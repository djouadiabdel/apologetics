CAUSE:
  Human desire is never self-generated — it is always borrowed from a model (mediator).

MECHANISM:
  When the mediator is distant, the borrowing is harmless and even heroic.
  When the mediator is close (same social world), the borrowing becomes rivalry.
  The rivalry escalates because both parties begin imitating each other simultaneously.
  The escalation destroys the object — the rivals forget what they originally wanted.
  At the extreme, the rivalry becomes apocalyptic — pure mutual destruction.

CONSEQUENCE:
  The only escape is "novelistic conversion" — seeing that your desire was never yours.
  This conversion is structurally identical to religious conversion.
  The great novelists (Cervantes, Stendhal, Flaubert, Proust, Dostoyevsky) each
  underwent this conversion and wrote the record of it.
