"Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!" the boys chant behind him.

He doesn't even look at Itagaki. He keeps looking past him, behind him, at the boys who have gone quiet now, at the gym that has stopped making noise. Itagaki's hand is full of fabric and his arm is shaking and the body behind that shirt will not move. Not a centimeter. Not a fraction. It is like pulling at a boulder. Not just a boulder — a boulder guided by a mountain behind it, rooted into something Itagaki cannot reach.

"You are like a dog," Imai says. He still hasn't moved an inch. "Bark and no bite."

The gym is silent. Takamura's grin has widened but his mouth is closed. Aoki's hands are frozen mid-chant. Kimura's arms are crossed and his jaw is tight. Ippo is between them now, worried, pushing them apart with both hands, telling them that he'll do it instead, he'll take the spar, there's no need for this.

Itagaki closes his eyes.

His hand releases the fabric. He steps aside. "As I said," he mutters, but the sentence has no weight. It falls out of his mouth like something he rehearsed for a moment that already passed.

He stands there. Imai isn't worried. Imai doesn't think Itagaki can stop him. The acknowledgment Itagaki wanted — even as an enemy, even as someone worth fighting — isn't there. His hands unclench. The anger doesn't leave fast — it just goes cold, sitting in his chest like something heavy that stopped burning.

Ippo steps in. The spar is agreed to. Preparations begin. Itagaki is left standing there, his hand still feeling the fabric of a shirt that didn't move. His fingers close around nothing.

Ippo is getting his gloves on. Coach walks over and leans close — his mouth near Ippo's ear, his hand on the kid's shoulder. He whispers something. Itagaki can't hear it. Nobody can hear it.

But he can see the change.

Ippo's eyes lose the lamb. Those are not the eyes of someone shy, someone respectful, someone who wants peace. Those are the eyes of someone excited. Someone ready. Itagaki has seen that look before — right before the first bell, right before the body stops thinking and starts hunting.

Ippo is looking at him.

No. Not at him. Something behind him.

Who might that be?
