# Chapter 45 

---

I am on this desk swallowed up by an emotion I thought was gone. The shower did not help. I thought it would make me feel fresh after all that running but the water just moved over me and left, and the feeling stayed.

It is on me tight like a rope. I look at all sides of my surrounding for a comforter but there is none. For company but there is none. Only that memory. The whisper of that disturbing sound.

I did hope this emotion was gone a long time ago. How it captures every part of me and takes away my sleep. The way it slips through me into the hold of each corner. It raises up inside my entirety slowly until it becomes one with me.

I get a book to read. I cannot focus at all on the words in front of me. I re-read each sentence over and over again yet there is no meaning in what I am reading. I change the language. Same outcome. Only now I feel captured by the emotion just like how I was once captured by the beauty of football.

---

Those thoughts I try to keep away — they were like strangers to me. Far away from the hold of my hands. Unfamiliar I am with their sight.

But the colors seep through. An orange, a red, velvety, mixing together. They make a ball. It is beautiful. It is flying and rotating in its spot and I cannot move even if I tried.

It pulls me toward it. My legs want to stand up and be there for it. Just like how you think someone is a stranger and then you are surprised to know that you of all people looked up to that stranger for warmth.

I try to stare away from it. The warmth that is being sent down at me is too strong to not feel. It captures all of my skin. I can feel it moving on top of me — not like ants, no feet — like water. Wrapping me all over with its warmth. Now they are truly a part of me.

If I took a chance, maybe I can exchange a glance from it. The way I always felt when I entered a stadium in my professional life.

But no. No more. I am already retired. It has been five years since I last played my professional European game. I called it all done at the top.

Maybe it was a mistake. The feeling is telling me I can probably find it out and move forward with it. But I cannot let myself fall for the trap. The same trap she told me I would fall into.

---

This feeling is making it impossible to sleep.

The more I think of it, the more I observe its trajectory, a black haired kid just shows up in my mind. The same kid I saw today boxing. I do not know who he is other than that he is a boxer with a father. But by the look of it he seems professional. I can try to search for something like that online.

I can as a professional athlete know when a fellow member is one too. You look at another and feel like you are looking at a mirror. The mirror shows a different version than yourself. You lift your right hand up and the mirror lifts its left. But you know that you are looking at someone who is you. Or someone whose soul looks like yours.

Or maybe I should go for another run. Although this means I would mess up my sleep, my calorie intake, the whole cycle of my habit.

Now all of a sudden my hand is shaking. Trembling. Wait — am I sure there is no vibrator in my hand? Why would there be. But the way it is trembling like that out of nowhere makes me remember every time I would have a big game. It would turn out to be this way. The hand going first. The hand always knew before I did.

Not like I have a match tomorrow or something.

---

It is just obvious that I am caved in this place, surrounded by all this darkness, all because of one whisper that keeps trying to mutter a voice in my ears. The more I think of that boy, the warmer the ball grows above me.

I never looked at boxers and wanted to fight them. I am not stupid. But that kid at the sight of my window did whatever he wanted in that gym. He always looked like he is in another world. He may not even have noticed my existence as I looked at him. The whisper of the music coming on and off.

Just stay where you are, little ball. I do not need you to walk me through it all over again. I do not need you to tell me what to do. You have done well in all of my life and pushed me forward. But I guess I have no choice but to try to find the boy.

Or not?

---

It is ok. I am going to keep reading my book even if I cannot get its words. My mind seems to be busy with something else.

The smooth feeling of the book. I personally had asked for it to be made in this format, so that every time I touch it a memory so dear to me comes back to calm me down. I have read this book thousands of times. Every time I touch it my fingertips want to lie down across the cover. Then slowly move over every piece of paper.

The smell can never grow old on me. Paper catching itself, running down onto me with its earthy scent. Even if I try to control myself from falling into the smell I cannot. I have to give in. It directly reminds me of that time I first read this exact book.

It is weightless now. A bit cracky from how old it is. But I am reminded of that spark I always had before big games. The one that made me want to win yet relaxed me enough to focus on the now.

I am searching for what I am now, after I have experienced what I did for the first time in five years. I will find you. If it is urgent or if I am hurting more than I am now.

This feeling — I had seen my teammates feel it over and over again. Looked at myself in the mirror with this exact same heart beating more than once too. I will come back here some day and laugh about it. Memories never fade.

_How it felt like I was burning while you were not._

No. Let me not think of that night. It was a self-conscious remark and I felt lousy after saying it. But it is peaceful in a way to let it go like that.

My heart is beating faster as I think of it. I do not know what to do and how to act. Man of the world, why are you feeling like your heart is going to jump out of its place. You do not know the world. You are feeling it, yet you do not know it. Why does it have to be in this exact time. After that event.

The cold air from the spot I live in, the place I call my home, is the exact opposite feeling of what I touched in that gym. The gym that I certainly do not belong to.

This is why I need to make this feel like home. This country should feel like home.

And I found what would help me make it feel so.

I have to search for your name, black haired kid.
