**3-4 chapters later — Third seed**

This is where he makes noise for the first time without meaning to. A press moment, a weigh-in, a post-fight interview — wherever journalists gather in your story. The reporter asks a question or makes a comment that doesn't fit the room. Everyone else is asking about strategy, about the knockout, about the next opponent. He asks something that comes from a completely different angle — something about the fighter's relationship to pain, or why the crowd cheers louder when there's blood. It's not rude, it's just alien. The room reacts. Maybe someone laughs. Maybe someone gets annoyed. But one character — ideally Ippo — hears it and doesn't laugh. It sticks with him for a panel or two. Then the scene moves on.
