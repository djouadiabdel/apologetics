There's a fire in me  
and I didn't light it.

it came in through her.  
came in the way the sea comes in  
through one hole in the boat 
quiet, then all at once,  
then there is no boat,  
you are the water.

for years I wrote the wound down  
so somebody would lean over the page  
and tell me  
there was something good in there.

that was a mirror.  
you look in,  
it hands you your own face,  
you go under.

this is not that.

the fire doesn't say good.  
the fire doesn't say anything.  
it takes whatever in me  
can't hold the light  
and burns it off,  
and I yell at 4am  
with nobody up to hear it,  
and the hand doesn't move.  
it stays on me.  
it knows when I'm done.

you want to know how it knows.

it leans over the metal  
and waits  
until it can see its own face  
in the shine.

so this is everything I've got:  
the nights I can't breathe,  
the name I won't put down,  
the same cut, page after page.

it isn't a wound held up  
for a verdict.  
it's me in the furnace.

and if you ever read this 
look close.

the face coming back  
isn't mine anymore.

I'm all here.  
I'm suffering.  
and something is making me  
into a thing that can carry  
a face  
that was never mine.
