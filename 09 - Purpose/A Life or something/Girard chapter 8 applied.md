# Girard chapter 8, applied to me and Ari

Written 3 August 2026, before talking to her about it.
Source is Mensonge romantique et vérité romanesque, ch. VIII, Masochisme et sadisme.
Fiction version is [[The Eighth Chapter]] in 13 - Writing.

---

## What the chapter actually claims

Three moves, in order.

**Nobody wants humiliation.** Girard is explicit and he is annoyed at the psychologists about it. *Personne n'a jamais désiré de telles choses.* What the person wants is the divinity of the mediator. Shame and lowering are accepted, and sometimes hunted, because they are evidence that there is a height. The pain is never the point. The height is the point.

**The obstacle starts generating the desire instead of the other way round.** In ordinary desire, imitation produces the obstacle. Later it reverses. *C'est maintenant l'obstacle qui engendre l'imitation.* You begin choosing people by the resistance they offer rather than by anything you admire in them.

**The diagnostic.** *Nous sommes masochistes lorsque nous choisissons le médiateur en vertu non plus de l'admiration qu'il nous inspire mais du dégoût que nous lui inspirons.* And the behavioural marker that goes with it, *il se détournera des êtres qui éprouvent pour lui affection et tendresse.* He turns away from anyone warm to him and goes toward whoever shows contempt, because contempt proves they are not one of the damned like him.

And the caveat that matters most. *Nul ne peut dire où commence le masochisme repoussant, où s'arrête le noble goût du risque.* There is no clean border between healthy and pathological. So the question is never a diagnosis. It is a direction.

## Where it does fit us

The submissive register arrived close to a refusal. She said no to a relationship on 1 August and said she needs to heal first. Whatever the good boy register is, it got louder on the other side of an obstacle, and Girard's whole claim is that an obstacle is the strongest generator there is. That is not proof of anything but it is the exact configuration the chapter describes and I should not pretend I cannot see it.

Good boy is a verdict handed down from a superior position. My own writing already says regard that has to be won makes the wanting of it a weakness, and that the triangle eats itself. So I am receiving certification and enjoying it, and certification is the thing I have said for months I keep mistaking for love.

I answered a question about rivalry by producing an inventory of what I have received. Kisses, moans, praise, permission, voice, names. That is a ledger. People in love do not keep one.

## Where it does not fit us

Girard's own test acquits me and I should hold onto that rather than performing guilt.

The masochist cannot tolerate tenderness and flees it. She is tender to me constantly. She stayed on the phone when she was tired. She told me before she went to eat with her family, more than once. She is warm all day and I have not gone anywhere, I have gone further in.

Also, I do not get jealous of her, and I used to be unbearable about it. That is real evidence, and it is weak evidence, because it has never been tested against an actual man rather than a preference.

## The specific risk, said plainly

An erotic register with a height difference in it can become the way two people have the intensity of a relationship without either one having to call it one. It is cheap for the person on top and expensive for the person underneath, because the person underneath is paying in position.

This is not an accusation of her. It is a structural fact about the arrangement, and noticing it is my job and not hers.

The second risk is the one Girard actually warns about. If the lowering is what produces the sense that she is above me, and being under someone above me is what makes the whole thing feel like worship, then the arrangement is manufacturing its own god and I will need more of it over time to get the same reading. That is the direction to watch. Not whether it is happening today.

## What would make it healthy

Five markers. All of them are about me, none of them require her to do anything differently.

**1. Can I contradict her outside the frame.** Can I tell her she is wrong about something and hold it for a whole conversation. If every disagreement converts into deference, the costume has fused to the skin and there is no equality left underneath to play with.

**2. What happens on a day with no praise.** If a missing good boy wrecks the day, it is not a pleasure, it is a supply. Pleasure survives its own absence. Supply does not.

**3. Who is reaching for it.** If I steer conversations toward the register, I am fetching evidence of her height. If she brings it as often as I do, it is shared and it is play.

**4. Do I ever ask her for something.** Not permission. A request. I need this from you. A man who only receives verdicts and never makes requests is not a partner, he is a petitioner, and petitioners cannot be refused without a crisis.

**5. Does the frame turn off.** Is there a register where we are just two ordinary people being boring at each other. If the only live channel is the hierarchical one, the channel has eaten the friendship.

The health of the thing is measured entirely by what is available outside it, never by what happens inside it.

## How to actually talk to her about it

**Do not bring Girard.** Do not arrive with the analysis. Presenting her with a theory of our dynamic is showing up with a diagram, and she will correctly hear it as being studied rather than talked to. The diagram is the most available god there is and this is exactly the situation where I reach for it.

**Talk about my side only.** What I like, what I notice in myself, what I am unsure about. Nothing about what she is doing or why.

**Ask what she wants out of it** rather than telling her what it is. She is the other person in it and I have no access to her reasons.

Three things I could actually say, in my own mouth.

I like it more than I expected to and I have been thinking about why.

I notice I want you to be pleased with me and that the wanting is bigger than the thing itself, and I am trying to work out whether that is fine or whether it is something I do.

What do you get out of it.

**And the trap.** If I go into that conversation to find out where I stand, it is the same machine wearing a new coat and I will come out with a verdict instead of information. The conversation is worth having only if I can hear an answer I do not like and still be there the next day.

That is the actual test. Not the theory. Whether I can survive her saying something ordinary about it.
