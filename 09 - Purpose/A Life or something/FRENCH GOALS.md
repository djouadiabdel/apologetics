**FRENCH GOALS — April to December 2026 (36 weeks)**

**Daily non-negotiables (already in your schedule):**

- French Girard reading + writing: 30 min/day
- Bedtime French audiobook + shadow reading: 15-30 min/day

**Girard books in French:**

1. _Mensonge romantique et vérité romanesque_ (1961) — the one you already have. ~300 pages. Mimetic desire through literature. You already know the content from English, so this is the easiest entry point.

2. _La Violence et le Sacré_ (1972) — ~400 pages. Mimetic theory applied to anthropology, ritual, sacrifice. Harder vocabulary but by the time you start this you'll have 3 months of Girard-French under your belt.

3. _Je vois Satan tomber comme l'éclair_ (1999) — ~250 pages. The explicitly Christian book. Girard connects mimetic theory to the Gospels, the scapegoat mechanism to the Cross. Feeds directly into your Christology and apologetics work.

You could do **8-12** depending on length. A 5h novel takes 2-3 weeks, an 8h novel takes 3-5 weeks. Over 36 weeks that's a lot more reading input than I calculated — probably **400-500K words** from novels alone.

**Writing:** 50,000-70,000 words of French produced by December

In your 30 min French Girard block, say 15 min reading, 15 min writing. At B2 writing speed:

- April-June: ~150-200 words/day (slow, lots of pausing)
- July-September: ~300-400 words/day (connectors becoming automatic)
- October-December: ~500+ words/day (starting to flow)

**Vocabulary palace:** 300 surgically chosen palace words plus 2,500 absorbed words gets you to 3,000 new word families, which is the B2→C1 gap.
