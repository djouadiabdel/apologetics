# The Sunni system for the eternal Quran — how they actually built it

The Sunni case rests on a _systematic theological structure_, not a single verse. Here are its pillars:

**Pillar 1: God's attributes are eternal.** _Tawḥīd_ requires that God is never deprived of any perfection. God's knowledge, power, life, and _speech_ are all eternal attributes (_ṣifāt azaliyya_). To say any attribute is created is to say God was once without it — which makes God incomplete, which violates _tawḥīd_.

**Pillar 2: Speech (_kalām_) is one of these attributes.** Allah is _al-Mutakallim_ — the Speaker. The Quran is the manifestation in time of this eternal attribute. Therefore the Quran's _meaning_ (the eternal _kalām nafsī_, inner speech) is uncreated, even if the Arabic letters and sounds in time are a separate matter.

**Pillar 3: Ahmad ibn Hanbal's ruling.** "The Quran is the Speech of Allah uncreated, **whichever way it is used**" (_kayfa kāna taṣarrufuhu_). This is the operative phrase. Hanbal explicitly forbade making fine distinctions about _which sense_ of "Quran" or "speech" was uncreated. The doctrine is meant to apply universally and uniformly to anything called _kalām Allāh_.

**Pillar 4: The Mu'tazila were condemned precisely for trying to separate the _referent_ (created world, created Quran) from the _attribute_ (eternal speech).** The Sunni position was that any such separation was a Mu'tazila trick to smuggle in createdness.

**Pillar 5: The systematic principle: whatever proceeds from God's eternal attribute partakes of that attribute's nature.** Allah's speech is eternal _because_ Allah's attribute of speech is eternal — meaning, anything that _is_ God's speech is uncreated by participation in this eternal attribute.

This is the system. Now watch the trap close.

**Reason 1: The "minhu" problem.** Q 4:171 says Jesus is _kalimatuhu alqāhā ilā Maryam_ — "**His Word** which He cast into Mary." The verse does not say "Jesus is the result of His Word." **Reason 3: The "all souls would be God" reductio.** The Ahmadis argue: "if 'word of God' makes Jesus divine, then all creatures are divine, since all are 'words of God's command.'"
