

# The Falsification of "Perfect Preservation"

The most direct conclusion supported by the sources is that the claim of "word-for-word, letter-for-letter" preservation since the 7th century is historically and physically unsustainable.

- **Manuscript Evidence:** The discovery of the **Sana'a palimpsest** provides physical proof of an earlier, erased Quranic text (the "lower text") that contains substantive word-level variants, different verse orderings, and even different surah orderings compared to the standard Uthmanic version. This proves that multiple versions of the Quran existed simultaneously during the era of Muhammad’s companions.

## What the Sanaa Manuscripts Show


- The manuscripts are palimpsests — the lower layer of text was scraped off and written over. The earlier text underneath differs from the standardized Uthmanic text that replaced it.
- The lower text contains verse ordering differences. Chapters appear in sequences that do not match the canonical Uthmanic arrangement.
- The lower text contains actual word differences, not merely diacritical variations, from the canonical text.
- The existence of a palimpsest means that at a very early date — within living memory of Muhammad's companions — someone deliberately erased an older version of the Quran and replaced it with a different one.

### **What a Palimpsest Is**

Parchment was expensive in the ancient world. Scribes sometimes washed or scraped off old text and wrote new text on top — called a palimpsest.

The Sana'a manuscript is a palimpsest with **two layers of Quranic text:**

- **Upper text** — a later Quranic version conforming largely to the Uthmanic standard text
- **Lower text** — an earlier Quranic version that was **erased** and written over

Someone deliberately erased an older version of the Quran and replaced it with the standardized Uthmanic version. [en.wikipedia](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FSanaa_manuscript)

Radiocarbon dating of the parchment: **578-669 CE** with 95% confidence — placing it **within the lifetime of Muhammad's companions.** [reddit](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.reddit.com%2Fr%2FDebateReligion%2Fcomments%2Fjn8tka%2Fsanaa_manuscript_corrects_popular_quran_on_at%2F)

This is not a medieval copy. This is the earliest physical Quranic manuscript evidence — and it contains a **different text underneath.**

Multiple places where the lower text uses a different Arabic word carrying the same or similar meaning — but a different word nonetheless.
