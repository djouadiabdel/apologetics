# PART 1 — THE INJIL PROBLEM

## The Muslim Claim

> "The Injil (Gospel) that Allah gave to Jesus is not the New Testament we have today. The real Injil was a single revealed book given directly to Jesus. What Christians have now is a corrupted human document written by men after Jesus."

### VERSE 1 — Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:46-47

> _"And in their footsteps, We sent Isa son of Maryam, confirming the Torah that had come before him, and We gave him the **Injil**, in which was guidance and light... Let the people of the Gospel **judge by what Allah has revealed therein.**"_

The command is in **present tense** at the time of Muhammad:

**"Let the people of the Gospel judge by what Allah has revealed therein."**

Parse this carefully:

- **"The people of the Gospel"** = the Christians living at the time of Muhammad
- **"Judge by what Allah has revealed therein"** = use the Gospel you have right now to make judgments
- **"Therein"** = in the Gospel that is in your possession

**The question that cannot be answered:**

> "How can Allah command Christians to judge by the Injil if the Injil had been corrupted and lost centuries before Muhammad? Allah is telling them to judge by it. They must have it. What Gospel did Christians have at the time of Muhammad?"

The only Gospel Christians had at the time of Muhammad — historically, provably, without any dispute — is the **New Testament.** There is no other document. There is no lost original Injil anywhere in history. The manuscripts, the church fathers, the Dead Sea region — nothing contains a "pure original Injil."

If the Muslim says "they had a corrupted version" — then Allah is commanding Christians to judge by a corrupted book. That makes Allah either deceived or deceptive. Neither is acceptable in Islamic theology.

The Quran says the description of Muhammad is written **in the Torah and the Injil that the Jews and Christians have with them.** Present tense. At the time of Muhammad. With them.

The Muslim cannot say the Injil is not the New Testament and simultaneously use John 14:16 as the fulfillment of 7:157. They are using the New Testament as their own proof and calling it corrupted at the same time.

And the Quran says that scripture is **with them, preserved, and to be judged by.**

# PART 3 — THE WORD "CONFIRMS" (MUSADDIQ / مُصَدِّق)

## The Key Arabic Word

The Quran repeatedly uses the word **مُصَدِّق (musaddiq)** — "confirming" — to describe the relationship between the Quran and the previous scriptures.

- Quran 2:41: *"Believe in what I have sent down **confirming** (musaddiq) that which is with you"*
- Quran 2:89: *"There came to them a Book from Allah **confirming** what is with them"*
- Quran 2:97: *"He brought it down upon your heart **confirming** what was before it"*
- Quran 3:3: *"He has sent down upon you the Book in truth, **confirming** what was before it"*
- Quran 5:46: *"We gave him the Injil... **confirming** the Torah that had come before him"*

### What Does "Musaddiq" Actually Mean?

The Arabic root is **صَدَقَ (sadaqa)** — to be truthful, to verify, to bear witness to the truth and integrity of something.

This is the same root as:
- **Sadaqa** (صَدَقَة) — charity given sincerely, from a truthful heart
- **Sadiq** (صَادِق) — truthful person
- **Tasdiiq** (تَصْدِيق) — verification, authentication, confirmation of truth

**Musaddiq does not mean "confirming only parts of."**

It means: **bearing witness that the confirmed thing is true and authentic.**

### The Grammar Trap

The Muslim will say: "Confirming doesn't necessarily mean confirming ALL of it — maybe just parts."

**Closure — Use the Quran Against This:**

Quran 3:39 says John the Baptist (Yahya) came **musaddiq** — confirming a Word from Allah. That Word is Jesus.

Ask the Muslim:

> "Is John the Baptist confirming only part of Jesus? Is John saying 'Jesus is mostly reliable but some of what he says might be wrong'? If musaddiq can mean 'confirming only parts of,' then John did not fully bear witness to Jesus — which makes John an unreliable prophet. You just committed kufr (disbelief) by weakening a Quranic prophet's testimony."

The Muslim must either:
1. Accept that musaddiq means full, complete confirmation — in which case the Quran fully confirms the Bible as authentic
2. Accept that musaddiq means partial confirmation — in which case John only partially confirmed Jesus, making Jesus' prophethood uncertain and Islam undermined from within

**There is no escape.** Musaddiq is all-or-nothing. The Quran uses it for both the Bible and for John's testimony about Jesus. Whatever standard you apply to one, you must apply to the other.

# PART 5 — THE CORRUPTION CLAIM HAS NO QURANIC BASIS

## The Verses Muslims Use — And Why They Fail

Muslims typically cite four categories of verses to argue the Bible is corrupted:

### CLAIM 1 — "They change words with their tongues" (Quran 3:78)

> _"And indeed, there is among them a party who alter the Scripture with their tongues so you may think it is from the Scripture, but it is not from the Scripture. And they say, 'This is from Allah,' but it is not from Allah."_

**What this actually says:**

This describes people **speaking falsely and claiming it is scripture** — oral deception in interpretation and presentation. It says nothing about the written text being altered. The Arabic **يَلْوُونَ أَلْسِنَتَهُم (yalwūna alsinatahum)** means "they twist their tongues" — a description of **oral misrepresentation**, not textual corruption.

This verse is about false teachers misquoting and misrepresenting the scripture — which happens in every religious tradition including Islam. It is not a statement that the Biblical text itself was changed.

### CLAIM 2 — "They have forgotten a portion of what they were reminded" (Quran 5:13-14)

> _"So for their breaking of the covenant We cursed them and made their hearts hard. They distort words from their proper usages and have forgotten a portion of that of which they were reminded."_

**What this actually says:**

**Forgotten** and **corrupted** are not the same. To forget means to neglect, to not practice, to lose emphasis on certain teachings — not to alter the written text. The accusation is of **neglect and abandonment**, not textual falsification.

### CLAIM 3 — "A group of them used to hear the words of Allah and then distort them" (Quran 2:75)

> _"Do you covet that they would believe for you while a party of them used to hear the words of Allah and then distort it after they had understood it while they were knowing?"_

**What this actually says:**

This refers to a specific group who heard Allah's words and **distorted them in their interpretations and presentations** — again oral and interpretive, not written textual corruption. The same verse says they did this "after they had understood it" — meaning the original was clear and understood, and they chose to misrepresent it in speech.

### CLAIM 4 — "They write the scripture with their own hands and say it is from Allah" (Quran 2:79)

> _"So woe to those who write the scripture with their own hands, then say, 'This is from Allah,' in order to exchange it for a small price. Woe to them for what their hands have written and woe to them for what they earn."_

**What this actually says:**

This describes a specific group — probably scribes or religious leaders — who produced **additional documents** claiming divine authority for personal gain. It does not say the entire Bible or Torah was replaced. It describes corrupt individuals adding to or producing false documents — something Islam's own tradition acknowledges happened with hadith fabrication as well.

**None of these four verses says:**
- The Torah was corrupted
- The Gospel was corrupted
- The Biblical text as a whole cannot be trusted
- Muhammad's audience had a corrupted Bible

**What the Quran actually says about the Bible is the opposite:**
- It is with them ✓
- They are reciting it ✓
- They must judge by it ✓
- The Quran confirms it ✓
- Muhammad is described in it ✓

**In every option, either:**
- The Bible is preserved and reliable (Option A), or
