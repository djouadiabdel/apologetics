
### The 5 Objective Historical Tests

These tests do **not** assume the Bible is true. They rely solely on external evidence and consistency:

1. **Prior Independent Foretelling** Was the person explicitly predicted in neutral, independent sources _before_ they appeared?
2. **Large-Scale, Independent Corroboration** Were their key claims or extraordinary events witnessed and recorded by large numbers of unrelated people from diverse backgrounds?
3. **Specific, Verifiable Fulfilled Predictions** Did they make precise, testable predictions (with clear timelines or unique details) that later history confirmed — without post-event reinterpretation?
4. **Non-Circular Validation** Does the proof of their prophethood rest on external historical evidence, or does it loop back to their own claims and writings?
5. **Alignment with Established Historical Record** Does their core message and reported events align with — or contradict — the consensus of independent historical sources from multiple civilizations?

### Why Muhammad Fails the Objective Tests (Evidence-Based)

1. **Prior Independent Foretelling — Fails** No pre-610 AD Jewish, Christian, or pagan document unambiguously predicts Muhammad. The verses Muslims cite (Deuteronomy 18, Isaiah 42, John 14–16) were understood for centuries by Jews and Christians as referring to Joshua, the Messiah, or the Holy Spirit — never an Arabian prophet. No neutral scholar before Muhammad made that connection.
2. **Large-Scale Independent Corroboration — Weak** The first revelation, the Night Journey, and the splitting of the moon were witnessed only by Muhammad or a tiny group. Unlike Moses (national event) or Jesus (crowds + hostile contemporary sources), there are no large-scale, independent, non-Muslim records from the 7th century confirming these miracles.
3. **Specific Fulfilled Predictions — Weak** While some broad statements exist (e.g., Surah 30 on Roman victory), they lack the precision, timing, and verifiability of Biblical examples such as Daniel’s exact succession of empires or Isaiah’s naming of Cyrus 150 years in advance. Many claimed fulfillments rely on later Islamic reinterpretation.
4. **Non-Circular Validation — Clear Failure** The main proof is the Quran itself (which claims to come from God through Muhammad) and Muhammad’s own testimony. The “prophecy” argument only becomes “clear” _after_ accepting his prophethood. This is classic circular reasoning: “Believe me because my book says I am a prophet, and believe my book because I am a prophet.”
5. **Alignment with Established History — Fails** The Quran directly contradicts well-established 1st-century history:
    - It denies the crucifixion of Jesus (Quran 4:157).
    - Yet Roman historian Tacitus, Jewish historian Josephus, and every early Christian and pagan source confirm the crucifixion. This is not a minor theological difference — it rewrites documented history.

### Biblical Standards for Testing Prophets (Non-Circular)

For those who accept the Bible’s authority, Scripture itself provides clear, external tests:

1. **Deuteronomy 18:20-22** — 100% accuracy required. Even one failed prediction in God’s name = false prophet.
2. **Deuteronomy 13:1-5** — Even with signs and wonders, if the message leads people away from the God of the Bible or to a different gospel, it is false.
3. **Matthew 7:15-20** — “By their fruits you will know them” (doctrine + character + results).
4. **1 John 4:1-3** — Test the spirits: Does it affirm that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh (the Biblical, crucified-and-risen Jesus)?
5. **Galatians 1:8** — Even if an angel from heaven brings a different gospel, it is cursed.

These tests are **external** — they do not rely on the claimant’s own word.

### True Prophets According to the Bible (They Pass All Tests)

- **Moses** — Public miracles before millions; Torah given openly before the nation; consistent with prior patriarchal revelation.
- **Isaiah** — Dozens of specific prophecies fulfilled centuries later.
- **Daniel** — Precise predictions of successive empires (Babylon → Persia → Greece → Rome) that unfolded exactly as foretold.
- **Jesus** — Fulfilled hundreds of Old Testament prophecies; public miracles; resurrection attested by multiple independent sources; perfectly consistent with prior Scripture.
- **John the Baptist & the Apostles** — Fulfilled prior prophecies; confirmed Jesus; no failed predictions; aligned with the Old Testament.

None of them rely on circular reasoning. Their validation comes from **prior independent Scripture**, **public evidence**, and **historical fulfillment**.

1. **Fails the “Jesus Test” (1 John 4:1-3)** — The Quran explicitly denies the crucifixion (4:157) and the Sonship of Jesus (9:30, 5:116). This directly contradicts the Biblical Jesus.
2. **Different Gospel (Galatians 1:8)** — Islam teaches salvation by works + belief in Allah and Muhammad. Christianity teaches salvation by grace through faith in Jesus’ death and resurrection.
3. **No Prior Biblical Prophecy** — As shown above, the cited verses do not refer to him in context.
4. **Leads People Away from the Biblical God (Deuteronomy 13)** — Redirects worship and doctrine to a fundamentally different conception of God and a new final prophet.
