### 2. Detailed Talking Points by Prophet

#### **Joseph Smith (Latter-day Saints)**

**What makes Joseph Smith a “prophet”?**

- Claimed direct visits from God the Father and Jesus Christ (First Vision, 1820)
- Received multiple visits from **Angel Moroni** starting in 1823
- Translated the **Book of Mormon** from golden plates
- Claimed to restore the true Church of Jesus Christ

#### Strongest Criteria Matches (Very Detailed)

**1. Brought a new book that confirms + corrects corrupted parts**

- The **Book of Mormon** claims to be “Another Testament of Jesus Christ”
- It says it confirms the Bible where correct and restores “plain and precious” truths that were lost or removed from the Bible
- Joseph said an angel told him the Bible was missing many important parts

**2. Claimed he was prophesied in previous scriptures**

- Joseph and early Latter-day Saints taught that the Bible prophesies about him and the Book of Mormon
- Key verses they use: Ezekiel 37 (two sticks = Bible + Book of Mormon), Isaiah 29, Deuteronomy 18, Revelation 14:6

**3. Books are corrupted + new book is most correct and supersedes**

- Joseph declared: “I told the brethren that the Book of Mormon was the most correct of any book on earth, and the keystone of our religion”
- Latter-day Saints believe the Book of Mormon is more accurate and complete than the Bible in its current form

**4. Rapid growth despite opposition**

- Started in 1830 with 6 members
- Grew to thousands within years despite mob violence, Joseph’s imprisonment, and his murder in 1844
- Today: Approximately **17–18 million members** worldwide

#### The 11 Witnesses – The Strongest Part (Bulletproof Details)

This is Joseph Smith’s **biggest advantage** over almost all other false prophets.

**The Three Witnesses** (Saw the angel + golden plates in vision):

1. **Oliver Cowdery** – Joseph’s main scribe
2. **David Whitmer**
3. **Martin Harris**

- All three testified they saw **Angel Moroni** and the golden plates in a **bright light**
- They heard the voice of God declare the translation was correct
- All three later left the church but **never denied** their testimony, even on their deathbeds

**The Eight Witnesses** (Physically handled the plates – no angel):

1. Christian Whitmer
2. Jacob Whitmer
3. Peter Whitmer Jr.
4. John Whitmer
5. Hiram Page
6. Joseph Smith Sr. (Joseph’s father)
7. Hyrum Smith (Joseph’s brother)
8. Samuel H. Smith (Joseph’s brother)

- These eight men testified they **saw and handled** the golden plates with their own hands
- They described the plates as having “the appearance of gold” and engravings on both sides
- Most of them also later left or were excommunicated, but **none ever recanted** their testimony

**Total: 11 Witnesses**

**Why this is powerful**:

- Two completely different groups with different experiences
- Written testimonies published in every copy of the Book of Mormon since 1830
- Most witnesses were related or close associates, but several were not (the Whitmer family + Hiram Page)
- Several lived long lives and were questioned repeatedly — they never changed their story

**Talking Line**: “Joseph Smith has **11 named witnesses** who saw or handled the golden plates — something almost no other religious founder in history can match. Even after many left the church, they never denied what they saw.”

#### Miracles & Signs with Strong Chains

- Multiple early Saints reported healings when Joseph laid hands on the sick
- Eyewitness accounts of Joseph dictating the Book of Mormon for hours without notes or manuscripts
- The translation process itself is considered miraculous by members

#### The Circular Reasoning Problem (Honest Counter)

Even with 11 witnesses, the core issue remains:

- The **witnesses only prove the plates existed** — they do **not** prove the _content_ is from God
- The claim that the Book of Mormon is scripture still rests on **Joseph’s word**
- The witnesses’ testimony ultimately depends on Joseph being a true prophet
- It creates a circle: “The plates are real because the witnesses saw them → The witnesses are reliable because Joseph was a prophet → Joseph was a prophet because the plates are real”

**Talking Line**: “Even with 11 witnesses, it’s still circular. The witnesses prove Joseph had something physical — but only Joseph’s claim makes it _divine revelation_.”

#### **Baha’u’llah (Baha’i Faith) – The Closest Parallel**

**What makes him a “prophet”?**

- Claimed to be the **Promised One** of all previous religions (the return of Christ, the Mahdi, the Kalki Avatar, etc.)
- Received revelation over many years and wrote the **Kitab-i-Aqdas** (Most Holy Book)

**Exact Claims That Mirror Prophet Muhammad ﷺ**:

- **New Book that confirms + corrects previous ones** Baha’u’llah wrote that his revelation **fulfills and supersedes** all previous religions. He said the Bible and Quran were true in their time but are now completed by his revelation.
- **Claimed to be prophesied in previous scriptures** Baha’is teach that Baha’u’llah is prophesied in:
    - The Bible (many verses)
    - The Quran
    - Hadith collections
    - Hindu and Buddhist scriptures
- **Books are corrupted + new revelation is final and most correct** He taught that previous scriptures have been **corrupted or misinterpreted** over time, and his revelation is the **final and most complete** one.
- **Rapid global growth despite opposition** Started in 1863 in Persia. Faced severe persecution. Today Baha’i has millions of followers in over 200 countries.

**Miracles & Signs (Hadith-Style Chains)**:

- Early Baha’is recorded many stories of healings and supernatural events around Baha’u’llah.
- Multiple independent early followers wrote about his charisma and claimed miracles.
- Chains exist within Baha’i historical records, though not as strong as Joseph Smith’s 11 witnesses.

**The Circular Reasoning (Clear & Direct)**:

- Baha’u’llah claims: “I am the Promised One because God revealed it to me.”
- The proof is only in **his own writings**.
- He says previous prophets prophesied about him — but those interpretations only appear **after** he made the claim.
- Classic circle: “I am true because my revelation says I am true, and my revelation is true because I received it from God.”

**Talking Line (Very Effective)**:

> “Baha’u’llah does **exactly** what Prophet Muhammad ﷺ did — brings a new revelation, claims to be prophesied in previous books, says earlier scriptures are corrupted or completed, and builds a global movement. The only difference is that with Muhammad ﷺ, the prophecy existed **before** he claimed it, and hundreds of people memorized and wrote down the Quran during his lifetime. With Baha’u’llah, everything starts with him.”

- **New revelation that supersedes current understanding** He received many revelations and wrote over 80 books. His followers believe his writings clarify and complete Islamic understanding.
- **Rapid global growth** Founded in 1889. Despite heavy opposition (including from mainstream Muslims), Ahmadiyya now has millions of members in over 200 countries.

**Miracles & Signs (Hadith-Style Chains)**:

- Early Ahmadiyya sources record many stories of his revelations and some claimed miracles.
- Multiple companions wrote detailed accounts of his life and signs.
- Chains exist in Ahmadiyya literature, though they are internal to the movement.

**The Circular Reasoning (Very Clear)**:

- He says: “I am the Mahdi because the hadith prophesy about me.”
- But the specific way he applies those hadith only appears **after** he made the claim.
- No scholar before 1889 interpreted those hadith as referring to Mirza Ghulam Ahmad.
- The prophecy only “exists” because he says it does.

**Talking Line (Very Strong)**:

> “Mirza Ghulam Ahmad uses the **exact same method** as Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — claiming to be the one prophesied in previous scriptures. But with Muhammad ﷺ, the prophecies existed in the Bible **centuries before** he was born. With Mirza Ghulam Ahmad, the ‘prophecy’ only becomes clear after he claims it. That is the definition of circular reasoning.”

### 3. The Circular Reasoning Problem (Most Important Section)

**The Pattern All False Prophets Share**:

1. **Claim**: “I am a prophet / the Promised One.”
2. **Proof**: “Because God/angel told me directly.”
3. **Evidence for the Proof**: “Because I said so / my book says so.”
4. **Result**: A perfect closed circle with **no independent verification** from before their claim.

**Key Talking Point**:

> “Every single one of these prophets uses the same circular reasoning: ‘I am true because my revelation says I am true, and my revelation is true because I received it.’
> 
> Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is different because:
> 
> - He was **illiterate**
> - He was **prophesied** in previous scriptures (Bible & others)
> - His revelation (**Quran**) was memorized and written by **hundreds** of companions during his lifetime
> - The message was confirmed by **external sources** before and during his time
> - His movement became a **major world civilization** in one generation”


 The Universal Pattern (Most Powerful Section)

**All False Prophets Follow This Exact Formula**:

1. **Claim** new revelation or new status (prophet, Messiah, Mahdi, Promised One)
2. **Claim** this was prophesied in previous books
3. **Claim** previous books are corrupted or incomplete
4. **Provide** their own revelation as the proof
5. **Build** a movement around themselves

**The Fatal Flaw (Circular Reasoning)**:

- The prophecy only “exists” because **they** say it does.
- There is **no independent record** from before their claim.
- The evidence always loops back to the claimant himself.

**How Prophet Muhammad ﷺ Breaks This Pattern**:

- He was **illiterate** (unique among major prophets)
- Prophecies about him existed in the **Bible and other books centuries before** he was born
- His revelation (Quran) was **memorized and written by hundreds** during his lifetime
- The message was confirmed by **external sources** (previous scriptures + his character)
- His movement became a **major world civilization** in one generation
