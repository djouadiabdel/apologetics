### The Phantom Prophecy Problem (Clean Version You Can Use)

**The 4-Question Test:**

1. **Did a previous prophet predict your coming?**
    - Joseph Smith: Yes — Joseph (son of Jacob) in Genesis 50:33.

4. **Who told you about this prophecy?**

### Even If Joseph Smith Looks “Better on Paper” Than Muhammad…

One common defense from Latter-day Saints is that comparing Joseph Smith to Muhammad is unfair because their stories are very different. They point out several ways in which Joseph’s case actually looks **stronger** than Muhammad’s on the surface:

- Joseph was much younger (14–17) when he claimed to receive his first vision, while Muhammad was 40.
- Joseph produced the Book of Mormon in roughly **two to three months**, while the Quran was revealed to Muhammad over **22 years**.
- Joseph had **multiple people** who claimed to see visions and objects with him (Oliver Cowdery, David Whitmer, Martin Harris, etc.), whereas Muhammad’s experiences were almost entirely private.
- Joseph had **physical objects** associated with his revelations (golden plates, Urim and Thummim, breastplate), while Muhammad had none.
- The Book of Mormon is a **coherent narrative** spanning over a thousand years, while the Quran is mostly a series of repetitive sermons and short stories.

Even if we fully accept all of these points and agree that Joseph Smith’s story looks **cleaner and more impressive** than Muhammad’s on paper, **it still doesn’t save him**.

Because the real test isn’t about age, speed, shared visions, or physical objects.

The real test is this:

**Both men used the exact same method to prove they were prophets:**

1. They claimed that earlier prophets had foretold their coming.
2. They said the original prophecy had been lost or corrupted.
3. They claimed that God conveniently re-revealed that lost prophecy **directly to them**.
4. Therefore, the **only evidence** for the prophecy about them comes from the very man the prophecy is supposedly about.

This is the fatal flaw. It creates an unbreakable circle:

> “I am a true prophet because previous prophets foretold me… and the only proof that they foretold me is my own word.”

This is the same exact pattern used by Joseph Smith (Genesis 50:33 “restored” only in his translation) and Muhammad (Surah 61:6 — the prophecy of “Ahmed” that exists nowhere except in the Quran).

So even if we give Joseph Smith every advantage and say his story looks better on paper than Muhammad’s, he is still guilty of the **same circular reasoning** that makes Muhammad’s claim obviously fraudulent.

In other words: **Joseph Smith may be the more impressive fake prophet… but he’s still a fake prophet.**
