The structure is never “Paul says X, therefore X is true.” 

The actual structure historians use is: “
Paul says X. 
• X is extremely early. 
• X was checked by the named eyewitnesses who were still alive and could have falsified it. 
• X is consistent with every external detail we can test. 
• Paul had every worldly reason not to say X (he lost status, career, safety). • He suffered horribly for decades and never recanted. 
• No contemporary source contradicts him.”
