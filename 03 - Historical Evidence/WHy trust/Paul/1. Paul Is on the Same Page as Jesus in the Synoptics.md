There is a well-known list (widely circulated and documented) of **34 specific places** where Paul’s teaching directly echoes or parallels Jesus’ words in Matthew, Mark, or Luke. These are not forced stretches — they are clear overlaps in ethics, prayer, money, forgiveness, enemies, marriage, etc. Paul is passing on the same message he received from the eyewitnesses.

We’re not going to memorize all 34 (you don’t need to). The instruction in Track 1 was: **own four or five deeply** with chapter-and-verse for both sides. Here are the **strongest four** (the ones that hit hardest against the skeptic claim):

1. **Love fulfills the law** (the greatest commandment) **Paul** — Romans 13:8-10
    
    > Owe no one anything, except to love each other, for **the one who loves another has fulfilled the law**. For the commandments, “You shall not commit adultery, You shall not murder, You shall not steal, You shall not covet,” and any other commandment, are summed up in this word: “**You shall love your neighbor as yourself**.” Love does no wrong to a neighbor; therefore **love is the fulfilling of the law**.
    
    **Jesus** — Matthew 22:36-40 (also Mark 12:28-31)
    
    > “Teacher, which is the great commandment in the Law?” And he said to him, “**You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart** and with all your soul and with all your mind. This is the great and first commandment. And a second is like it: **You shall love your neighbor as yourself**. On these two commandments depend all the Law and the Prophets.”
    
    → Exact same summary: love God + love neighbor = the whole law fulfilled. Paul is repeating Jesus’ teaching, not replacing it.
    
2. **Bless those who curse you / love your enemies** **Paul** — Romans 12:14, 17-21
    
    > **Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse them**… Repay no one evil for evil… if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink… Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.
    
    **Jesus** — Luke 6:27-28 (also Matthew 5:44)
    
    > “But I say to you who hear, **Love your enemies, do good to those who hate you, bless those who curse you**, pray for those who abuse you.”
    
    → Almost identical wording and command. This is the opposite of “Paul invented a harsher religion” — he’s teaching the exact radical enemy-love Jesus commanded.
    
3. **Return evil with good / turn the other cheek in action** **Paul** — Romans 12:17-21 (same passage as above)
    
    > “Repay no one evil for evil… never avenge yourselves… if your enemy is hungry, feed him…”
    
    **Jesus** — Matthew 5:38-48
    
    > “You have heard that it was said, ‘An eye for an eye’… But I say to you, **Do not resist the one who is evil**. But if anyone slaps you on the right cheek, turn to him the other also… Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you…”
    
    → Paul is living out and teaching the same non-retaliation / enemy-love ethic Jesus gave in the Sermon on the Mount.
    
4. **The Lord’s Supper (communion) — Jesus’ death as the new covenant** **Paul** — 1 Corinthians 11:23-26
    
    > For **I received from the Lord** what I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus on the night when he was betrayed took bread, and when he had given thanks, he broke it, and said, “This is my body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of me.” In the same way also he took the cup, after supper, saying, “**This cup is the new covenant in my blood**…”
    
    **Jesus** — Luke 22:19-20 (also Matthew 26:26-28; Mark 14:22-24)
    
    > And he took bread, and when he had given thanks, he broke it and gave it to them, saying, “This is my body, which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me.” And likewise the cup after they had eaten, saying, “**This cup that is poured out for you is the new covenant in my blood**.”
    
    → Paul even uses the exact technical language of “I received… I delivered” (the same rabbinic terms from the creed in Point 1). He is passing on the exact words and meaning Jesus used at the Last Supper.
### Why this destroys the “Paul contradicted Jesus” claim

- These are **not** minor overlaps. They are core ethical teachings (love, enemies, law, the meaning of Jesus’ death) that sit at the heart of both men’s messages.
- Paul repeatedly says he is handing on what he **received** — from Jesus directly (the revelation) and from the eyewitness apostles who walked with Jesus.
- The supposed “big contradiction” (law vs. grace) is actually Paul **explaining the same story**: Jesus came to fulfill the law (Matthew 5:17), and his death/resurrection is how that fulfillment works for everyone (Jews and Gentiles). Paul isn’t abolishing Jesus’ ethics — he’s showing how the cross makes them possible.
