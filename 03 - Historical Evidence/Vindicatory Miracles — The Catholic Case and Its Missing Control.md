## 1. What is being claimed

Not that miracles happen. Something narrower and much more aggressive.

That there exists a public, checkable signal, called in advance or inspected afterwards by hostile parties, which identifies one claimant as speaking for God. And that when you read what the signal is attached to, the doctrines confirmed are Catholic distinctives.

If that is right, it is the answer to [[The Communication Objection]], and it is the only kind of thing that could be.

---

## 2. The distinction the whole argument rests on

This is the good move and it should be stated at full strength before anything is done to it.

A **non-vindicatory** miracle is compassion. A healing in answer to prayer, in any tradition, by anyone. It confirms nothing beyond itself.

A **vindicatory** miracle is a signature. God performing a public sign with the intent of authenticating a message or a messenger, where the messenger is binding consciences to accept the message as from God.

The distinction does real work, and it disposes of the standard reply in one line. *Every religion has miracle claims.* Yes, and most of them are the first kind. A Pentecostal minister healing someone in Jesus' name does not authenticate Pentecostal ecclesiology, because nobody called the sign forth in witness to Pentecostal ecclesiology. Peter worked miracles after denying Christ three times. The miracle does not certify everything in the worker's head.

So the question becomes empirical and narrow. **What has God performed vindicatory miracles to support?**

## 4. The engine, and it is one move repeated

Every case is individually deniable. Fraud, misdiagnosis, coincidence, lying witnesses. The argument does not contest that. It says: fine, take the exit, and then keep taking it, and watch what you are carrying by the end.

> Promise me that you are out of coincidences.

That is the whole engine. Not any single case. The conjunction, and the fact that the conjunction has a direction.

## 5. The audit

This vault does not get to like an argument because it points the right way. The rule from `interlock/DESIGN-RATIONALE.md` is the rule here.

> Never tune the instrument, and never tune the sample, to pass.

### 5.1 The shape is the shape this vault exists to catch

*Multiply the improbabilities* is a conjunction argument, and a conjunction argument cannot be lost by any single counterexample, because each counterexample is re-priced as one more coincidence the other side must swallow.

That is the accusation against Girard, restated. Hand a Girardian a text where the victim looks guilty and the mechanism explains it. Hand him one where the victim looks innocent and the mechanism explains that too. There is no text that produces the words *this one does not fit*.

Ask the same question here and it is a fair question. **What case, if it came back clean and non-Catholic, would produce the words *this one does not fit*?** If the answer is none, the case is not evidence in the sense being claimed for it, whatever its conclusion.

### 5.2 The sample is selected by outcome, and the control is missing

The corpus was assembled by going looking for well-attested Catholic miracles. It is not a blind pass over a matched non-Catholic corpus scored by the same rubric.

The invitation is made explicitly. *Go mount a case this compelling for another sect.* An invitation is not a control.

And this vault knows exactly what an unrun control costs, twice, in writing.

- **F3**, the apocryphal control on the contradiction count. Pre-registered. Still not run. The count's discriminating power is therefore unmeasured.
- **The restraint control.** Pre-registered 5 August, orphaned nine days while two other studies began, and when it finally ran on 14 August, **two of its five falsifiers fired**, and H2 came back at 0.577 against an invalidity bound of 0.50. The instrument was not clean.

An unrun control is not a control that would have passed. That is the finding this repository already paid for.

### 5.3 The escape hatch

The sampling objection is anticipated and answered like this. If God intends to send a message, verification is providential, so a bias in which miracles get scientifically verified is a bias God could have corrected and did not, therefore the distribution is the message.

Look at what that does. It converts the missing control into evidence. Any absence in the record becomes intentional, and no possible distribution of verified miracles could count against the thesis.

That is the escape hatch refused in Round 4, named in the file so it would not be walked into. It should be refused here.

### 5.4 The rubric was written after the corpus was known

Protestant healings are conceded as real and then classed as non-vindicatory, because no one called them forth in witness to a Protestant distinctive.

The reasoning is sound. But notice who wrote the rubric. The criterion *vindicatory means attached to a distinctive doctrine* was chosen by someone who already knew which distinctives had an apparition corpus attached to them, and Marian apparitions speak in propositions while a healing at a men's retreat does not. The rubric rewards traditions whose miracle tradition is verbal.

That may be a feature. It has not been shown to be one.

### 5.5 What survives the audit, and it is not nothing

Split the corpus the way [[Myths — Forensic Record]] splits the interlock run, into the half that depends on charity and the half that does not.

**The half that depends on charity.** Every doctrinal reading. What a private revelation vindicates, what the odor of sanctity signifies, whether the crown seen at Zeitoun is a proposition about Mary. These are interpretations and they are where a knowing thumb rests, which is precisely why the interlock fit-ratio was disowned in its own file.

**The half that does not.** A mercury thermometer reads 52 C or it does not. A blinded laboratory in Białystok returns cardiac muscle or it returns bacteria. A social security fraud investigation closes or it does not. Two translations of a deposition either agree or they do not.

The second half is the same class of fact as *a schoolmaster dies in one infancy gospel and blushes in the other*. Facts on the page, not requiring anyone's good will.

**So the honest position is that this corpus has an objective half that has never been examined here, and a doctrinal half that should be treated as untrustworthy on principle.** That is not a rejection. It is a triage, and it is the same triage that made the interlock run publishable.

## 6. What this does to the Church Question, and it is not what it looks like

Read as a whole, the case is aimed at Rome. Two of its strongest items do not go there, and one of them is mine.

**Zeitoun happened over a Coptic Orthodox church.** Saint Mary's, Zeitoun. The first witnesses were Muslim garage workers. The investigating commission was appointed by **Coptic Pope Kyrillos VI**, and the bishops who verified it, Athanasius and Gregorios, were his. The largest mass Marian apparition of the twentieth century, roughly a million witnesses, occurred over a church in communion with Alexandria and was authenticated by the Coptic patriarchate.

Its vindicatory payload is Marian intercession and Marian devotion. **Rome and Alexandria both hold those.** It discriminates between Christianity and its denial. It does not discriminate between Rome and Alexandria at all, and it is being counted as though it does.

**Soufanieh is a unity claim before it is a papal one.** Myrna Nazzour is Melkite Greek Catholic. Her husband is Orthodox. Among the primary eyewitnesses is the **Syriac Orthodox patriarch**, who wrote the foreword to the chronicle. The message that cites Matthew 16 is read as vindicating Peter's office. Read the sentence again and it is a call for the churches to be one, delivered in Damascus to a mixed household, in a country whose Christians are overwhelmingly not Latin.

That reading may still be right. It is contested at exactly the point where a vindicatory argument needs it clean, and the contest is not acknowledged.

**And the discipline that applies is already written in [[00 - Le Plan]].** *Ne jamais ajuster l'instrument, ne jamais ajuster l'échantillon.* It applies in both directions. If I find myself discounting this corpus because it points at Rome, that is tuning. If I find myself reading Zeitoun as Latin evidence because the case I heard it in was Latin, that is also tuning. Both get written down.

## 7. The round to run, and the instrument is already on the disk

This is the part worth doing rather than arguing about.

`interlock/build_run.py` already emits witnesses as packets under opaque codes W1, W2, W3 in shuffled order, seals the identities in `key.json`, logs gaps per witness before the key is opened, and scores fits against collisions. It was built for gospel pericopes. **The Fatima depositions and the Zeitoun testimonies are the same data type.** Many independent witnesses, incidental detail, no coordination, and a hostile press.

The specific claim it would test is the strongest one in the whole corpus and it is not a miracle claim. It is an undesigned coincidence. Witnesses at Alburitel, fifteen kilometres away, facing the direction of the Cova rather than the sun, describing a three-fold descent that matches what the Cova crowd described, with no contact between the groups. That is a fit in the exact technical sense already defined, where one account's dangling detail is incidentally completed by another.

**Pre-registration, before any coding.**

- P1. The Fatima independent-pair fit ratio exceeds the apocryphal control's 0.00 and is not below the canonical 0.50.
- P2. Hard collisions per thousand words fall below the apocryphal 0.95.
- F1. If the coder cannot log a fit without already knowing the apologetic literature on Fatima, the run is disqualified for the same reason the canonical fit-ratio was disowned.
- F2. If a matched non-Catholic apparition corpus is not run alongside it, this study inherits the F3 defect and does not get reported as a result.

F2 is the one that matters. It is the control section 5.2 says is missing, and running it here would be the first time anyone has.

## 8. Where this leaves it

The case is stronger than I expected and weaker than it presents itself.

Stronger, because the objective half is real, recent, documented, and has never been assessed in this vault. Weaker, because its engine is the shape that cannot lose, its sample is chosen by outcome, and its answer to that charge converts the gap into a proof.

**And it does not reach the thing it is offered for.** Even a clean pass on the Fatima run gives a public sign attached to a claimant. The ceiling from [[Naturalism as a Presupposition — Not a Conclusion]] is untouched, and a man who holds that the prior decides will still hold it afterwards.

What it would do is smaller and worth having. It would take [[The Communication Objection]] out of the category of things nobody has tried to answer.
