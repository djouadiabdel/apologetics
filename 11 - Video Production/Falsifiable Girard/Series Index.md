# Falsifiable Girard — Series Index

A video series built out of `~/Myths`: a project that took René Girard's theory
of the scapegoat, turned four sentences of it into code, tested it eight times,
failed most of them, and caught itself eight separate ways.

**The subject is not Girard. The subject is what it takes to build something that
can tell you no.**

## What each season is doing

**Season 1 — Girard From Zero.** For someone who has never heard of him. Mimetic
desire, the model becoming the rival, doubles, the crisis, the scapegoat, the
**one episode per stereotype**, myth against Gospel. Then an episode on **who
says he is wrong**, and four reading the actual texts. **Plants six numbers that later seasons detonate.**

**Season 2 — Building Something That Can Say No.** Why "unfalsifiable" is the
charge that matters, and what answering it costs. Then the half nobody teaches:
corpus craft and lexicon craft. Two fetcher bugs, 3,037 undecoded entities, a
threefold undercount caused by a comma, 960 junk hits deleted from your own
instrument, and the fix that was refused because it would have been cheating.

**Season 3 — Four Ways To Fail.** The instrument meets the texts four times and
loses three. Every failure has an exact mechanism and the mechanism is the
lesson. Contains **episode 12, what a null holds constant**, which is the most
transferable idea in the series.

**Season 4 — The Repairs.** Reconstructing a p-value whose script was lost, and
identifying it by its standard deviation rather than its p-value. The null that
had never been built for any round. A translation control across 290 years.
Localization, twice, because the first null destroyed the thing it was measuring.
And the diagnostic that explains four rounds of null results.
**Plus round 9, run for this series in August 2026**: the `king` deletion the
project flagged in July, deferred as real-but-deferrable, and never carried out.
It removes half the `marks` bucket and moves nothing load-bearing.

**Season 5 — The Bible Instrument.** A different study on the same principles.
Blinded witness codes and a sealed key. 671 alleged contradictions reduced to 2.
Nine Aristotelian gates. A falsifier that fires **on the coder**. The fathers,
graded on the same scale as everyone else, including Eusebius stating this
project's own falsifiability criterion in the fourth century.

**Season 5b — One Contradiction, Worked Properly.** The most repeatable format in
the series. One allegation per episode: what both sides say, what the Greek
actually says, what the rescue costs.

**Season 6 — Novels.** Girard on Quixote, Bovary, Stendhal. Why Bovary scores
zero and what that says about where the author is standing. And your own novella
scored on the same rubric.

**Season 7 — The Method, Extracted.** The transferable part. Verdict flatness on
both sides. Dissolution beats harmonisation. Bundling. A fired falsifier is a
success. **A check that cannot fail prints reassurance.** And what the project
accidentally learned about the texts.

## Production

**Batch by episode type, not by season.** Six types:

| type | shape | examples |
|---|---|---|
| concept | one idea, one character anchor | S1 ep 1-14 |
| tool | one statistic taught from zero | S3 ep 2, 9, 12, 15 |
| craft | one practical skill | S2 ep 4-10 |
| failure | what we expected, what happened, why, what it cost | S3 most |
| worked-item | one contradiction or one text, end to end | S5b all |
| reading | read a passage, locate the structure by hand | S1 ep 15-18 |

A worked-item episode is the same shape every time. Once the pattern is set you
can film six in a sitting, which is what makes 132 achievable rather than
theoretical.

## The two-document rule

Every episode has **two** documents, and this folder holds the first kind.

**`Research/`** teaches the topic. Prose, cited, long, with the numbers cold and
the conclusions absent. That is what these season files are.

**`Filming/`** is the board you annotate while the camera runs. Cold content is
evidence you must not misremember. Hot content is the short phrases that fire
delivery. **The conclusion is not on the page.** You supply it live.

## Sources

Everything in these documents traces to `~/Myths`, which is a git repository with
47 commits. Every claim can be checked against a file and a commit hash, and the
series should say so often enough that a hostile viewer goes and looks.

**Girard citations, VERIFIED 15 Aug 2026 against the books.** Four were tier K.
All four have now been opened, and three of the four needed a correction.

| claim | cited as | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "no dissenting voice is ever heard" | *I See Satan*, p. 3 | **VERIFIED, exact, p. 3** |
| the conversion, three bits | *DDN*, p. 294 | **VERIFIED, exact, p. 294** |
| the dissident minority | *I See Satan*, p. 121 | quote exact, **page is 122**, fix it |
| the four stereotypes | *Scapegoat*, ch. 2 | ch. 2 is titled "Stereotypes of Persecution" and gives **1 to 3**; the fourth is named at the head of **ch. 3**. Cite **ch. 2-3** |

Editions used: *The Scapegoat*, Johns Hopkins, trans. Freccero; *I See Satan Fall
Like Lightning*, Orbis, ISBN 1570753199; *Deceit, Desire, and the Novel*, Johns
Hopkins, trans. Freccero. **All four are on disk.**

**Two quotations elide an intervening sentence with no marker**, at Season 1 episode 12 and Season 3 episode 17. Both quoted halves are genuine and exact; a
sentence sits between them in the original. **Fix before filming**, because reading
them as continuous is the kind of small overstatement this series exists to object
to.

**And the check paid for itself.** *The Scapegoat* ch. 3 contains Girard's own
decision rule, which nobody in the project had read: **"Not all the stereotypes
must be present: three are enough and often even two."** That is a level-2 rule in
Season 1 episode 10's ladder, it is weaker than the version this project measured,
and **this project's own 90% figure refutes it directly.** Season 1 **episode 11 is now an entire episode on it.** *DDN* p. 294 also lists **seven** transformations, of which the
conversion instrument scores **three**, and that selection was never declared.

The full forensic record, 85 pages, lives at
`14 - Method & Memory/Myths — Forensic Record.pdf` and is the reference to check
facts against when a season file compresses something.

## Glossary

Every term the series uses, defined once. If a term is not here it does not get
used on camera.

### From Girard

| term | one line |
|---|---|
| **mimetic desire** | you want it because a model wants it, not because of what it is |
| **model / mediator** | the person whose wanting you copied |
| **double bind** | the model saying "imitate me" and "do not take it" at once |
| **doubles** | rivals converging until the difference between them fails |
| **sacrificial crisis** | a whole society's distinctions collapsing at once |
| **scapegoat mechanism** | all-against-everyone becoming all-against-one, and the peace that follows |
| **the four stereotypes** | crisis, crimes, marks, violence. The traces the mechanism leaves in a text |
| **sparagmos** | the tearing apart of a victim by a crowd |
| **the universal negative** | "in mythology no dissenting voice is ever heard" |

### From measurement

| term | one line |
|---|---|
| **corpus** | the texts you actually scored |
| **population** | everything you want the claim to be about |
| **sampling frame** | the list you could actually draw from. The gap nobody examines |
| **lexicon / bucket** | a word-list standing for one concept |
| **surface form** | an exact spelling counted, with no stemming |
| **rate per thousand** | hits divided by words, times 1000. Discards position entirely |
| **SUM** | the four buckets added. Accumulation |
| **MIN** | the smallest of the four. **Conjunction.** The algebraic form of "together" |
| **pre-registration** | predictions committed before scoring, with a timestamp somebody else can check |
| **falsifier** | a number fixed in advance that declares the round unreliable if crossed |
| **null** | fake data like yours in every way except the thing you are testing |
| **permutation / permutation test** | build the null by shuffling, thousands of times, and count |
| **p-value** | probability of a result this extreme **if nothing were going on** |
| **z** | how many typical wobbles from nothing your result sits |
| **power** | the chance of finding an effect that is really there |
| **sensitivity analysis** | rerunning with one choice changed, declared in advance |
| **denominator** | the number of chances there were. Without it a count means nothing |
| **contamination disclosure** | writing down what you already knew, before you look |

>> **A claim nobody can check is a claim nobody should believe, including from
>> me.** Put the repository on screen and let people go and look.

## Citation tiers, applied to this series

The project this series describes tiers its own sources. **Use the same rule on
camera.**

| tier | meaning |
|---|---|
| **V** | source fetched, citation checked, quote verified against the original |
| **K** | the argument and the person are real, the citation is unverified. **A lead, not a fact** |

**Never attribute a precise claim to a named scholar from memory.** The project
caught itself doing exactly that twice, and both times the memory was wrong.
