# Filming Doc Spec — Building the Rnote Board

Instructions for Claude. When Abdel asks for a filming doc, read this first, then build to it.

Output is a PDF he imports into Rnote and annotates live while filming. One PDF per topic. It is not a script and not a summary. It is a board he regurgitates from.

---

## 1. The density rule, which is the whole thing

**Wrong:** a nearly empty page with two words and blank space. He gets to the page, there is nothing on it, and he stalls.

**Right:** a dense page he can retrieve an entire beat from at a glance.

The distinction is not how much is on the page. It is **fragment versus sentence**.

Everything he will say out loud goes on the page. Every citation, every Greek or Hebrew word, every date, every list, every pairing, every number. If he would have to recall it mid-sentence, it is on the board.

What does not go on the page:

- Connective prose. The sentences that join two facts.
- The conclusion. The thing the two facts prove.
- Anything in paragraph form.

Look at `James 2 part 2 - Filming Script v2` test one. The argument is two lists side by side. James's works are 2:16 clothe and feed, 2:25 Rahab, 1:27 orphans and widows. Paul's works of the law are circumcision, food, sabbath, calendar. Put both columns on the board complete. Do not put "so they mean different things by the same word" on the board. He says that. The columns being adjacent is what lets him say it well.

That is the pattern everywhere. **Arrange the evidence so the conclusion is obvious to him and unstated on the page.**

---

## 2. The two-document contract

**Research doc** teaches him the topic. Prose, cited, long, dense. Read cold to get confused, then organised. Lives in `<Series>/Research/`.

**Filming doc** is the board. Dense fragments, spatial, no prose. Lives in `<Series>/Filming/`.

What the research doc owes the board:

- The thesis in one sentence, stated explicitly
- Every number, date, Greek or Hebrew form, and citation
- At least one concrete anecdote per beat
- The objection in its strongest form, written as an opponent would say it
- The pairings and lists that will become columns

If any are missing, say so before building the board rather than inventing them.

## 3. Persuasion at the word level

Studied from Hormozi and Morgan. These are the moves that make a listener concede rather than merely follow. Most of them Abdel already does. The board's job is to leave room for them.

**Refuse the softening out loud.** "Now the problem, and I am not going to soften it, because everybody softens it and you can always tell." This is the strongest device in the v2 script. It discredits the comfortable answer before anyone offers it, so the viewer cannot retreat there. Board cue: mark the difficulty page with the objection at full strength, never a hedged version.

**Restate at lower abstraction so it lands as discovery.** "A body without a spirit is not a person doing badly. It is a corpse." Hormozi: "That's literally what a business is." Take a thing the viewer thinks they understand, say it one level more concretely, and the restatement feels like being shown something.

**Absurd boundary case to break a category.** Hormozi on "it's not worth my time": "It is worth your time if I give you a trillion dollars." Push to the extreme where the viewer must concede, then walk back. This dissolves an objection rather than answering it.

**Number instead of adjective.** Never "close together," always "three lines apart." Never "much later," always "thirty years later." Never "a lot," always "$45,000 a month." Numbers make an argument feel like engineering.

**Name the mechanism.** `Nekra`. `Prosōpolēmpsia`. `Sales latency`. A named thing is portable. The viewer leaves with a token they can repeat, and repeating it re-runs your argument for you.

**Voice the objection in their idiom, not yours.** Morgan: "it's still just not worth my time, bro." Using the viewer's own register proves you have heard it. On the board, write the objection in the viewer's words, in quotes.

**Lower yourself before the claim.** Morgan opens by calling his sixteen-year-old self an idiot. Abdel's equivalent is already in the v2 landing: "it is the thing that has bothered me all week." Status lowered, so the claim does not read as lecturing.

**Two columns beat one paragraph.** Every time a distinction is the point, build it as adjacency. Abraham and Rahab. James's works and Paul's works. Genesis 15 and Genesis 22. The eye does the argument before the mouth does.

## 4. The Girardian layer, and the line through it

He asked for the mechanism that makes a viewer feel desire. It is real, it is in both those channels, and it has an honest form and a dishonest one. The line matters more here than anywhere else, because this channel's subject is the mechanism itself.

**The mechanics as those videos run them:**

- *Model and obstacle.* Morgan displays the Dubai palm, the mansions, the forty million, then says he now prefers to sit like a goblin in a flat in London. Object shown, then partially withdrawn. The viewer wants what he has and also wants what he wants.
- *Manufacture the lack, then sell the sedative.* "You've sat here fifteen minutes, this is a sign you really want this, and the reason is it sedates some pain you might not be aware of." Name a pain, confirm it, position the product as its resolution.
- *Rivalry framing.* "Everyone's broke, everyone's struggling." The viewer is set against a mass so that distinction itself becomes the object of desire.
- *Mediated scarcity.* "It's a 2.5 billion dollar company, it's out of my control." A third party controls availability, so wanting it feels urgent rather than chosen.

**The line.** Hormozi states the honest version at 13:46 of the first transcript: "if you can articulate someone's problem better than they can, they will inherently believe that you can solve it." Naming a desire or a fear the viewer already carries is diagnosis. Installing one they did not arrive with is manufacture.

**The test, to apply per beat:** would this be true and worth saying to someone who will never subscribe, never buy, never come back? If yes, it is diagnosis. If it only functions as pressure toward an action, it is manufacture, and on this channel it is self-refuting, because the content would be performing the mechanism it exists to expose.

The v2 landing passes cleanly. "You cannot detect the demon's faith by checking your beliefs" names a real anxiety with precision and offers no relief for sale. Build toward that, and use the first four mechanics only in their diagnostic form.

## 5. Page layout

Rnote annotation over an imported PDF. Build the PDF to be drawn on.

- **One beat per page.** Page turn equals beat change. Camera framing stays stable, he never hunts.
- **Header band:** the beat name, two to five words, 36pt or larger. This is what he underlines as he opens the beat.
- **Body: the evidence, at full density.** Fragments, columns, verse pairs, term glosses, lists. Sized so the whole beat is retrievable in one glance. This is the part my first draft got wrong by leaving it empty.
- **One deliberate drawing zone per page.** A bounded empty area, roughly a quarter of the page, where the live diagram gets built. Label it with a small grey cue describing what he is building. The rest of the page is dense.
- **Foot strip:** the citations and numbers he must not recall from memory.
- **No paragraphs anywhere.** A full sentence outside a scripture quotation means the spec has been violated.

The drawing zone is what keeps his hand moving, which is what keeps him fluent. The dense body is what keeps him from stalling. Both are required. Empty pages and prose pages fail in opposite directions.

## 6. Mapping the six moves

The spine in [[Homily Format - The Video Spine]] stays authoritative. The board serves it.

| Move | Pages | Board treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The address | 0 | Camera only. Never put the hook on a page. |
| 2. The text | 1 | Passage printed whole, wide margins, no annotation until after the read. |
| 3. The difficulty | 1 | Both sides at full strength, quoted, adjacent. Objection in the viewer's words. |
| 4. The turn | 1 | The single move. Densest page in the doc. |
| 5. The pressure | 3 | One page per test. Each is usually a two-column adjacency. |
| 6. The landing | 1 | Sparse on purpose, the only page that is. The hand stops. |

Eight to nine pages standard. Over twelve means it is two episodes.

---

## 7. Hard rules

**Put every fact on the page, never the conclusion.** The governing rule. He supplies the joins and the verdict live. If the page states the insight, he has nothing to discover on camera and the delivery flattens.

**No prose.** Fragments and quotations only.

**Do not pre-draw the diagram.** Cue it, leave the zone empty.

**Scripture is NIV.**

**No em-dashes, no ellipses, no dramatic colons.** Anything spoken or shown.

**Nothing recalled from memory.** Every citation, date, and Greek form is visible.

```
  body:    JAMES, works                 PAUL, works of the law
           2:16  clothe, feed           circumcision
           2:25  Rahab hides spies      food laws
           1:27  orphans, widows        sabbath
                                        calendar
           every one a mercy            every one a boundary marker
           done to a body               between Jew and Gentile

  zone:    [empty, bottom band]  cue: "two circles, then the gap between"

  foot:    2:16 | 2:25 | 1:27
```

```
PAGE 8 — PRESSURE, TEST THREE

  header:  THE SAME VERSE, TWO DAYS

  body:    Gen 15:6  "Abraham believed God and it was
                      credited to him as righteousness"

           PAUL reads it where it sits      JAMES reads it forward
           Genesis 15, the stars            Genesis 22, the knife
           before Isaac                     thirty years later
           before circumcision              Romans 4, the timing

           2:22-23  "made complete"   "was fulfilled"

  zone:    [empty, right third]  cue: "one timeline, mark 15 and 22"

```

Note what is absent from all three. Page 4 does not say the Paul conflict dissolves. Page 6 does not say they mean different things. Page 8 does not say Paul freezes the frame and James runs the tape. Those are the best lines in the script and he says them. The board only puts the evidence at the right distance apart so that saying them is easy.
