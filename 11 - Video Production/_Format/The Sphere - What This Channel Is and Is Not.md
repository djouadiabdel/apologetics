# The Sphere

Sits above [[The Lens System - One Thesis Many Episodes]], which generates episodes, and above [[Homily Format - The Video Spine]], which structures one. This file decides what is allowed to exist at all.

---

## The claim the whole channel argues

**Before you ask how big the effect is, ask what the number is measuring.**

That's it. Every episode enters it through a different door. A viewer who watches three videos should have absorbed the same claim three ways without noticing it was the same claim.

It is the surface form of [[Person vs Nature — A Learning Challenge|the person-vs-nature thesis]]: a score is a statement about a nature, applied to a person, and the channel asks whether the statement survives inspection. **The thesis stays underneath. It is never the topic.** The day it becomes the topic, the niche is gone and this becomes another philosophy channel.

---

## Who it is for

**Internes, PADHUE, and medical or psychology students who have to read research or produce a thesis.** In French first.

This was not a marketing choice. The CNOM charte for *médecins créateurs de contenu* (Jan 2025) forbids personal and commercial promotion, and patient-facing content under a French medical identity with a foreign diploma is a real exposure. Professional education sits outside that. **The legal boundary and the good niche turn out to be the same line**, which means compliance costs nothing here.

The one-sentence differentiation, unchanged: *someone who can compute the pooled estimate and argue about what it is an estimate of, and do both in French.* There is almost no accessible French-language psychometrics content, and a large French-speaking audience currently assembling it out of English blog posts.

---

## In

Anything where a number in psychiatry is **made, moved, or pooled**.

- How a construct becomes an item, an item becomes a score, a score becomes an endpoint.
- Rating scales and what they actually do. Diagnostic criteria as sets.
- Effect sizes, pooling, heterogeneity, small-study effects, quality appraisal.
- Measurement invariance, DIF, factor structure, reliability.
- Live teardowns of one published paper, with the data and the code on screen.

The test for any idea: **can it be demonstrated, on screen, from something public?** If it can only be asserted, it does not belong here.

---

## Out, and this half matters more

- **Anything patient-facing.** No "signs you have X", no advice, no case material. Not a soft preference, it is the compliance line.
- **No verdicts on whether a treatment works.** Dismantling how an estimate was built is the work. Concluding "antidepressants don't work" is a clinical claim, it walks straight back into the exposure this channel was designed to avoid, and it is exactly the trap the existing critical-psychiatry channels fall into.
- **No theology.** Set down as channel content, see [[project-james-video-series]]. Keep it as private work.
- **No philosophy episodes.** The thesis is subtext.
- **No news reaction, no drama, no "psychiatry is broken" positioning.**
- **The forbidden move from the lens file stays forbidden:** "you can't argue with this." Also no appeals to AI as an oracle.

> [!important] The line that separates this channel from every other one in the space
> **They conclude. This one verifies.**
> Everyone doing critical psychiatry on YouTube arrives at a verdict they held before they started. The credential here is the opposite habit, and it is already on the record: two falsifiable instruments that were allowed to fail and whose failures were published ([[project-girard-myth-detector]] round 3 failed 1 of 5; the interlock's F4 and G1 both fired), plus the standing rule never to tune the instrument or the sample to make it pass.
>
> That is the cost-as-credential move and almost nobody in this niche can make it. Say it out loud early.

## The three tiers ideas sort into

Every idea goes in exactly one. If it fits none, it is out of the sphere.

**Tier 1 — the spine series.** The chain from one patient to one pooled number, in order. Finite, roughly ten episodes, and it is the curriculum. A new viewer can enter at episode one and the whole channel becomes legible. Each link is one episode:
one patient → a construct → items → a score → a scale's properties → a group difference → an effect size → a study → a pool of studies → one number in a guideline.

**Tier 2 — teardowns.** One published paper, taken apart on screen. Renewable forever, cheap to make, keeps the channel alive between spine episodes. This is the Recency lens.

**Tier 3 — the break log.** Wherever his own comprehension breaks, per [[The Lens System - One Thesis Many Episodes]]. Never empties, and the entries arrive pre-sharpened because a break already contains its own difficulty.

---

## Unit

10 minutes maximum. **One idea, worked over and over until it is definitive.** Not a topic slate. Something every ~3 days, scheduled on non-gym days.

10 min at speaking pace is ~1,400 words, which is exactly the length [[Homily Format - The Video Spine]] is built for. The six moves survive with one substitution: **"the text" becomes "the study."**

Packaging still runs through [[Title and Packaging System]], with the fourth rule adapted: *keep the scripture reference in* becomes **keep the citation in.** The citation is the credential in this corpus.

## The keystone topic, decided 11 Aug 2026

**Measurement invariance in instruments developed in English and used in French.**

Chosen because it is a real and silent failure (back-translation checks semantics, not measurement properties), and because understanding it forces latent variables, sum scores, ordinal versus interval, alpha, and DIF in the order where each is needed for the next. One topic, five foundations, no extra study.

First thing to verify before filming: the actual DIF literature on French versions of the common scales. **If the effect is smaller than the folklore says, that is the better video and the honest one.**
