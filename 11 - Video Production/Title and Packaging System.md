# Title and Packaging System

Built 2026-07-21 while packaging James 2 part 3. The winning title, **"Neither Man in James 2 Has a Name. That's the Whole Point."**, scored **92** on vidIQ. Everything before it scored 70 to 82 and was wrong in a way the score did not catch.

---

## The four rules

**1. A concrete object in the title.**
A ship detail, a chronological marker, an insult, an archaeological find, a missing name, a Greek word. Never an abstraction. "The Mask You Wear Before God" is mood. "Neither Man in James 2 Has a Name" is a fact you can check in ten seconds by opening the text.

**2. Small thing proves big thing.**
The grammar is *this tiny specific detail* establishes *this large claim*. "This Weird Ship Detail Shows Acts Is Historically Accurate." The gap between the size of the detail and the size of the conclusion is the hook.

**3. Keep the scripture reference in the title.**
John 2, Isaiah 29, Daniel 2, Acts 15, James 2. It does not cost the hook and it earns the search. Moving the reference out to "make it psychological" was tested and was wrong.

**4. Nobody-noticed framing.**
Secret moment, hidden connection, skeptics can't explain, nobody noticed, stumped me at first. The promise is that the viewer is about to see something they have read past.

Length runs about 6 to 11 words.

## What failed, and why the score did not catch it

Scored candidates for the same video, in the order they were tried:

| Title | vidIQ | Why it was wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Why "Love Your Neighbor" Was Never Going to Work | 70 | Abstract. No object. |
| The Sin That Makes a Man Into a Thing | 71 | Abstract. Sounds like a sermon. |
| The Man With No Face | 77 | Closer, has an image, but no scripture ref and no claim |
| Prosopolempsia: The Sin Nobody Names | 82 | Unreadable first word |
| The Mask You Wear Before God | 82 | Generic devotional. Could sit on any channel. |
| God Has Favorites. That Is Not Favoritism. | 81 | Duplicated part 1's published title |
| **Neither Man in James 2 Has a Name. That's the Whole Point.** | **92** | Object, scripture ref, nobody-noticed, checkable |

**The lesson: a 5-point vidIQ gap is noise and the score cannot see a duplicate, a mood-title, or an unreadable word.** Use the four rules to generate, use vidIQ only to rank what already passes them. Do not let an 81 talk you into a title that repeats the previous episode.

---

## Channel-specific notes

- Published James 2 part 1 is titled "The Favoritism God Commanded". It underperforms this system. Do not use it as the shape to match.
- Best historical performers on the channel are second-person psychological titles ("You're Not Addicted to Them. You're Addicted to the Feeling.", "You Have Never Wanted Something First"). That register works for the mimetic-desire material. It does **not** override rule 3 for scripture-exposition videos. Both can be true: the James videos want the object plus the reference, the desire videos want the second person.
- Thumbnails in play for James 2 are icon-style, painted, single concrete image with two or three words in the corner ("NOT FAIR", "A THING"). The thumbnail carries the image, the title carries the checkable fact. Do not make them say the same thing.
