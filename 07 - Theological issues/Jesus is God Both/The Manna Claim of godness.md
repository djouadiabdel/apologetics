### 1. Jesus says it plainly and repeatedly in John 6

|Verse|Exact Words of Jesus|What He Claims|
|---|---|---|
|John 6:38|“**I have come down from heaven**”|Personal descent from heaven|
|John 6:41|“**I am the bread that came down from heaven**”|He is the bread that descended|
|John 6:50|“This is the bread that **comes down from heaven**”|The bread = Himself|
|John 6:51|“**I am the living bread that came down from heaven**”|Explicit self-identification|
|John 6:58|“This is the bread that **came down from heaven**”|Repeated for emphasis|

He says this **six times** in one chapter. This is not poetic language. It is a deliberate claim.

### 2. The Jewish crowd understood it as a claim of heavenly origin (not just “sent”)

**John 6:42**

> “Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? **How does he now say, ‘I have come down from heaven’**?”

They were not shocked that He said “God sent me.” They were shocked that He claimed **He personally came down from heaven** while having human parents. This was the offense.

### 3. Jesus adds even stronger proof of pre-existence

**John 6:62**

> “What if you were to see the **Son of Man ascending to where he was before**?”

This is concrete:

- He existed **before** He came to earth.
- He will return to that same place. This is not the language of a prophet or messenger. This is the language of someone who **left heaven** and will **return to heaven**.

### 4. Supporting verses from the rest of John’s Gospel (same author, same theme)

**John 3:13**

> “No one has ascended into heaven except **he who descended from heaven**, the Son of Man.”

**John 8:23**

> “You are from below; **I am from above**. You are of this world; **I am not of this world**.”

**John 17:5** (Jesus praying to the Father)

> “Father, glorify me in your own presence with the **glory that I had with you before the world existed**.”

### Bottom Line – Concrete Proof

Jesus does **not** say: “I was sent by the Father with bread from heaven.”

Jesus says: **“I Myself came down from heaven. I am the bread that came down from heaven. I existed before I came here. I will return to where I was before.”**

This is why the Jews accused Him of blasphemy and tried to kill Him (John 5:18, 8:59, 10:31-33). They understood exactly what He was claiming.
