## CLOSING STATEMENT — New Testament Version

_"Let me show you what the argument actually was._

_It started with one verse you agreed to: Psalm 83:18. Only Yahweh is the Most High. One name. One being. You agreed._

_Isaiah 40 said a voice would prepare for Yahweh's personal arrival. Three independent Gospel writers — Mark, Matthew, and Luke — all independently identified John as that voice. John himself identified Jesus as the one he prepared for. Therefore Isaiah's Yahweh arrived as Jesus._

_Malachi 3:1 said Yahweh would send a forerunner before me — Yahweh speaking in first person about his own arrival at his own temple. Jesus quoted that verse and changed every first-person divine pronoun to second person, pointing at himself. Jesus took the "me" that Yahweh used to describe his own arrival and replaced it with "you" addressed to his own face. That is not grammatical adaptation. That is a divine claim in the structure of a pronoun._

_Malachi 4:5 said Elijah would come before the Day of Yahweh — Yahweh's personal arrival. Jesus said John was Elijah. John came before Jesus. The Day of Yahweh arrived as Jesus._

_Paul quoted Isaiah 45 — where Yahweh swears by himself that every knee bows to him alone — and applied it word for word to Jesus. Paul knew Isaiah 42:8: Yahweh gives his glory to no other. Paul applied Yahweh's exclusive glory to Jesus anyway. Paul was not confused. Paul was a trained Torah scholar. He knew exactly what he was saying: Jesus holds the position that Isaiah 45 reserves for Yahweh alone._

_Isaiah 6 — John 12:41 tells us explicitly that when Isaiah saw Yahweh of hosts enthroned in the Temple, he was seeing Jesus's glory._

_And then the Torah itself — before any of this — already embedded the Son, the eternal origins, the piercing, the divine name I AM at the burning bush, the two Yahwehs in Genesis, the child named God with us, the Messianic king called Mighty God._

_This was never just a New Testament theology. The New Testament is the announcement that the person the Torah was describing has arrived, and his name is Jesus._

_You came here to debate whether Jesus is God. The argument is over — not because I said so, but because Malachi said it, Isaiah said it, Zechariah said it, Daniel said it, David said it, and Moses embedded it at the burning bush._

_The question is not whether the scriptures teach this. They do. The question is what you are going to do with what you just read."_
