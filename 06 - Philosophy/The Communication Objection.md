# The Communication Objection

**Folder:** 06 - Philosophy
**Status:** stated, partly answered, and carrying a cost that has not been paid
**Links:** [[01 - The Whole Argument]] · [[Naturalism as a Presupposition — Not a Conclusion]] · [[00 - Le Plan]] · [[Vindicatory Miracles — The Catholic Case and Its Missing Control]]

Almost every objection in folders 01 to 08 is aimed at whether Christianity is true. This one is aimed somewhere else, and that is why it goes in a file of its own. It grants the truth of Christianity for the sake of argument and asks whether it can be identified. It is the only objection in this vault whose third premise is my own current condition.

---

## The argument, at full strength

Do not weaken it. The weak version is a complaint about denominations and it is not worth writing down.

1. God strongly wants sincere seekers to know who he is and what he wants from them.
2. God is fully able to communicate that clearly enough to be understood.
3. Sincere followers of God remain deeply divided about what he has revealed, and condemn one another for getting it wrong.

The third is not a premise. It is an observation, and it is the strongest part.

And the evidence is not the internet. It is the record. Servetus arrested in Geneva, convicted, executed in 1553 with Calvin supporting the condemnation, over the Trinity. Anabaptists imprisoned, tortured and killed by Catholics and Protestants alike, over infant baptism, which most people now file under secondary. Dutch Calvinists suppressing Arminians after Dort. Kirk Cameron in 2025 finding conditionalism persuasive and being called a false prophet by men who had known him forty years, and the most vicious attacks coming from his own side.

Then the part that lands hardest, because it is not the objector's standard.

> that all of them may be one, Father, just as you are in me and I am in you. May they also be in us so that the world may believe that you have sent me.
> John 17:21

Unity is offered there as the evidence. Not as a nice outcome. As the thing that makes the mission credible to outsiders. So an outsider looking at the fragmentation and saying the evidence is not present is not applying a hostile standard. He is applying the one in the text.

## The answers that do not work

**Scientists disagree too.** The comparison fails on the thing that matters. A rock is not trying to be understood and cannot correct a misreading. The whole claim under dispute is that this is not a rock. If discovering God's will works the way studying a silent object works, then disagreement is exactly what you should expect, and the theology has to be revised to say so.

**God is too vast for one tradition.** It is not vast questions that split. Infants are baptised or they are not. And the claim that diversity is intended is itself one disputed position among the others, held by some Protestants and denied by Rome, so it cannot referee.

**God gave a teaching authority.** This is the serious answer and it moves the problem one step without dissolving it. Grant a magisterium. You still have to identify it, and you identify it by reading Matthew 16, weighing apostolic succession, assessing church history, and choosing. Luther did exactly that work and concluded the papacy was the thing opposing Christ. Wes Huff does the same work and calls the Petrine reading a misreading. Sincere, trained, and opposite.

**The New Testament predicted division.** Prediction is not explanation. Acts 20 warning about savage wolves tells you the outcome was expected. It does not tell you why a living God permits it when he could correct it.

**Calvinism.** This one genuinely denies premise 1, and it is the only answer that does. God does not intend everyone to arrive. But the cost is heavy and it is at the other end. If the illumination was withheld, unbelief is not rebellion, it is the predictable result of not receiving what someone else received, and the moral charge attached to it collapses. Trent Horn calls the position heresy. So the escape is another disputed interpretation, which is the pattern again.

## The two answers that hold, and what they buy

Both are small. Neither dissolves the observation.

**One. The load-bearing word is *clearly*, and it is not defined.**

The argument needs a standard for clear enough, and the implicit one is convergence. Real divine communication would produce agreement among sincere people. That standard is never argued for. It is assumed, and it is a judgement about how a God would behave if there were one.

Which puts it in a category this vault already mapped. [[Naturalism as a Presupposition — Not a Conclusion]] says a prior is not a finding, and that no quantity of evidence moves one. This objection is a prior about divine pedagogy wearing the clothes of an observation about churches. Naming that does not refute it.

It reclassifies it. It stops being an evidential argument and becomes a plausibility judgement, which is the same category as the argument from suffering, and the honest concession in that file applies here unchanged, against me. **I cannot argue anyone out of it, and my own prior is doing work in the other direction.** Symmetric or worthless.

**Two. The regress is universal, and it defeats the conclusion too.**

*You need fallible judgement to identify the infallible authority.* True. It is also true of every authority anyone has ever had. The objector says it himself. He spent years on the Bible, theology and church history and reached a judgement, and then asks whose judgement he should trust instead. That is the same regress arriving at his own door and he does not treat it as disqualifying there.

So the regress cannot be what defeats the Christian claim, because if it defeats anything it defeats every position including his. What it establishes is that nobody gets a self-certifying starting point. That is a real result and it is not a Christian problem.

Note what neither answer touched. Servetus is still dead.

## The cost, which is mine and is not paid

Premise 3 is my own condition. [[00 - Le Plan]] exists because I do not know whether I belong in Rome, Constantinople or Alexandria, and the plan says the posture out loud. *Je pèse réellement la question.*

So this objection cannot be filed as answered. It can only be answered by running Module 8 and reaching a verdict that did not require the criteria to move.

The plan already fixed the discipline for exactly this, before this objection was in the vault.

> Bifurcation par bifurcation. Si tu te surprends à renégocier un critère, arrête-toi et écris pourquoi. **Cette renégociation est la donnée.**

Which gives this file a falsifier rather than a conclusion.

**If Module 8 lands only after a criterion is renegotiated, that is evidence for the objection and it gets recorded as such in [[01 - The Whole Argument]] section 6.** Not filed away. Written down as a hit.

## The one thing that would actually answer it

The objection asks for a public signal that identifies which claimant speaks for God, checkable by someone who does not already believe the claimant.

That is a specific request and it is answerable in principle. Not by better exegesis of Matthew 16, since exegesis is the disputed instrument. By something the claimant can call in advance and an outsider can inspect afterwards.

Whether such a signal exists is [[Vindicatory Miracles — The Catholic Case and Its Missing Control]]. The two files are one hinge. This one says no signal has appeared. That one says the signal appeared, was photographed, and points at one address.

They cannot both be right, and neither has been tested here.
