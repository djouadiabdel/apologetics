# Reading Delta Log

Sister to [[french-delta-log]]. Same instrument, different domain. Method: [[Reading for the Argument]].

**What goes here:** the specific joints that collapsed. Not concepts you found hard.

**Standing prediction to test:** your losses are joints, not content. You keep every claim and drop the relation between two of them. Same signature as your French errors, where the content survives and the small connective gets silently substituted.

If after five texts the losses are mostly *content* rather than *joints*, the prediction is wrong and the method should be re-aimed. Log it either way.
