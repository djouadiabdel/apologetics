# French Reading Plan — 30 Books to C2

Built 8 Aug 2026. Supersedes nothing — it selects from [[French Literature 57 books]] and executes the reading half of [[FRENCH GOALS]]. Production runs in parallel via [[Pascal Reconstruction — French Production Method]].

## The premise

Comfortable unassisted reading needs **98% coverage** of the page ≈ **8,000–9,000 word families** (Nation). B2 sits at 4,000–5,000. Getting to C2 territory means roughly doubling the working vocabulary, and that takes **~2 million words of input**.

At C1 the words you don't know are low-frequency *by definition* — they appear once per 50,000 words or more. You cannot flashcard your way there, because any single word is too rare to justify the card. **The only way to meet a word that appears once per 50,000 words is to read 50,000 words.** That is the whole reason the answer is volume and not method.

## Commitment

**1 hour floor, 2 hour ceiling, daily.** Anything between counts.

| daily | finishes in |
|---|---|
| 1h | ~8 months |
| 1.5h | ~5.5 months |
| 2h | ~4 months |

Assumes ~150 wpm silent, climbing to 200+. **The floor is what carries this, not the ceiling.**

## The rule that matters

**Chase words, not book count.** Thirty novellas is 30 books and 1.1M words — hits the count, misses the threshold.

**Job split for translations:**
- **Translations = volume engine.** Vocabulary, automaticity. Asimov counts fully.
- **Originals = construction training.** Only originals carry French sentence architecture, which is the actual weak point.

## Running order

1. Finish Plath.
2. **Le Horla** — short, fast win.
3. **One Asimov** — 170k words of genuine pleasure early is how the habit survives week three.
4. Then alternate: one long translation for volume, one shorter original for construction.
5. **Le Rouge et le Noir** in month three, once silent speed has climbed.

Front-load light. The first four weeks are the expensive ones and that is exactly when people quit.

## Per-session protocol

1. **Silent first, audio second.** Silent is the rep; audio is the correction pass.
2. **One page read aloud, by me.** Forces a committed syntactic parse. Trains reading structure and production in the same rep.
3. **One long-sentence skeleton per chapter.** Find the main subject and main verb, strip the subordination. Thirty seconds.
4. **Keep 1.75x** on the audio pass.

## The metric

After a silent-only chapter, write **3 sentences in French** on what happened. *Then* listen, and log the misses tagged `silent-read`.

**Progress = the shrinking gap between silent and audio-assisted comprehension. Not "it felt easier."** If the tagged misses don't trend down over a few weeks, the protocol is wrong and gets changed, not defended.

## Caveats

Word counts are **approximate**, estimated from page counts and English originals where applicable. The 8,000–9,000 word-family threshold is solid research; the "2 million words" conversion is a well-founded rule of thumb, not a measured constant. Treat 30 books as the right order of magnitude, not a finish line to count down.
