# Bookstores DZ — French Buy List

Catalogues pulled 20 Aug 2026 from bigdealbookstore.com, bookshivedz.com and bookberries.lovable.app. French-language titles only. **Where a title is stocked by more than one site, only the cheapest listing is kept**, and the site name is on every line. Prices in DZD, every title is a direct product link.

Filtered against [[French Literature 57 books]] (both the 57-book plan and the 100 incontournables list inside it) and against the books you said you already own or have read. Nothing below is a duplicate of either.

## What the filter removed

Already yours, by your own word: *Pensées*, *Les Carnets du sous-sol*, *La Peste*, *L'Étranger*, and the Maupassant (*Le Horla*, *Bel-Ami*, *Une vie*, *Pierre et Jean*).

Already on your 57-book plan: *La Chute*, *Candide*, *Zadig*, *Germinal*, *Le Colonel Chabert*, *La Peau de chagrin*, *Carmen*, *Colomba*, and the Sagan, Nothomb, Sartre, Duras, Modiano, Vargas and Houellebecq blocks, none of which these three sites stock anyway.

Already on the 100 incontournables inside that same file: *Les Misérables*, *Le Comte de Monte-Cristo*, *Le Rouge et le Noir*, *Crime et Châtiment*, *Moby Dick*, *Dune*, *1984*, *L'Art de la guerre*.

Say the word if you want any of those put back. The plan list and the incontournables list are aspirations rather than possessions, so the argument for re-adding a few of them is real.

## Not stocked by any of the three

No Proust, Céline, Gide, Sartre, Duras, Yourcenar, Colette, Rimbaud, Verlaine, Modiano, Ernaux, Houellebecq, Perec, Giono, Malraux, Simenon, Sagan, Nothomb, Vargas or Mérimée. On the Algerian side, no Feraoun, Dib, Sansal or Kamel Daoud. Among the moralists, no La Rochefoucauld, La Bruyère or Chamfort.

Nothing Christian in French beyond *Le prieur de Bethléem* above, and nothing from the classical liberal tradition in French at all. Bastiat stays a free download: *La Loi*, *Ce qu'on voit et ce qu'on ne voit pas*, *Sophismes économiques*, at bastiat.org.
