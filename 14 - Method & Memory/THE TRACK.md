## WORKING RULES

- **Position, never a daily count.** A quota becomes a ceiling. Five and you're free is worse than no number at all.
- **Never hours.** Hours are the unit the "that's enough" signal can argue with.
- **When it says enough, you're at about 60%.** Do two thirds of that again, in units, not on a timer. The 60% came off your prose; it gets replaced with your real number once there are readings for it.
- **Conditions, not clock times.** Novel at peak boredom. Lossky in the morning. Camera in the evening. Mystères at night, PC already on, no light to turn on.
