> **Read this before making any apologetic claim in any medium.** Video script,
> essay, carousel, thread, DM, debate. Enforced by `~/.claude/skills/case-ledger/`,
> not by remembering.

`01 - The Whole Argument` says what kind of argument this is and audits the
instruments. It does not state the case, and says so in its own second paragraph.
This file states the case.

**Numbers here are pointers, not copies.** A published round is frozen, so its
number does not drift, but the claim it licenses can. Where a number appears, the
file that owns it is named. If the two disagree, the owning file wins.

**Every plank carries a tier**, using the V/K scale from
`~/Myths/interlock/DESIGN-RATIONALE.md` §11. **V** means the sources were fetched
and the citations checked here. **K** means the argument and its defenders are
real and the citations are unverified on this disk. A K plank may be believed and
may not be filmed. Merging the two would make this file lie about its own
strength.

## 0. The ceiling, which is not a disclaimer

No amount of evidence forces the conclusion. Once the facts are fixed, the
inference is dominated by the prior on whether an event like this can happen, and
no amount of source checking touches a prior. Earman removes the in-principle
blocker against testimony, which means the other person has to make a specific
case about these witnesses rather than a general case about testimony. That is
the only thing available on this side and it is worth having.

Owner: [[Naturalism as a Presupposition — Not a Conclusion]].

**So the target is narrow. Not that Christianity is true. That the case is
honestly built, that it was capable of failing, and that at named points it did
not.** Any section below that forgets this is wrong, and so is any script.

## 1. What I actually say when someone asks

Say this. Not a longer version of this.

> Ancient stories about a crowd killing somebody tend to be told from inside the
> crowd, and they speak with one voice. The earliest Christian material does not.
> Somebody in it keeps saying no. I measured that rather than asserting it, and it
> survived two nulls and a swap to a different translation. Alongside that, the
> canonical gospels are about eleven times more restrained than the apocryphal
> ones written about the same figure in the same period, which is the opposite of
> what uncontrolled legend does. And the earliest proclamation is written by a
> named man inside a window too short for legend, who says he persecuted the
> movement first and lists what it cost him. Underneath all of that there is a
> Hebrew text, centuries older, that already has a second figure beside God and
> already has him killed. And there is a modern body of evidence I have not
> examined yet that would either strengthen this a great deal or embarrass it.
> None of it proves anything. The prior decides the rest, mine included.

Five moves. The measured finding that is yours, the counted finding anyone can
redo, the early named witness, the older text underneath, and the open file. It
ends on the concession and the open file on purpose. That is the part nobody else
says and it is what makes the first four worth listening to.

## 2. The five planks, in the order they carry weight

### 2.1 The anthropological plank. Tier V. Mine, and the only one nobody else has.

**Claim.** Myth speaks about its victim with one voice. In the Gospels somebody in
the crowd keeps saying no. Division, not exoneration.

**Strength.** Round 6, phrases only, beats a phrase-aware null at 0 of 2000 draws,
p = 0.0005. Survives the translation swap, KJV p = 0.00064 against ASV p =
0.00030, the two sitting 20.4% apart against a 25% bound. Round 8, dissent
language sits closer to violence language than chance places it, U = 0.4171,
p = 0.0015.

**Owner.** `~/Myths/RESULTS-6.md`, `~/Myths/RESULTS-8.md`, [[Myths — Forensic Record]].

**Ceiling.** Co-location of two vocabularies. Not that the dissenting words belong
to a person separating from a crowd, not that the violence is collective, not that
this is Girard's mechanism rather than passion narratives putting speech and
killing in the same chapters for ordinary narrative reasons.

**Say this before anyone finds it.** It is corpus against corpus, not inside any
single text. The *Bacchae*, Girard's own showcase, failed three separate
pre-registered tests at p = 0.66, p = 0.888 and p = 0.414. John's passion came
back at p = 0.876. Gospel within-text pass rate was 0.0%. That is the strongest
evidence that the instrument reads its input rather than confirming it.

### 2.2 The textual plank. Tier V. Pure counting, so it needs nobody's goodwill.

**Claim.** The canonical gospels are about eleven times more restrained than the
apocryphal gospels written about the same figure in the same period. 0.22
embellishments per thousand words against 2.54, against a pre-registered line of
3x. Hard collisions run the other way, apocrypha 0.95 per thousand words and canon
below it.

**The examples that carry it.** A schoolmaster dies in one infancy gospel and
blushes in the other. A walking cross speaks in the Gospel of Peter and nowhere
else. Child Jesus kills a boy who bumps into him in the Infancy Gospel of Thomas,
while the entire canonical childhood is a twelve-year-old staying behind in a
temple and being told off by his mother.

**Owner.** `~/Myths/interlock/RESULTS.md`, predictions P2 and P3.

**What it does not buy.** Restraint is not accuracy. A careful liar is restrained
and a sober report of something that did not happen is still sober. It rules out
one mechanism, uncontrolled legendary growth, and leaves the rest open.

**The half that is disowned, and I disowned it.** P1 and P5, the fit ratios. I
logged the fits already knowing which ones the undesigned-coincidence literature
points at. P5 failed outright, canon 0.50 against a pre-declared 0.60. Never cite
the fit ratio. **This matters for §2.4 below and it is the reason that plank is
smaller than the literature it comes from.**

**The honest limit.** The denominator does not exist. Two hard contradictions out
of what? `interlock/DESIGN-RATIONALE.md` §15 says this is the top priority and
that until it exists the whole thing is a fact about a list. It is still a fact
about a list.

**Claim two, the resurrection remainder. Tier V except where noted.** 1
Corinthians 15:3-8, received and passed on, inside a letter of AD 53-55 and older
than the letter. Paul's own hand on having persecuted the movement, in three

### 2.4 The scriptural plank. Tier K. New, and narrowed to what a twist cannot reach.

The convergence argument is not what goes here. It is a conjunction, it has no
losing condition, and its flagship file advertises being unfalsifiable. What goes
here is your own instinct: **the verses where the alternative reading has a cost
you can name.** That cuts the folder down hard, and the survivors are these.

| verse | the claim | what the alternative reading costs | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daniel 7:13-14** | a second heavenly figure receives dominion and the service of all peoples | the Aramaic verb is *pelach*, and in Daniel it appears to be reserved for religious service to a deity. If that distribution holds, "serve him" here is worship, and the alternative reading has to explain a lexical exception | **strongest, and it is a counting claim** |
| **Zechariah 12:10** | YHWH is the speaker, and the text reads "they shall look to **me** whom they have pierced" | the escape is the *'elav* / "to him" variant, which exists. But the MT reads *'elai*, and it is the harder reading | strong, one textual variant |
| **Isaiah 9:6** | the child is called *el gibbor*, and Isaiah 10:21 uses that exact phrase of YHWH four verses of context later | the escape is that theophoric names describe the God who names, not the bearer. Real, and it has to explain why the same phrase means God in the same author's next breath | strong, one grammatical escape with a cost |
| **Psalm 110:1** | "the LORD said to my lord" | **this one does not survive your own cut.** The word is *adoni*, not *Adonai*, and *adoni* is the ordinary address to a human superior across the Hebrew Bible. The twist is standard, grammatically available, and costs the other side nothing | **drop it as a hard-core verse** |
| Micah 5:2, Proverbs 30:4 | pre-existence, a son of God | *olam* can mean "long ago" rather than eternity. *Ben* is idiomatic | weak, do not lead with them |

**The instrument this suggests, and it is the one worth building.** The Daniel
7:14 point is not an interpretation. It is a distributional claim about one
Aramaic verb in one corpus, and distributional claims are exactly the shape you
have already run twice. Extract every occurrence of *pelach* in the Aramaic
chapters of Daniel, code each for whether the object is divine, and see whether
7:14 and 7:27 sit inside or outside the pattern. Pre-register the falsifier: **if
*pelach* takes non-divine objects anywhere in Daniel, the verse loses its force
and this row comes out of the table.** That is a week of work and it can lose.

**The modern scholarly form of this plank.** Brant Pitre, *Jesus and Divine
Christology* (Eerdmans, 2024), argues the Synoptics already show a more-than-human
Jesus, with Mark 14:62 fusing Daniel 7:13 and Psalm 110:1. Richard Bauckham's
divine-identity framework is what makes the Daniel 7 material carry weight, Larry
Hurtado's argument runs on devotional practice mutating within twenty years, and
Daniel Boyarin, a Jewish scholar, argues the divine Son of Man was already
available inside Judaism. **None of those three is cited anywhere in this vault.
Read them before this plank goes near a camera.**

**Ceiling.** These establish that a divine-messiah reading is available in the
Hebrew text and that its denial has a price. They do not establish that it is the
only reading, and Pitre's own case is explicitly cumulative, which is the shape
§5.1 of [[Vindicatory Miracles — The Catholic Case and Its Missing Control]]
refuses.

**Status.** No instrument, no null, nothing run. `~/Myths/prophecy/` holds the
Daniel 9 pre-registration, deadline 30 September 2026. Predicted in writing before
the run: **Jesus fails the Maimonides criteria.**

### 2.5 The maximalist plank. Tier K, and it conflicts with 2.2.

**Claim.** The gospels and Acts are reliable reportage, not a minimal core of
scholar-conceded facts. If that holds, the whole content is available rather than
the amputated remainder in 2.3.

**The engine that survives your own instruments.** Onomastics. The relative
frequency of personal names in the gospels and Acts is claimed to match the
frequency distribution of Palestinian Jewish names in Tal Ilan's *Lexicon*, and
not to match the diaspora distribution, with the disambiguation patterns following
the same logic that common names get qualifiers and rare ones do not. **That is a
frequency claim about a name corpus. It is countable, it has a clean falsifier,
and it is exactly your shape.** Alongside it, the incidental accuracy of Acts on
titles, ports, winds and local administration, which Colin Hemer catalogued.

**The engine you have already disowned, and this is the conflict.** The other half
of maximalism runs on **undesigned coincidences.** That is the same instrument
whose numerator you threw out in interlock run 1, on the stated grounds that you
logged the fits already knowing which ones the apologetic literature points at.
**You cannot take the maximalist case wholesale and keep that disavowal.** Take
onomastics and Hemer, which are counts anyone can redo. Leave undesigned
coincidences where you put it.

**What already belongs to you here.** The 11x restraint gap in 2.2 is a
maximalist-adjacent finding, it is yours, and it is tier V. It is the strongest
maximalist thing in this vault and it did not come from the maximalist literature.

**Ceiling.** Reliability is not inspiration and not inerrancy. A reporter who gets
the names, the winds and the harbour officials right is a reporter who was there
or talked to people who were, and that is all it buys.

### 2.6 The miracle plank. Tier K throughout. Believed, not filmable.

**Claim.** There is a public, checkable signal, called in advance or inspected
afterwards by hostile parties, attached to a claimant. Not that miracles happen.
That narrower and more aggressive thing.

**The half that needs nobody's charity, and it is the only half that goes public.**
A mercury thermometer reads 52 C or it does not. A blinded laboratory in Białystok
returns cardiac muscle or it returns bacteria. A social security fraud
investigation closes or it does not. X-rays exist before and after or they do not.
This is the same class of fact as *a schoolmaster dies in one infancy gospel and
blushes in the other.*

**The half that is untrustworthy on principle.** Every doctrinal reading. What a
private revelation vindicates, what the odor of sanctity signifies. That is where
a knowing thumb rests, which is precisely why the interlock fit ratio was disowned.

**The three gates, and none of them is open.**

1. **Tier K throughout. Not one primary source has been opened on this disk.**
   Every case in the corpus comes from one video and its resource document. Until
   the medical reports, laboratory analyses and depositions are in hand and cited
   by page, this plank does not go near a camera or the site.
2. **The engine is a conjunction.** *Multiply the improbabilities* cannot be lost
   by any counterexample, because each one is re-priced as another coincidence.
   That is the accusation against Girard word for word. **What case, if it came
   back clean and non-Catholic, would produce the words *this one does not fit*?**
   Until that is answered, this is not evidence in the sense claimed for it.
3. **F2 is unrun.** The matched non-Catholic apparition corpus. The proposal is
   written, the instrument is on the disk, and nobody has run it.

**And it does not point at one address.** Zeitoun happened over a Coptic church
and was authenticated by Kyrillos VI; its payload is Marian devotion, which Rome
and Alexandria both hold. Soufanieh is a unity claim delivered to a mixed
household with the Syriac Orthodox patriarch among the eyewitnesses. Two of the
four exhibits people summarise this corpus with do not discriminate between Rome
and Alexandria at all.

**Owner.** [[Vindicatory Miracles — The Catholic Case and Its Missing Control]],
especially §5 and §6.

**The round that would change this.** `interlock/build_run.py` already emits
witnesses as sealed packets under opaque codes and scores fits against collisions.
The Fatima depositions and Zeitoun testimonies are the same data type. The
Alburitel detail, witnesses fifteen kilometres away facing the wrong direction
describing a three-fold descent, is an undesigned coincidence in the exact
technical sense already defined. **F1 on that run has already fired: you can write
that detail out from memory, so you are not a blind coder.** Someone else codes,
or it is coded from a fixed rule applied before the corpus is known.

  list and gets presented as one.

  audit, it has drifted.
