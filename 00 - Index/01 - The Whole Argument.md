# The Whole Argument

**Folder:** 00 - Index
**Status:** first assembly, 29 August 2026
**Links:** [[00 - Master Index]] (folders 01 to 08, stale) · [[00 - Start Here]] (the five tracks)

Fifteen folders, six hundred files, and no document that says how they fit. This is that document. It is not a summary of the arguments. It is a claim about what kind of argument they are, which planks carry weight, which ones have been tested, and where the thing can be attacked.

Two pieces arrived today that were not in the vault before. An objection about which church, and a body of miracle evidence pointed at one address. They go in at the same joint, and that joint is the one this vault never built. Sections 6 and 7.

---

## 1. What it is not

It is not a proof and it does not accumulate into one.

[[Naturalism as a Presupposition — Not a Conclusion]] settles that, and it is the ceiling over everything below it. Two people holding the same facts and different priors both move a little, in the same direction, and finish on opposite sides. The sentence in that file was written against my own side and it stands. Anyone who says the case is overwhelming has not understood the shape of the problem.

So the target is narrower than the title of this file suggests. Not that Christianity is true. That the case is honestly built, that it was capable of failing, and that at named points it did not.

Everything below inherits that ceiling. Where a section forgets it, the section is wrong.

## 2. One test, five corpora

Every plank in this vault runs the same operation on different material. Say the operation once and the folders stop being separate subjects.

Take a record. Ask whether it behaves like a record, or like something people wanted.

| plank | corpus | the question |
|---|---|---|
| Girard | myth, tragedy, gospel | a myth is what a crowd says about itself afterwards. Does this text tell the same event from underneath? |
| Interlock | canon and apocrypha | do these accounts complete each other the way independent reports do, or collide the way legends do? |
| Contradiction count | the hostile docket | when someone alleges a clash, is it one, and what does the rescue cost? |
| Prophecy | the Tanakh | was the window closed before the event or after it? |
| **Miracles** | **the vindicatory corpus** | **is there a public sign a claimant can call in advance?** |

One question, five materials. And one failure mode, everywhere, which is the case that cannot lose.

That is the actual thesis of this vault and it had not been written down until now. The repository at `~/Myths` exists because of it. The site series *Something That Can Say No* (`~/quartz/content/`) says it about Girard alone. It is true of the whole thing.

## 3. The planks that were tested, and what the test cost

Full record in [[Myths — Forensic Record]]. The scoreboard there is now two rounds stale, because rounds 9 and 10 ran after it was compiled, and both were deletion tests. `king` and `kings` are 49.6% of the `marks` bucket and removing them moves nothing that matters. `death` is 61.9% of `crisis` and F1 fired on the measure rather than on the mechanism.

### 3.1 Girard, on the vocabulary

**What survives two nulls and a translation swap.** The Gospels put crowd-division language near violence language more than chance does. Phrases only, observed +0.9184, zero draws of two thousand beat it. GOSPEL over MYTH holds at p = 0.00064 when the whole thing is rerun on the KJV instead of the ASV, and the two translations sit 20.4% apart against a 25% bound.

**What died, and this is the correction the essays have not absorbed.** The `core` concepts are the ones that state Girard's claim. Innocent, guiltless, no fault, done nothing, unjust, false witness, protest, denounce, defend, plead. **They carry none of the effect, in either translation.** Restrict the lexicon to them and GOSPEL over MYTH collapses from p = 0.0003 to p = 0.254.

So the sentence to stop saying is *the Gospels are the only ancient text that says the victim was innocent*. That is the reading, and the instrument does not find it. The sentence that survived measurement is *the Gospels break the unanimity of the crowd, and myth does not*. Some said, others said, not all, spare him. Division, not exoneration.

That is a smaller claim and a better one, and it is closer to what [[Why The Crucifixion?]] is actually about. *Myth Versus the Cross* and *The Persecution Template* on the site both lean on the innocence line. Both need the amendment.

**The ceiling a pass does not lift.** Co-location of two vocabularies. Not that the dissenting words belong to a person separating from a crowd, not that the violence is collective, not that this is Girard's mechanism rather than passion narratives putting speech and killing in the same chapters for ordinary narrative reasons.

### 3.2 Interlock, on the texts

Canon fits 5, hard collides 1. Apocrypha fits 0, hard collides 3. Embellishment 0.22 per thousand words against 2.54, about eleven times. P1 to P4 hold. P5 fails, the canon ratio is 0.50 against a pre-declared 0.60.

**The half to trust and the half not to.** P2 and P3 are facts on the page. A schoolmaster dies in one infancy gospel and blushes in the other. A walking cross speaks in the Gospel of Peter and nowhere else. Nobody's charity is involved.

P1 and P5 are the untrustworthy half, and I produced them. The fits logged for the canon are the famous undesigned coincidences I already knew to look for. Bethsaida and Philip, Passover and green grass, John's "we". A blind coder without the apologetic literature logs fewer and the ratio falls. The numerator is five items and it is exactly where a knowing thumb rests.

### 3.3 The contradiction count

Instrument note: [[Counting the Contradictions]].

F4 fired at 67.6% against a ceiling of 40%. The dissolution was resting on my own sense-distinctions and the 33.3% admitted rate is not a finding.

What survives is directional, not charitable. An A2 error puts an item back as a contested clash at cost 1 or 2, and cannot manufacture a cost 3, because a cost 3 requires that no channel supplies a rescue and an A2 rejection is by construction an item where one does. Assume every A2 and A5 call was wrong and the hard-contradiction count is still 2 and the cost 4 count is still 0.

Report the fired falsifier at the top. Then show what survives and why structurally. Never assert survival.

Fifty-seven of 210 docket entries are restatements of an entry already on the list. The cock crow appears four times. Five entries misdescribe their own citations. Four put an interrogative on one side. That is counting, and anyone can redo it.

## 5. The door, and why it is forensic

The forensic posture is the rule and it holds here. Open with texts and patterns. Establish the template on ground the reader has no stake in. Drop one anomaly. Stop on a question.

This is not a marketing decision. It is the same discipline as the sample rule. An argument that begins at the conclusion has already tuned the sample, because the reader can see which texts were chosen and why.

*Anatomy of a Myth* on the site is the built example. Five case files, no argument for Christianity in any of them.

## 6. The joint the vault never built, part one

**The objection.** If God wants to be known and is able to make himself known, why does the result look exactly like people interpreting texts on their own? Sincere Christians praying and studying land in traditions that call each other heretical. Servetus burned over the Trinity. Anabaptists killed by both sides over infant baptism. Jesus himself makes the unity of his followers the evidence for his mission in John 17:21, and it is not there.

Full statement, the answers that fail, the two that hold, and the cost this vault has to keep carrying: [[The Communication Objection]].

**Why it belongs here and not in a folder of rebuttals.** Its third premise is my own condition. [[00 - Le Plan]] exists because I do not know whether I belong in Rome, Constantinople or Alexandria. That plan already contains the discipline for this. *Si tu te surprends à renégocier un critère, arrête-toi et écris pourquoi. Cette renégociation est la donnée.*

The objection is not answered by the vault. It is answered or not answered by Module 8, and Module 8 has not been run. Day to day that track is [[PARCOURS — où aller et où j'en suis]].

## 7. The joint the vault never built, part two

**The claim.** There is a public, checkable, in-advance signal, and it is attached to one address. Stigmata under medical examination across fifty years. Oil exuding from paper icons in front of an adversarial state investigation. A solar event predicted three months ahead to the day, hour and place, and reported from villages fifteen kilometres away by people facing the wrong direction. Consecrated hosts that come back from blinded laboratories as human myocardium, type AB, degraded, with the white cell influx of a heart in agony.

The corpus, the argumentative engine, the doctrines it claims to vindicate, and the control it is missing: [[Vindicatory Miracles — The Catholic Case and Its Missing Control]].

**Why it is not a detour.** Three connections, and the third is the one worth acting on.

1. **Its load-bearing premise is already in this vault.** The case runs on Earman, and [[Naturalism as a Presupposition — Not a Conclusion]] already holds that ground, with the boundary already drawn. Earman removes the in-principle blocker. He does not argue that anything happened, and citing him does not either.
2. **Its shape is the shape this vault exists to catch.** *Multiply the improbabilities* is a conjunction argument, and a conjunction argument cannot be lost by any single counterexample, because every counterexample is re-priced as one more coincidence the other side has to swallow. That is the accusation against Girard, word for word, in the site essay *The Accusation, and the Wager*. Same rule applies. Never tune the instrument, never tune the sample.
3. **The instrument for its best evidence already exists on this disk.** `interlock/build_run.py` emits witnesses as packets under opaque codes W1, W2, W3 in shuffled order, seals identities in `key.json`, and logs gaps per witness before the key is opened. The Fatima depositions and the Zeitoun testimonies are exactly that data type. Independent witnesses, incidental detail, and a fit-to-collision ratio that means something. Nobody has run it.

That is the next round, and it is buildable.

## 8. Where it can lose

Standing falsifiers, this document included.

- If the Girard essays keep using the innocence line after 3.1, the vault is tuning its own record.
- If the Fatima run happens and the fit ratio comes back at apocrypha level, the miracle plank drops out and section 7 gets rewritten to say so.
- If the Fatima run is designed and never coded, it joins the restraint control, and the pattern of pre-registering studies that are never run becomes the finding about me rather than about the material.
- If Module 8 lands only after a criterion is renegotiated, that renegotiation is evidence for [[The Communication Objection]] and it goes in this file under section 6.
- If any section here starts reading as a case rather than as an audit, it has drifted, and [[Failed tests — reading detectors]] is the record of what that drift costs.

The best available outcome is that the case is honestly built. Never that it happened.
