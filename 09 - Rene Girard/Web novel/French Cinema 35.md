**The 35 films — ruthlessly curated, no filler**

**Mimetic desire core — start here since it's your thing:**

1. La Règle du jeu — Renoir, Girard's favourite film, desire and imitation destroying an entire social class in one weekend
2. La Cérémonie — Chabrol, two women mirror each other's resentment until violence becomes inevitable
3. Le Boucher — Chabrol, a butcher and a schoolteacher, repressed violence and scapegoating
4. Les Diaboliques — two women plot against the same man, desire triangles everywhere
5. Lacombe Lucien — a boy joins the collaborators almost accidentally, pure mimetic drift
6. Funny Games — home invasion but really about the audience's complicity in violence, very Girardian
7. Teorema — Pasolini, a stranger enters a bourgeois family and each member mimetically desires him then collapses without him

**Philosophical slow burn — Monster and Lain energy in film:**

8. Ma Nuit chez Maud — Rohmer, one long conversation about Pascal, chance, and love in one night
9. Le Genou de Claire — Rohmer, a man becomes obsessed with touching a girl's knee, pure desire theory
10. L'Année dernière à Marienbad — dreamlike, memory and reality collapse, no clear plot
11. Hiroshima mon amour — trauma, memory, two people processing history through desire
12. La Maman et la putain — 3h40 of pure Parisian intellectual dialogue, the most French film ever made, save this for last

**Thriller and crime — accessible and gripping:**

13. Un prophète — best French film of the 2000s, prison, identity, power
14. Mesrine Part 1
15. Mesrine Part 2
16. 36 Quai des Orfèvres
17. Tell No One
18. Cache — Haneke, guilt and surveillance, slow and devastating

**Historical — formal French register:**

19. Ridicule — 18th century Versailles, wit as survival tool, most formally elegant French dialogue in cinema
20. Cyrano de Bergerac 1990 — Depardieu, the actual play filmed
21. La Reine Margot — brutal, political, gorgeous
22. Diplomatie — two men in one room negotiating whether to burn Paris, pure dialogue

**Contemporary:**

23. Portrait de la jeune fille en feu
24. Fatima — Algerian immigrant, you'll connect personally
25. Les Misérables 2019 — police drama Paris suburbs, completely different from La Haine register
26. Entre les murs — a teacher in a difficult Paris school, naturalistic dialogue
27. De rouille et d'os

**Dark and brutal — for when you're ready:**

28. Martyrs — disturbing but philosophically serious ending, very Girardian actually, scapegoat logic taken to its limit
29. Irréversible — Gaspar Noé, save for late in the year
30. Seul contre tous — pure rage monologue, uncomfortable but extraordinary French

**Comedy — colloquial register:**

31. OSS 117 Le Caire — deadpan spy comedy, very funny
32. OSS 117 Rio
33. Le Dîner de cons — one location, pure dialogue comedy

**Animation:**

34. Persepolis — Iranian girl, French narration, faith and identity, will hit you personally
35. Les Triplettes de Belleville — almost no dialogue but beautiful
