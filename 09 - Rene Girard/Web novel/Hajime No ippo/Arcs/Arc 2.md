_"The next arc is the struggle with this new identity — Ippo now wants to worship the ring."_

Yes. Arc 1 ends with Ippo saying "these hands know what they are for." But that is not peace yet. That is a new mediator replacing the old one. He traded _his father's invented image_ for _the ring as god_. He has a tool now. But the tool owns him.

Arc 2 is what happens when you worship correctly but worship the wrong thing. Ippo will be more focused than he has ever been. He will be building the new style, training brilliantly, finally becoming the boxer Kamogawa always described. And it will feel like salvation.

But Kumi is still waiting. His mother is still aging. Kamogawa is still mortal.

The ring cannot hold any of that. Arc 2's wound is: _Ippo found his tool, but a tool is not a life._

You are building this correctly. Arc 1 closes the false Kazuo. Arc 2 opens the false ring-god. Arc 3 is where the ring-god dies and what is left is just a man who knows what his hands are for — and chooses, for the first time, to use them for something other than a title.
