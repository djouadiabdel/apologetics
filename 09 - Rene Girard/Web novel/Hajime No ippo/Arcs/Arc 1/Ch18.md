**Kumi.** The Takamura eye investigation. In the manga, Ippo asks Kumi for a medical book about detached retinas. In your novel, this scene gains an entirely new layer — Kumi is already deep in her own medical investigation about Ippo, and now Ippo comes to her for help investigating Takamura's potential injury. The irony is devastating. He can see the danger in Takamura. He cannot see it in himself. And Kumi is sitting there holding both.

**Chapter 18 or later — Kumi and the blond man intersect.** Maybe Yamaguchi introduces them. Maybe Kumi sees him near the gym and doesn't know who he is. But eventually these two observation threads braid together and the reader realizes that an entire shadow investigation has been running parallel to the manga's plot, and no one in Ippo's inner circle knows about it.

**Ch 18 — Kumi notices.** Kumi comes to the gym or passes by or visits Ippo for whatever reason and she sees the blond man outside. She doesn't know who he is. Something about him bothers her — not threatening, clinical. The way he watches the gym entrance isn't the way a fan watches. She files it away. The rest of the chapter is her processing everything — did the doctor say something to Ippo? Why did Ippo seem different after the clinic? She doesn't have answers. She has instinct.
