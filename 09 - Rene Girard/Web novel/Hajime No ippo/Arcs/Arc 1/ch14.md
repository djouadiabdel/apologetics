**Kumi.** She visits Yamaguchi's clinic. Alone. With her books, her notes, her observations. This is the quiet chapter — two women in a room, one clinical, one scared, both precise. Yamaguchi looks at what Kumi has brought and begins to build the picture. This is not a diagnosis scene. This is a data-gathering scene. Yamaguchi asks questions. Kumi answers. The reader watches the case being built.
