**The gym.** Ippo returns to the Kamogawa gym. Kamogawa discusses plans. The challengers have withdrawn after Sawamura. Ippo is told to focus on recovery. Itagaki is there. The gym is alive. Write the gym at dawn. Write the sounds, the smell, the shadow on the wall. Let Itagaki watch Ippo from the middle distance — not saying anything, just watching. The grip check. The way Ippo moves. The small details that the reader is now trained to read as evidence.
