**Kumi.** The Takamura eye investigation. In the manga, Ippo asks Kumi for a medical book about detached retinas. In your novel, this scene gains an entirely new layer — Kumi is already deep in her own medical investigation about Ippo, and now Ippo comes to her for help investigating Takamura's potential injury. The irony is devastating. He can see the danger in Takamura. He cannot see it in himself. And Kumi is sitting there holding both.
