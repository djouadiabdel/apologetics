Crisis
Kumi shows him the doctor's report — his mother doesn't pressure him. She says "do as you please" — and means it — and that is worse than any pressure.
Because Ippo can push back against pressure. He cannot push back against someone who genuinely releases him and still looks tired. His mother's _permission_ is the crisis. She is aging. She is not asking him to stop. She is not asking him to stay. She is just there, doing the work, getting older, and meaning every word when she says she only wants him happy.

That is the moment the invented Kazuo narrative collapses. Because the real Kazuo didn't leave his family to chase one glorious thing. He stayed. He worked. He was _there_. And Ippo has been using the ring as permission to be somewhere else his entire adult life while his mother ages alone on a boat.

That single image — his mother saying "do as you please" and meaning it — is your crisis. Everything else can orbit around it, but that is the knife.
