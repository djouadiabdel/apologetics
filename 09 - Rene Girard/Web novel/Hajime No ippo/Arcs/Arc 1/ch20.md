**Ch 20 — Ippo's panic.** The Miyata thing spirals. Ippo wants the fight. The emotions flood him. But something physical happens during training — a hesitation, a twitch, something small. He ignores it. He calls Kumi or sees her and asks about the book. Why? Because somewhere in the back of his mind Yamaguchi's words are sitting. He doesn't connect them yet. Kumi hears him ask and her blood goes cold because she knows exactly why that question matters and he doesn't.
